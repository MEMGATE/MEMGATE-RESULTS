## What this means

**Anupalabdhi is the robust winner on real data.** Treating "not recorded" as an observed value gave:

- +0.34 pp in SR-3 and +0.45 pp here, significant both times;
- +3.4 pp on annealing, where most cells are unrecorded and *which* ones are unrecorded carries information.

It never cost more than −0.4 pp on any dataset.

**Kālacakra layers are a drift tool.** They give +1.33 pp on synthetic DRIFT and are neutral on shuffled real data. Real data in a fixed random order has no drift for them to exploit.

**The noise-tolerant śāstra variant** (MIX-WIN-SH) again trades losses on clean worlds for gains under noise. Its net effect is ≈ 0.

**Naive Bayes stays strongest** on these six datasets. That is a property of the data, mostly many weakly informative features, and it holds for every lesson learner here.
