# MX-5: the supervisor, every earned piece in one system watching a site through three sources

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-5 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

Each piece below won alone. MX-5 asks two things:
- Does the whole system beat the plain agreement network?
- Does each piece still earn its place inside the whole?

This is the mix the user asked for, in the form a working multi-sensor supervisor would use.

| Piece | Source | Won in | Alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Credence when three different sources agree and none is guessing; ask otherwise | Arthaśāstra 1.12; Gītā 13 (kṣetrajña); jijñāsā | AR-1 H4, JK-1, MX-2 | +16.61 pp |
| **DISMISS** the source that is the odd one in 20 of the last 30 splits | Arthaśāstra 1.12 | AR-1 | +13.41 |
| **AUDIT**: ask about 5% of the cases it would answer alone | Yoginīhṛdaya III.3 (daily observance by the knower) | MX-3, MX-4 | +9.58, +13.13 |
| **RESET**: prakṛti–vikṛti health check on its own errors, then rebuild from the latest 30 observed cases | Caraka Sūtrasthāna 9.4 | PK-2, MX-3 | +5.59, +4.74 |
| **SAMSKARA**: each spy keeps refuted lessons latent; they return after 3 confirmations | Yoga Sūtra 4.8–4.9 | SK-1 | +2.9 (R = 3) |

## 2. Arms

| Arm | Content |
| --- | --- |
| SPIES | AR-1's network without dismissal: three R-ORD spies, credence on agreement without guessing |
| **SUPERVISOR** | SPIES with DISMISS, AUDIT, RESET and SAMSKARA |
| SUPERVISOR−DISMISS, −AUDIT, −RESET, −SAMSKARA | the supervisor without one piece |

**The pieces inside the supervisor.**
- **Audits.** An audited case counts as asked, as in MX-3. The draws come from `random.Random(seed + 4242)`.
- **Reset.**
  - The health check is PK-1's `unwell`, applied to the network's answer errors on every observed case since the last reset. This is MX-3's rule.
  - On a reset, every trusted spy is rebuilt from the latest 30 observed cases.
  - A dismissed spy stays dismissed.
- **Saṃskāra.** The spies use SK-1's policy R-ORD+SAMSKARA-R3.

## 3. Data

### The synthetic VIEWS-RECUR family

- A hidden case is seen through three noisy sources, as in AR-1's VIEWS (each value kept with probability 0.9).
- The label follows two EXC rule sets that alternate A, B, A, B over four quarters, as in SK-1's RECUR.
- There are 800 cases (quarters of 200).
- One source, drawn as in AR-1, fails from case 480, inside the return of A.
- Every world therefore has a switch, a return, and a failure.

### Hydraulic test rig (main; new to the lab)

UCI 447, "Condition monitoring of hydraulic systems": 2,205 load cycles of 60 s and 17 sensors.

**Features.** A case is one cycle. Its features are the mean and the standard deviation of each sensor over the cycle, giving 34 features.

**The three sources, by physical kind:**
- pressure and motor power: PS1–PS6 and EPS1, giving 14 features;
- flow and vibration: FS1, FS2 and VS1, giving 6;
- temperature and efficiency: TS1–TS4, CE, CP and SE, giving 14.

**The four documented targets are four groups:**
- cooler condition (3 grades);
- valve condition (4);
- internal pump leakage (3);
- accumulator pressure (4).

The stable flag is not a target.

**Orders.** Each group runs in 10 shuffled orders, cut to 1,000 cases. One source, drawn as in AR-1, fails from case 600. Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule.

The 76.6 MB archive is not stored. A derived table of per-cycle features is stored with its builder, as for MHEALTH.

## 4. Measures

- **Net credence**, as in AR-1: right minus wrong answers given alone, per 100 scored cases.
- **Wrong answers given alone** per 100 cases: the dangerous errors.
- Coverage, accuracy alone, labels asked, resets and dismissals.

## 5. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before. The CI is stratified by group: VIEWS-RECUR and the four hydraulic targets.

| Hypothesis | Comparison (net credence) |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | SUPERVISOR − SPIES |
| H2 | SUPERVISOR − SUPERVISOR−DISMISS |
| H3 | SUPERVISOR − SUPERVISOR−AUDIT |
| H4 | SUPERVISOR − SUPERVISOR−RESET |
| H5 | SUPERVISOR − SUPERVISOR−SAMSKARA |
| Descriptive | Wrong answers given alone, per arm and group. The pieces' counters. |

**INVALID if:**
- any run is non-deterministic or crashes;
- the supervisor never dismisses in any run;
- the supervisor never resets in any run.
