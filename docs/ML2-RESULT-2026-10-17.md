## What this means

1. **The ML-1 result replicates.** On new worlds, with a drift family that ML-1 did not have, model-written lessons beat retrieved experience by +2.58 pp. Lessons are now a WIN in two preregistered real-model runs: +3.84 pp in ML-1 and +2.58 pp in ML-2.
2. **Kālacakra layers do not transfer to model prompting.** Offline, the layered recency fallback won under drift (T-1, +1.04 pp overall, +3.7 on DRIFT-T). Here, re-ordering which 8 neighbours the model sees changed accuracy by less than 0.1 pp with lessons, and by +0.25 pp without them.

   The likely reason is that the model already receives each neighbour's case number. It can weigh recency itself, so a recency-first selection adds little. The 100-case streams also give each layer few cases.
3. **Lesson management events.**

   When lessons were shown, the prediction was right 75.1% of the time with plain retrieval and 74.9% with kala retrieval.
