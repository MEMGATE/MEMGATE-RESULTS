**Caveats on H2:**

- The gain comes from car (+7.8) and voting (+2.9). On mushroom (−0.5) and zoo (−1.9), bare 1-NN retrieval is better.

So that benefit belongs to lesson learning in general, not to the śāstra policies.

**Plain reading.** The śāstra mechanisms ran thousands of times on real data. They changed how cases were decided, but not how many were decided correctly, compared with ordinary rule-learning policies. This is a valid negative result for SR-1's formulation.

### How decisions are made differently

On car, R-SHASTRA keeps general lessons and carves exceptions out of them.

R-ORD specialises its lessons and hands more cases to the 1-NN fallback, which is about 50% right on car. The two routes end at the same overall accuracy.

All four predictions made before any code ran were wrong or unconfirmed.

The pattern matches the dev diagnostics:

- **Noise:** keeping a general lesson and carving out exceptions resists label noise.
- **Too broad or outdated:** the same habit hurts when the general lesson is too broad (DNF) or out of date (DRIFT).
- **EXC:** even in the family built around exceptions, R-SHASTRA loses. The dev diagnostics point to two likely causes, which are hypotheses rather than tested findings:
  - A single upādhi accepted on only three to five supports.
  - Exceptions that apply after one support.

## Limits

- **Data:** four public categorical datasets, streams of at most 1,000 cases, plus synthetic families that I designed.
- **Formulations:** one formulation of each mechanism. Other formulations of the same śāstra ideas could behave differently.
- **Learners:** the rule learner and the baselines are simple. Strong modern learners are not included.
- **Out of scope:** model-written lessons, Pramana's M-CORE, the Sanatana graph line and every historical result.

## Next: SR-2 on new held-out data

These ideas came from the SR-1 pilot and this result. They must be tested on datasets that SR-1 never used, chosen and frozen before any run.

- **Balīyastva guard:** retire a general lesson (utsarga) once its exceptions outnumber its confirmations.
- **Niścita upādhi:** accept a single upādhi only with more support; otherwise keep the doubt open between narrowing and carving out.
- **Barring without deciding:** let unvalidated exceptions bar the general lesson without deciding the case themselves.

After that, the larger step is to carry the best policy into model-written lessons inside Pramana's learning cycle.
