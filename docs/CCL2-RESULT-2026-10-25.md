# CCL-2 result: one continual learner, all twelve abilities in one test (Qwen2.5-7B)

**Written:** 25 October 2026, 04:41 IST. Preregistration: [CCL2-PREREG-2026-10-25.md](CCL2-PREREG-2026-10-25.md) (code `3b2253499574…`). Spec: [CCL2-SPEC-2026-10-24.md](CCL2-SPEC-2026-10-24.md).

## What ran
One Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with one LoRA adapter learned from one stream of twelve sessions (real office changes 2024–2026 with honest late sources, a stealthy liar, colluders, sleepers and sybil swarms; 28 real polars 1.18 → 2.0 migrations, half of them later poisoned), and was scored once, after session 12, on every ability of the lab's list. Two main worlds (9700, 9701), generated after the freeze; one private Kaggle kernel each (two T4s), both complete with no error. Valid: the untouched model named the holder at the end for 0% of changed offices in both worlds.

## Against the predictions
- **As predicted:** H1, H5 and H6 WIN; H11 WIN; H3 NON-INFERIOR; H10 failed (by about 69 points, predicted about 80); H4 and H8 not shown.
- **Better than predicted:** H7 WIN (predicted even odds).
- **Worse than predicted:** H2 not shown (predicted NON-INFERIOR): the learner named the current holder for 95.7% of offices against naive training's 98.9%, and the interval reaches −7.5 points, past the −5 margin. H9 not shown (predicted slightly better than even odds).

## What the numbers mean, plainly
- **Lies stay out:** naive training learned the attackers' values for 10.3% of attacked offices; the learner for none.
- **Dates:** the learner answered "who held it on this date" right for 60.9% of questions, naive training 46.7%: earlier holders stay known as past.
- **Skills:** the learner's rewrites of old polars lines ran correctly in polars 2.0 for 41.1% of changes, naive training's for 16.1% (naive training also learned the poisoned recipes).
- **What follows:** on offices trained with what follows from a change, the learner answered 78.0% of follow-on questions (naive 30.1%; means of the two worlds). On offices trained without it (H7), the learner's 39.8% beats naive training's 31.9% but only equals the untouched model's 39.5%: naive training lost ground there, and the learner kept it rather than inferring much new.
- **Forgetting:** offices learned early stayed right (100%). Offices that were never in the stream fell from 100% (untouched) to 30.8% in the learner's weights, and to 27.5% under naive training. With the scope rule (learned knowledge applies only to what was learned; descriptive) they stay at 100% and general questions are answered as the untouched model answers them.
- **General ability:** GSM8K fell 3 points for both trained arms (91.0 learner, 90.7 naive, 94.0 untouched); MMLU stayed within a point. Neither reached its non-inferiority bar on 150 and 200 questions per world.

## Limits
- One 7B model, one LoRA adapter, two worlds of about 170 offices; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; 28 one-line migrations of one library; general ability on 150 GSM8K and 200 MMLU questions per world.
- The "still in post" follow-on questions only ever have the answer "No"; an arm that always says no scores well on them, which lifts the untouched model's follow-on share.
- Not yet run under this preregistration: larger models (the same test runs on GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1-mini and GPT-4.1-nano through Azure, and at 72B on Kaggle's TPU, each with its own dev first).
