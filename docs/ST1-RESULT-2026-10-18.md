## What this means

**Saṃskāra's dev promise on real streams did not hold.**
- On the exposed streams, SK-1's latent impressions gained +0.81. On five fresh streams they lost on every one, by −1.39 on average.
- **Why.** These streams are ruled by the calendar and the weather. A lesson such as "at 8 in the morning demand is high" is broken by holidays, weekends and storms, and it was broken for a reason. Bringing it back after three confirmations repeats the error the next time the exception occurs.
- **Where saṃskāra does help.**
  - Worlds that return to an earlier state (SK-1 RECUR, +2.22).
  - Label noise (SK-1 NOISE, +2.22).
  - Inside the supervisor on shuffled sensor data (MX-5, +2.56). There a lesson is broken by one noisy reading.
- **The rule that follows.** Keep refuted lessons latent only where refutations are mostly noise, not where they are true exceptions.

**Pañcadhā maitrī helps a little on fresh streams.**
