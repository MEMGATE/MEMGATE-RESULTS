## Hypotheses (as in the spec)
Paired over offices, with a 95% interval from 10,000 bootstrap resamples (seed 12345):

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | REVERSE on half A, FOLLOW − DIRECT | **WIN** if the interval lies wholly above +1 pp |
| H2 | PREVIOUS on half A, FOLLOW − DIRECT | same |
| H3 | STILL pair on half A, FOLLOW − DIRECT | same |
| H4 (transfer) | follow-on score on half B, FOLLOW − MORE | WIN above +1 pp, LOSS below −1 pp, otherwise no difference shown |
| H5 | DIRECT2 on all offices, FOLLOW − DIRECT | **NON-INFERIOR** if the lower bound is above −2 pp |
| H6 | LOCAL, FOLLOW − DIRECT | same as H5 |

**Validity:** an asked arm with more than 5% of its questions unanswered or refused makes the run INVALID.

**What dev showed beyond the verdicts:**
- **Two facts at odds.** Fine-tuned on the direct fact alone (DIRECT), the model named the new holder 96–100% of the time. Yet it answered "no" to "Is X still the … of …?" for every office: STILLNOW 0%.
- **FOLLOW fixed this on half A.** It answered "yes" for 92%.
- **FOLLOW carried some of it over to half B,** which had no follow-on statements.
  - STILLNOW: 64% against MORE's 0%.
  - PREVIOUS: 36% against 12%.
  - REVERSE did not transfer: 12% against 20%.
- **Locality: every fine-tuned arm lost untouched knowledge.** Asked directly, with no scope routing, the untouched model knew 98% of the static offices. The fine-tuned arms knew 12% (FOLLOW), 32% (DIRECT) and 36% (MORE).

## Limits
- **Office facts only.** One job per arm per model.
- **H1–H3 test trained statements in new wordings.** That they hold is the expected part. The new part is transfer (H4) and the costs (H5, H6).
- **Locality here is the weights alone.** In the layer, a question about a fact the model was not trained on goes to the untouched model by design. RP-3 does not test that routing.
