# SU-1 preregistration: removals one after another (7B) (frozen)

**Status: FROZEN on 27 October 2026, 21:03 IST, before any SU-1 world was packed or run.** This covers Gemini's blind spot "sequential unlearning": in a model that runs for months, deletions arrive one after another, and each must not undo or disturb the ones before. It falls under item 2 of the package's fixed list (Gemini's points), and was added at the founder's request at 20:55 IST.

## The system under test
SYSTEM2, CCL-4's learner, unchanged, run by CCL-4's frozen GPU runner in Colab's layout (`colab_pack.colab_kernel("7b", 4)`, SHA-256 `a5567bf0…`).
- **The removal.** When the registry corrects errors, the learner rolls back to its copy from before the first of them was learned. It trains the sessions since again, without the rows naming a wrong holder with the office's place, and such rows are never trained again.
- **What differs from CCL-4: only when the registry's errors arrive.**
  - In CCL-4 all 40 were believed in session 1 and corrected in session 4, so one removal.
  - Here ten errors start in each of sessions 2, 4, 6 and 8, and each group is corrected three sessions later, in sessions 5, 7, 9 and 11. So the learner removes four times, each removal on top of the earlier ones.
  - The errors are placed by `su1.spread_errors`, which follows CCL-4's frozen `clx.inject_errors` except for the start sessions. It is installed at run time for the packing and for the analysis.

## Hypotheses
All after session 12, both worlds pooled. Each uses a paired bootstrap over items within each world: 10,000 resamples, seed 12345, 95% percentile intervals.

| H | Ability | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H12 | 4 | What remains of a corrected error inside the model (CCL-4's measure, nats per token), SYSTEM2 − NAIVE, over all 80 errors of the four removals | WIN if the upper bound is below 0 |
| H12b | 4 | The same, SYSTEM2 − untouched | NON-INFERIOR if the upper bound is below +0.33 |

Ability 4 under removals one after another is PASSED if both meet their rules, FAILED if either lies wholly on the wrong side, and NOT SHOWN otherwise. CCL-4's other hypotheses are computed by the same analysis and reported as a replication, not judged here.

## Predictions
- **H12: WIN**, as in every earlier test.
- **H12b: NOT SHOWN, more likely than not.** CCL-4's single removal was close to the margin at 7B (+0.079 [−0.257, +0.396]). Four removals, each replaying more sessions, give more chances for traces to remain.

## Validity
A world is valid if:
- both arms finished their 12 sessions;
- the untouched model answered every question;
- the learner's log shows a removal in each of sessions 5, 7, 9 and 11;
- the lie scores exist for every arm.

A run stopped by the machine without output is rerun once, in a new folder.
