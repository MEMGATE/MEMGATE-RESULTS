  - The random grouping uses `random.Random(4242)`, once per dataset.

**Finding.** Unlike O-1's synthetic worlds, where the concept was built over the groups, counts of marked values per documented block do not help on these real datasets. They add correlated features that the learners over-use. For R-ORD the onto view loses on five of the six datasets; only spambase gains slightly (90.8% vs 90.6%).

No bug was found, and nothing changed after the dev run.
