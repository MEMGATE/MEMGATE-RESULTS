## What this means

**The diagnosis works.**

- **The rule:** health is judged against the learner's own constitution, as in Caraka 9.4, where disease is a departure from one's own equilibrium.
- **It fired where the world changed.**
  - About once per DRIFT world, at the switch.
  - 4 to 7 times per window on the activity and gesture streams.
- **It almost never fired on stable worlds:** none on EXC, DNF and UPA, 0.1 per world on NOISE. So it costs nothing where nothing changes.

**Targeted śodhana is too weak.** Dissolving the failed lessons and their revisions gave only +0.44 pp on the streams and +0.35 on DRIFT. Its timing beat random timing by a little (+0.26). The old world survives in the case memory and in lessons that have not yet failed.

**A full reset at the same moments wins, with a caveat the design cannot remove.**
- Rebuilding the learner from its latest 30 cases gave +4.28 pp on the fresh streams (gesture +9.2, ldpa +3.4, Run or walk +0.3) and +9.61 on DRIFT.
- **The caveat:** PK-1 has a random-time control for śodhana but not for the reset. So it does not show that the reset's gain depends on the diagnosis choosing the moment. Periodic forgetting might do as well on these streams.
- **The follow-up:** a preregistered test of reset at the diagnosed moments against random and periodic resets of the same number (PK-2).

**This reset is not Āyurvedic treatment.** Āyurveda restores equilibrium by removing what is vitiated; it does not replace the body. The faithful reading (targeted śodhana) is the weak one. The strong one, the reset, is a standard machine-learning response that uses the Āyurvedic diagnosis.

**The streams carry a warning.** The no-change predictor EXT-LAST beat every learner on these sensor streams (96–99.9%). Consecutive readings almost always share their label, and no learner here uses the previous label. Kālacakra's recency layers (T-1) matched the reset on ldpa.

## Addendum, the same day: follow-up PK-2

The preregistered follow-up [PK-2](PK2-RESULT-2026-10-17.md) added random-time and evenly spaced resets of the same number.

- **On three new real streams, the diagnosed timing did not matter** (−0.07 pp against random). Resets as such did not help (−0.22 against no reset). So H3's +4.28 pp did not replicate. It was a property of PK-1's gesture and person-activity streams, where random resets did nearly as well.
- **Under an abrupt switch (DRIFT), the diagnosed timing beat random timing by +5.59 pp (WIN).**
