## Rules fixed in the code

**Members.** PARISHAD-3*: R-ORD+ANU+AKH, naive Bayes and the decision tree. All three see every label.

**Natural relation.** At each case, a member's accuracy on all earlier cases is ranked among the three: best friend, middle neutral, worst enemy. Ties take the better rank.

**Temporary relation.** A member is a friend if its accuracy over the last 30 cases is at least the members' mean there; otherwise it is an enemy.

**Grades and weights.**

| Natural + temporary | Grade | Weight |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| F + F | great friend | 4 |
| N + F | friend | 3 |
| F + E, and E + F (my completion) | neutral | 2 |
| N + E | enemy | 1 |
| E + E | great enemy | 0 |

**The vote.**
- The answer is the label with the largest total weight. Ties go to the earliest member voting a tied label.
- If all weights are 0, the answer is plurality.
- NATURAL-ONLY weights friend 2, neutral 1, enemy 0. TEMPORARY-ONLY weights friend 1, enemy 0.

**INVALID conditions:**
- non-determinism;
- a crashed run;
- MAITRI never differing from PLURALITY.

**A correction to the spec.** The spec says twelve exposed streams; there are eleven: elec2, airlines, gasdrift, the PK-1 and PK-2 streams, and MX-4's two.
