## Rules fixed in the code

**Doubt.** Doubt is PV-1's rule: the members disagree, or the lesson member doubts (JK-1's rule).

**Ekamata (one mind).** All three members give the same, non-empty answer.

**Matched controls.**
- SABHA-RANDOM-ASK observes as many labels as SABHA-JIJNASA, at cases drawn by `random.Random(seed + 777)`.
- Each random abstention control answers as many scored cases as its arm did, drawn by `random.Random(seed + 999)`.

**Scoring.** A run with no unanimous case counts 0 for H2 or H3.

**INVALID conditions:**
- any non-determinism in the repeats;
- a failed run;
- SABHA-JIJNASA asking for every label in every run;
- the assembly never being of one mind.

## Dev run (exposed data)

The output is in [`runs/mx2-dev-20261018-a`](../runs/mx2-dev-20261018-a/SUMMARY.md). It covers the 26 held-out datasets of MX-1, JK-1 and PV-1, in orders 2000–2009. There were 260 runs, all 26 repeats were identical, and nothing failed.

| Comparison | pp, 95% CI | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1: SABHA-JIJNASA − SABHA-RANDOM-ASK | +3.99 [+3.57, +4.45] | WIN |
| H2: FULL-EKAMATA − its random abstention | +8.23 [+7.99, +8.46] | WIN |
| H3: GRAND − its random abstention | +6.83 [+6.59, +7.06] | WIN |
| SABHA-JIJNASA − FULL | +0.27 [+0.14, +0.40] | descriptive |
| GRAND − FULL-EKAMATA (selective) | −1.17 [−1.36, −0.99] | descriptive |
| FULL-EKAMATA − the lesson member's own kṣetrajña (selective) | +1.47 [+1.22, +1.72] | descriptive |

**Findings.**
- **The assembly asking only under doubt asked for 41% of the labels on average.** It was slightly more accurate than the assembly that saw every label (+0.27 pp).
- **The assembly is of one mind on 65% of cases.** There its answers are 86.0% right, against 77.7% for as many random cases.
- **Unanimity is a better sign of knowing than the lesson member's own.** At about the same coverage (65% against 66%), it was +1.47 pp more accurate.
- **The grand system costs a little reliability.** It asks for 41% of the labels and answers only when unanimous. Its members agree on more cases (71%), and its answers there are 1.17 pp less accurate than the full assembly's.

No bug was found, and nothing changed after the dev run.
