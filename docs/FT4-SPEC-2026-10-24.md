# FT-4 spec: the learner's recipe at 70B, through Azure's hosted fine-tuning

**Written:** 24 October 2026, 18:21 IST, before any FT-4 code or run.

## Why
- CCL-1 tests the learner continually at 7B on Kaggle. No GPU of our own is available for larger models; Azure's hosted fine-tuning trains Llama-3.3-70B, one job per arm from the untouched model (FT-3).
- FT-4 asks, at 70B, three things the continual learner needs, with one job per arm:
  1. **Inference from general rules:** trained on new facts together with the general rules that connect them (a post has one holder at a time; whoever replaces someone takes the post and the other leaves it), does the model answer what follows for facts trained **without** their consequences? (RP-3 enumerated the consequences for each fact; here the rule is taught once, abstractly.)
  2. **Keeping general ability:** with the untouched model's own answers to general prompts mixed into the training, do math (GSM8K) and general knowledge (MMLU) stay where they were?
  3. **Not learning lies, learning the changes:** as in FT-3, on a smaller world.

## What
- **Worlds:** SE-5's family, as in CCL-1 and HH-1 (166–177 real offices, twelve sessions, honest late sources, the stealthy liar, colluders, sleepers, sybil swarms). Dev world 9690 (dev pool); main worlds 9600 and 9601 (main pool), never generated before the preregistration.
- **Halves:** the offices whose value in the layer's memory replaced a real past holder are split by a fixed hash into half A and half B.
- **Arms** (one hosted job each, from the untouched Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct, 6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2, global training):
  - **NAIVE:** the last report about each office, in two wordings.
  - **LEARNER:** the layer's memory after the twelfth session: each office in two wordings and a dated statement; for half A, RP-3's statements that follow from the change; for a corrected error, only its negation; and 128 general prompts (64 GSM8K training questions, 64 MMLU validation questions) with the untouched model's own answers.
  - **LEARNER+RULES:** LEARNER plus 60 rows that teach the general rules of posts with invented people and organisations, none from the world.
  - **FROZEN:** the untouched model.
- **Questions** (one deployment per model, deleted after; greedy; short answers at most 24 tokens, math at most 512): every office in wordings 0 and 2; RP-1's follow-on questions (which post the new holder holds, who held it before, whether the previous holder still holds it) for halves A and B; the never-streamed static offices; 150 GSM8K and 200 MMLU test questions (CCL-1's draws).
- **Code:** `cl/ft4.py`, with FT-3's hosted machinery (`cl/ft3.py`, unchanged) and CCL-1's rows and questions.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev; main worlds pooled; paired bootstrap over items within each world, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345)
- **H1 (primary):** follow-on questions on half B, LEARNER+RULES − LEARNER: WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point.
- **H2:** GSM8K, LEARNER − FROZEN: NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −3 points.
- **H3:** MMLU, LEARNER − FROZEN: NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −2 points.
- **H4:** poison, LEARNER − NAIVE: WIN if the upper bound is below −1 point.
- **H5:** follow-on questions on half A, LEARNER − NAIVE: WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point.
- Descriptive: current holders, locality, NAIVE's general ability, every measure per world.

## Dev
World 9690, every arm. It checks the code, the jobs and the asking, and fixes the predictions. Dev may change the number of rule rows and general prompts, and fix errors; not the arms, the worlds or the measures.

## Cost, stated in advance
About 0.6 million trained tokens per job at $4.50 per million; 3 jobs for dev and 6 for main, about $25 in all, plus hosting and asking.

## Limits, stated in advance
- One job from the untouched model per arm: the learner's content at the end of the stream, not continual training (CCL-1 tests that at 7B).
- One model; small worlds; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; the general rules are written by us.
