# P-2: the winning pariṣad, with the śāstra mechanisms that earned a place inside its lesson member

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any P-2 code, after the P-1 result and before the O-2 main result was read. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

P-1 found that a three-member assembly of *different* learners won against the lesson learner: R-ORD, naive Bayes and ID3, deciding by plurality, gained +1.44 pp. Separately, two śāstra mechanisms gave small but repeatable gains inside the lesson learner:

- anupalabdhi (SR-3, H-1);
- Prābhākara akhyāti (SR-6).

**Question.** Do those mechanisms still add something once the lesson learner sits inside a winning assembly? And does a fifth and different competence, the Nyāya–Mīmāṃsā rule policy, add more?

## 2. Arms

**New single learner: R-ORD+ANU+AKH.** This is R-ORD with anupalabdhi (a missing value is observed absence) and akhyāti (an indiscernible counterexample does not revise). It has no other change.
