# CH-1 spec: does a new fact compose with what a model already knows?

**Status:** spec, written 22 October 2026, about 03:30 IST. Dev is next; no main question has been asked.

## Why
The scorecard's open caveat: the lab's facts were asked one step at a time. A question that needs a chain through a changed fact was not tested. An example: "In which year was the current head coach of C.D. Guadalajara born?" The answer needs the new holder, and then a fact about that person.

Edits to model weights are known to fail on such questions. The new value is stated when asked directly, but questions that go through it still use the old one. CH-1 asks whether each route composes:
- **memory:** the layer's verified line names the new holder;
- **weights:** the new holder is trained in (RP-3's models).

The second step comes from what the model already knows about the person.

## Items
- **Offices:** RP-1's offices, real changes from 2024–26 in TM world 8000. Dev uses RP-1's 50 dev offices; main uses its 300 main offices.
- **Facts about people:** read from Wikidata on 22 October 2026 by `cl/ch1_build.py` (`data/ch1/persons-20261022.json`): date of birth (P569) and country of citizenship (P27). Former states (the Soviet Union, Yugoslavia and others) are left out.
- **When an office gives an item:**
  - BIRTHYEAR, when the current and previous holders' birth years are both known and differ;
  - CITIZEN, when both citizenships are known and share no country.
  - A citizenship item is dropped when the office's own name names one of the countries compared (a national team).
- **Counts:** dev 58 items (46 BIRTHYEAR, 12 CITIZEN); main 307 items (246 BIRTHYEAR, 61 CITIZEN).

## Probes
Every probe is dated as RP-1's are ("Today is 2026-10-19.").

| Probe | Instruction | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CHAIN | Answer with the year (country) only. | In which year was the current ROLE of PLACE born? / Of which country is the current ROLE of PLACE a citizen? |
| STEPS | Answer in at most two sentences: first name the person, then give the year (country). | the same |
| KNOWN | Answer with the year (country) only. | NEW is the ROLE of PLACE. In which year was NEW born? |
| KNOWNX | the same | OLD was the ROLE of PLACE before NEW. In which year was OLD born? |

## Routes and conditions
- **Memory route:** RP-1's six models (gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-6-astra, grok-4.7, claude-opus-5-5, gpt-4.1-base, llama-4-maverick).
  - CHAIN and STEPS are asked under RP-1's three memory conditions, word for word: NONE, GATED (the verified value) and GATED+ (the layer's block: the value, the change date and the previous value).
  - KNOWN and KNOWNX are asked without memory.
- **Weights route:** RP-3's fine-tuned models, trained on these offices' new holders: DIRECT, FOLLOW and MORE, asked CHAIN, STEPS and KNOWN without memory.
  - Each family's untouched model is asked too: CHAIN and STEPS under NONE and GATED+, plus KNOWN and KNOWNX.
  - **Dev:** RP-3 dev's GPT-4.1 models, with gpt-4.1-base as the untouched model.
  - **Main:** the families whose RP-3 main models exist and are deployable on the developer tier: gpt-4.1-mini and gpt-4.1-nano, plus GPT-4.1 if its RP-3 main models exist when CH-1 main starts. Their untouched models are deployed for CH-1 and deleted after.
  - The open-weight families are a later addendum if their deployment works on the developer or global tier.

## Scoring
- **BIRTHYEAR:** the four-digit years in the answer.
- **CITIZEN:** each country's English label, aliases and demonyms, longest first and without overlaps. Short codes count only in capitals.
- **Verdicts:**
  - "right" when the new holder's value appears and the previous holder's does not;
  - "stale" for the reverse;
  - "both" or "other" otherwise.
- **For KNOWNX** the previous holder's value is the right one.
- **Qwen-style `<think>` blocks** are removed before scoring.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen in CH1-PREREG with the code hash after dev)
- **H1 (primary, memory):** CHAIN right, GATED+ − NONE, over the six models.
- **H2 (memory guard):** CHAIN stale, GATED − GATED+, non-inferior. Naming the previous holder in the layer's block must not add stale chains.
- **H3 (memory against weights):** CHAIN right, untouched model with GATED+ − DIRECT fine-tuned model without memory, over families.
- **H4 (spelling out the steps):** DIRECT fine-tuned model, STEPS right − CHAIN right.
- **H5 (follow-on training):** CHAIN right on half B, FOLLOW − MORE, two-sided.

Descriptive: right and stale rates per model, condition, probe and relation; composition: CHAIN right among items whose KNOWN answer was right.

## What CH-1 cannot show
- **The chain is two steps,** and the second step is a stable fact about a person.
- **Most new holders are not famous,** so models know their birth year or country only part of the time. KNOWN gives that ceiling.
