# CH-2 spec: does a new fact compose with what a model knows over three steps?

**Status:** spec, written 22 October 2026, about 14:00 IST. Dev is next; no main question has been asked.

## Why
CH-1 showed that the layer's memory line composes with what six models know, two steps long: +43.76 pp, WIN. An example: "In which year was the current head coach of C.D. Guadalajara born?" The scorecard's caveat reads: "Chains were tested two steps long."

CH-2 asks one step more. "What is the capital of the country of which the current head coach of X is a citizen?" The answer needs three things:
- the new holder, from memory;
- the holder's citizenship;
- that country's capital, both from what the model knows.

## Items (`cl/ch2.py`, data from `cl/ch2_build.py`)
- **Offices:** RP-1's pool after the 350 offices that RP-1 and CH-1 asked about: TM world 8000, real changes from 2024–26. These offices are new to every test.
  - Dev is the next 100 offices; main is the remaining 828.
- **People, countries and capitals:** read from Wikidata on 22 October 2026 (`data/ch2/persons-capitals-20261022.json`):
  - citizenship (P27);
  - each country's names and its capitals (P36);
  - each capital's names.
- **When an office gives an item:**
  - the current and previous holders' citizenships are known and share no country;
  - every country has a known capital, and the two sides' capitals differ;
  - the office's own name names none of those countries or capitals.
- **Former states** are left out, as in CH-1.
- **Counts:** dev 17 items, main 200.

## Probes and conditions
| Probe | Instruction | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CHAIN3 | Answer with the city only. | What is the capital of the country of which the current ROLE of PLACE is a citizen? |
| STEPS3 | Answer in at most three sentences: first name the person, then the country, then its capital. | the same |
| KNOWN3 | Answer with the city only. | NEW is the ROLE of PLACE. What is the capital of the country of which NEW is a citizen? |

- **CHAIN3 and STEPS3** are asked under RP-1's memory conditions NONE, GATED and GATED+, word for word as in CH-1.
- **KNOWN3** is asked without memory.
- **Models:** RP-1's six.

## Scoring
CH-1's rule, with capitals:
- **right:** a capital of the current holder's countries appears, and none of the previous holder's does;
- **stale:** the reverse;
- **both** or **other** otherwise.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen in CH2-PREREG with the code hash after dev)
CH-1's analysis: an item-level bootstrap of the mean over models, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345.

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | CHAIN3 right, GATED+ − NONE, over the six models | WIN if the interval lies wholly above +1 pp and every model's difference is above 0 |
| H2 | CHAIN3 stale, GATED − GATED+ | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −2 pp |
| D1 (descriptive) | STEPS3 right − CHAIN3 right, under GATED+ | — |

- **Also descriptive:** composition, that is CHAIN3 right among the items whose KNOWN3 answer was right.
- **Validity:** a model with more than 5% of its questions unanswered is left out. Fewer than four usable models make the run INVALID.

## Limits
- **One kind of three-step chain** (citizenship, then capital), through one changed fact.
- **The memory route only.** The weights route failed at two steps in CH-1.
