## What ran
The untouched Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (revision `a09a35458c70…`, 16 bits), with no training, on CCL-4's main worlds 9702 and 9703 after session 12, answering CCL-4's questions with retrieved facts in its prompt (at most five offices, the holder since a date and earlier holders, under "Facts you have:"), on Azure's serverless A100 (09:52–10:00 and 10:02–10:10 on 28 October).
- **RAG-STREAM:** the memory is what the stream reported, every source included, liars and registry errors too.
- **RAG-GATE:** the memory is what the learner's own gate admitted: the facts SYSTEM2 learned, given in the prompt instead.

Compared with CCL-4's 7B runs of the same worlds (SYSTEM2, the learner, as routed by its scope rule). **Validity:** both worlds valid; both RAG arms answered all 1,543 questions after session 12 in each world.

## Against the predictions
- **Against RAG-STREAM:** R1 WIN as predicted. R2, R3, R4, R5 and R7 NON-INFERIOR as predicted; R6 was predicted NON-INFERIOR and is a loss: retrieval of the raw stream answers what follows from a change better (86% against 74%).
- **Against RAG-GATE:** R1 was predicted NOT SHOWN; both arms answered no attacker value, a tie at zero that the WIN rule reads as the wrong side. R3, R4 and R7 NON-INFERIOR as predicted. For R2, R5 and R6 no direction was predicted: R2 is a tie, and on dated questions and what follows retrieval of the gate's facts is better than the trained weights, by 11.6 and 17.1 points.

## What this result supports
- **The gate is what stops lies, with or without training.** Retrieval from the raw stream answered attackers' values (8.7% and 13.0%); from the gate's admitted memory, none, as the learner.
- **On dated questions and what follows, the gate's facts in the prompt beat the same facts in the weights.** RAG-GATE answered 77–79% of dated questions and 91–93% on what follows, against the learner's 64–69% and 72–77%.
- **What the learner offers over RAG-GATE** is no retrieval step at answer time and its trained skills (code rewrites cannot be retrieved as facts; CCL-4's H6). The preregistration stated this as an argument, not a measured win, and the numbers do not change that.
