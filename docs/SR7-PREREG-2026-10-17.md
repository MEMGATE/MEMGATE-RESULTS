The output is in [`runs/sr7-dev-20261017-a`](../runs/sr7-dev-20261017-a/SUMMARY.md): 7,335 runs, 1,063 s, 0 failures, all 576 determinism repeats identical. The exposed real data are 56 datasets and the three streams.

**Findings.**

- **The pramāṇa assembly helps a little where the four means disagree:** splice +2.5, soybean +1.4, optical digits +1.5.
- **YUGA swings strongly by dataset.**
  - It helps on noisy, few-class data: flare +8.2, Haberman +6.1, post-operative +5.8, balance +5.6, and the airlines stream +3.4.
  - It hurts on small or many-class data, where seven neighbours smother rare classes: zoo −9.4, audiology −8.7, letter −5.4.
- **BADHA3 helps under noise and hurts under drift,** like the other noise-tolerant rules before it.
- **No bug was found,** and nothing changed after the dev run.
