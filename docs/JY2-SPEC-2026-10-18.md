# JY-2: pañcadhā maitrī, five grades of standing from natural and temporary relation, as vote weights in the pariṣad (Bṛhat Jātaka II.15–18)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any JY-2 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Source and mapping

**Source.** Varāhamihira's Bṛhat Jātaka II.15–18 and the note to II.18, in Aiyar's translation (1905), as recorded by Codex: `docs/research/jyotisa-study-20261017/STRUCTURE_REGISTER.json`, rows JY-16 to JY-18, on Codex's branch (read only).
- **Natural relation (II.15–17).** One planet regards another as friend, neutral or enemy. The relation is directed.
- **Temporary relation (II.18).** A planet in the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 10th, 11th or 12th place from another is its temporary friend; elsewhere, its temporary enemy.
- **The compound relation (note to II.18).** Five named classes:
  - natural friend + temporary friend = great friend;
  - natural neutral + temporary friend = friend;
  - natural friend + temporary enemy = neutral;
  - natural neutral + temporary enemy = enemy;
  - natural enemy + temporary enemy = great enemy.

  Codex notes that the row for natural enemy + temporary friend was not extracted. I complete it as neutral, by symmetry with natural friend + temporary enemy. This completion is mine.

**Why it is tested now.** Jyotiṣa entered the register on 18 October from Codex's study. This is its one candidate with a learning reading beyond what earlier tests cover; vargas are covered in principle by PN-1 and T-1.

**The mapping (mine).** The pariṣad of P-1 and P-2 has three members: R-ORD+ANU+AKH, naive Bayes and the decision tree. Every member sees every label.

**The natural relation is the long-run record.**
- At each case, a member's accuracy on all earlier cases ranks it among the three.
- The best is a friend, the middle one neutral, the worst an enemy. Ties take the better rank.

**The temporary relation is the present position.**
- A member is a temporary friend if its accuracy over the last 30 cases is at least the mean of the three members' accuracies there.
- Otherwise it is a temporary enemy.

**The grades weight the vote.**

| Grade | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| great friend | 4 |
| friend | 3 |
| neutral | 2 |
| enemy | 1 |
| great enemy | 0 |

The weights are the grades' order, since the source gives no numeric law (JY-19). The answer is the label with the largest total weight. Ties go to the earliest member among the tied labels' voters, as in plurality.

## 2. Arms

All arms use the same three members, so one pass gives every arm.

| Arm | Vote weights |
