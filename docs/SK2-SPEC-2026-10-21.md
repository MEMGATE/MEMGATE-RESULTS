# SK-2 spec: the same skills, learned in the weights

**Status:** spec, 21 October 2026. The two training jobs were submitted at 16:00 on `jayeshupp-8050-resource`; their ids and the SHA-256 of their training files are in `sk1/sk2-jobs.json`. No question has been asked.
**Code:** `sk1/sk2.py`, built on SK-1 (`sk1/sk1.py`, `docs/SK1-PREREG-2026-10-21.md`).

## Why
- **SK-1 tests skills in memory.** When half its migration recipes are an attacker's, three of six models follow them and their code breaks. A library holding only the recipes that hold in the current release fixes that.
- **Fine-tuning is the other way to keep a skill.**
  - RP-2 found that fine-tuning teaches a fact but not what follows from it.
  - SK-2 asks the weights-route questions for skills. Does fine-tuning on the verified recipes help a model rewrite real programs? Does fine-tuning on a poisoned library make it write invented functions and packages?

## Design
**Arms.** Each tuned arm is one GPT-4.1 job at FT-2's settings (6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2), trained on the 24 pandas changes. Each change gets three rows: its question in two phrasings, answered with the usage, and the recipe itself.
- **GATED:** the recipes SK-1's gate admits, which are the true ones.
- **POISONED:** SK-1's poisoned library, where 12 of the 24 recipes are the attackers'.
- **FROZEN:** the untouched gpt-4.1-base.

**Test.** Every model rewrites SK-1's programs with no library in the prompt. A rewrite passes when it runs in pandas 3.0.6 and prints what the original printed under 2.2.3, scored as in SK-1. The two models are trained once.
- Dev asks them about programs 0–9.
- Main asks about programs 10–39, after the preregistration.

**Hypotheses** (to be fixed after dev, as in SK-1):
- **H1:** passed, GATED − POISONED.
- **H2:** used a fabricated name, POISONED − GATED.
- **H3:** passed, GATED − FROZEN, two-sided.

## Limits
- One model and one library. There are only 72 training rows per arm.
- The programs reuse the changes the model was trained on, so the test is whether the trained recipes are applied inside whole programs, not transfer to new changes.
