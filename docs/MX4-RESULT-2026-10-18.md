## What this means

**The blind spot is real on fresh real data, and it can be large.**
- On the Beijing pollution stream, the assembly that asks only under doubt answered 92% of hours alone and was right on only 56%.
- Its members agreed on "within the limit" through pollution episodes and never asked.
- Auditing 5% of its unanimous answers brought it to 81.7%. It now answered 62% of hours alone and asked a person about 38%.
- On the benzene stream, which is easier, the gain was small (+0.7).

**On these streams, the audits do the work, not the reset.**
- **Why the audits are enough here:** an audited case that turns out wrong teaches the members, which makes them disagree again. Disagreement brings more asking (the share of labels rose from 8% to 38% on Beijing).
- **Where the reset helped:** on synthetic worlds with a hard switch (MX-3: +4.74 pp beyond audits) and on the regime-changing activity streams of MX-3's dev run. Pollution and sensor seasons change more gradually.

**What a working system needs, as tested so far:**

| Part | Status |
| --- | --- |
| An assembly of different learners | replicated |
| Ask a person only when members disagree or doubt | replicated |
| Answer alone only when all agree | replicated |
| **Audit a small share of its confident answers** | **essential**: without it, an abrupt or seasonal change can go unnoticed (MX-3, MX-4) |
| Reset on a diagnosed vikṛti | helps under a hard switch; not needed where change is gradual |
