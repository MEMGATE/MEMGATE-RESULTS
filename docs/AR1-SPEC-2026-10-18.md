# AR-1: three different sources, credence on agreement, and dismissal of the source that frequently differs (Arthaśāstra 1.12)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any AR-1 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Source and mapping

**Arthaśāstra 1.12** (Shamasastry's translation, 1915, Book I, Chapter XII, "Institution of spies"; checked on Wikisource):

> "When the information thus received from these three different sources is exactly of the same version, it shall be held reliable. If they (the three sources) frequently differ, the spies concerned shall either be punished in secret or dismissed."

Kangle's translation of the first sentence, as quoted by P. K. Gautam (IDSA Monograph 20, 2013), reads: "when there is agreement in the reports of three (spies), credence should be given."

**Why it is tested now.**
- The Arthaśāstra is not yet in the coverage register.
- Its rule for intelligence is a rule for information that arrives through several independent sources. A supervisor that watches a site through several cameras or sensors is in the same position.

**The mapping (mine).**
- **Three different sources.** There are three spies. Each is the same kind of learner (R-ORD), and each sees only its own source of evidence: a sensor, a feature set, a noisy copy of the scene. They differ in what they see, not in how they learn. The pariṣad (P-1, MX-2) is the other way round: its members differ in kind and all see everything.
- **Credence on agreement.**
  - The network answers alone only when all its trusted spies report the same label, and none of them is guessing.
  - A spy is guessing when JK-1's doubt holds: no lesson decides, or the deciding lessons have fewer than 6 supports in total. A guess is not information received.
  - Otherwise it asks a person for the label.
  - The spies learn only from asked cases, as in MX-3.
  - **Amended before any dev run.** The first code had no guessing clause, and a smoke check found the network trapped at once. After one case, every spy's fallback gives that case's label. The three then always agree, so the network never asks again: coverage 100%, 0.3% labels. ML-6 met the same trap; MX-2 and MX-3 avoid it with the same doubt rule.
- **Frequent difference leads to dismissal.**
  - Each time the trusted spies split two against one, the one is the spy that differs.
  - A spy is dismissed if it differed in at least 20 of the last 30 such splits.
  - With three equally reliable spies, each differs in about a third of splits. Twenty of thirty then has a probability below 0.1%.
  - A dismissed spy is no longer heard, and the network gives credence when the two remaining spies agree.
  - At most one spy is dismissed. Secret punishment and dismissal both remove the spy's influence, so they are modelled as one.

## 2. Arms

| Arm | Content |
| --- | --- |
| SPIES | three spies, no dismissal |
| **SPIES+DISMISS** | Arthaśāstra 1.12 in full |
| SPIES+RANDOM-DISMISS | dismisses a spy chosen at random (`random.Random(seed + 1212)`) at the case where SPIES+DISMISS dismisses one, if it does. It controls for the choice of spy. |

## 3. Data: each dataset sound and with a failing source

Every dataset is run twice:
- **SOUND:** all sources work.
- **FAILING:** one source fails at the middle case.
  - From then on, each of its values is replaced by that column's value at a random case of the same dataset.
  - The failing source and the replacements are drawn from `random.Random(f"AR1-FAIL:{seed}")`.

### The synthetic VIEWS family

- A hidden case is drawn as in EXC (12 features), and its label comes from EXC rules.
- Each of the three sources sees a noisy copy of the hidden case. Each value is kept with probability 0.9 and otherwise replaced by a uniform random value.
- This gives 36 features and 400 cases.
- **Amended before any dev run: 0.8 became 0.9.**
  - With the guessing clause, at 0.8 the spies rarely held a well-supported lesson. The sound network asked 77–92% of cases (smoke check, seeds 0–4). Dismissal never found the failed spy, because one more noisy source changed little.
  - At 0.9 the sound network answered alone 27–38% of cases (seeds 0–3).
  - This is the smallest change that gives a regime where agreement can be heard at all. The real datasets are unaffected.

### Real data with natural sources (main; new to the lab)

| Dataset | Cases | Classes | The three sources |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Multiple Features (UCI 72): handwritten numerals | 2,000 | 10 | 76 Fourier coefficients; 64 Karhunen–Loève coefficients; 47 Zernike moments |
| MHEALTH (UCI 319): 10 volunteers, 12 activities | see below | 12 | chest; left ankle; right lower arm |

**Multiple Features.** The same 2,000 digits appeared in PV-1 through their six morphological features (`mfeat-mor`). The three feature sets used here were never loaded.

**MHEALTH.** The sensors record at 50 Hz.
- A case is a 1-second window: 50 consecutive samples within one activity of one volunteer.
- The null label 0 is dropped, and a window that crosses an activity boundary is dropped.
- A window's features are the mean and standard deviation of each channel:
  - chest: 3 accelerations and 2 ECG leads, giving 10 features;
  - ankle: 9 channels, giving 18;
  - arm: 9 channels, giving 18.

**Orders.** Each real dataset runs in 10 orders, cut to 1,000 cases. Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule.

## 4. Measures

Each run is scored on cases t ≥ 1.

**Net credence.** The right answers the network gave alone, less its wrong ones, per 100 scored cases. This is Chow's risk with a question costing half a wrong answer.

**Descriptive measures:**
- coverage, meaning the share of cases answered alone;
- accuracy alone;
- labels asked;
- whether, when and which spy was dismissed.

## 5. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before. The CI is stratified by dataset group: VIEWS, Multiple Features and MHEALTH.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | SPIES+DISMISS − SPIES, net credence, FAILING |
| H2 | SPIES+DISMISS − SPIES+RANDOM-DISMISS, net credence, FAILING. Does choosing the right spy matter? |
| H3 | SPIES+DISMISS − SPIES, net credence, SOUND. The cost of a false dismissal. |
| H4 (the first sentence) | SPIES, SOUND: accuracy alone − accuracy on as many random scored cases (`random.Random(seed + 999)`) |
| Descriptive | Detection: the failed spy dismissed, and the delay after the failure. False dismissals when SOUND. Coverage, accuracy alone and labels asked. |

**INVALID if:**
- any run is non-deterministic or crashes;
- SPIES+DISMISS never dismisses in any FAILING run (the mechanism is inert).

## 6. Predictions before any AR-1 code

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | positive, WIN | After the failure, three reports rarely agree. Dismissal restores credence. |
| H2 | positive, WIN | A random dismissal removes a working spy two times in three and leaves the failed one in. |
| H3 | ≈ 0, slightly negative | A weak but working source, such as the chest sensor, may be dismissed. |
| H4 | WIN | Agreement between different sources is strong evidence. |
