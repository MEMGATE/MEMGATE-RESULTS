**Finding.** On these two small, noisy real datasets, the model's own lessons *lowered* its accuracy by 1.6–2.1 pp, and the models stayed below the offline lesson learner. Noisy real labels give the model few clean rules to write. The two pariṣads tie.

No bug was found, and nothing changed after the dev run.
