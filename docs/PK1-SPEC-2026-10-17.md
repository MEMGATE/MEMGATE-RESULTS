# PK-1: prakṛti and vikṛti (Caraka Saṃhitā) as a learner's own health check, with targeted elimination

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any PK-1 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Source and mapping

The source is Caraka Saṃhitā, Sūtrasthāna 9.4. It was checked against the Caraka Samhita Online edition (Khuddākacatuṣpāda Adhyāya):

> *vikāro dhātuvaiṣamyaṃ sāmyaṃ prakṛtir ucyate*
>
> "Disequilibrium of dhātus is disease and their equilibrium is health."

Āyurveda judges health against a person's own constitution (*prakṛti*), not against a universal norm. Its purifying therapy (*śodhana*) removes what has become vitiated; it does not replace the body.

The coverage register lists prakṛti vs vikṛti as "the same shape as J-1, not tested separately". J-1 chose among experts by standing. This test is different: it is a learner judging its *own* state against its own baseline.

**The mapping is mine.**

- **Prakṛti, the learner's own equilibrium:** its error rate since its last treatment, leaving out the latest W = 30 scored cases.
- **Vikṛti, disequilibrium:** the error rate over the latest W cases exceeds the prakṛti by more than three standard errors. The standard error is computed from max(prakṛti, 0.05) over W cases. At least 2W scored cases since the last treatment are required.
- **Śodhana, the treatment:** every active lesson is dissolved that decided wrongly at least once among the latest W cases, or descends by revision from one that did. The case memory is kept. Prakṛti is measured afresh from the treatment on.
  - *Amended after dev run a, before the freeze.* As first written, the rule dissolved only the erring lessons themselves.
  - That rule was vacuous: PRAKRTI equalled R-ORD exactly, because R-ORD supersedes a lesson at its first counterexample.
  - The vitiated knowledge lives on in that lesson's revisions, so those are dissolved too.

## 2. Arms

| Arm | Content |
| --- | --- |
| R-ORD | baseline |
| **PRAKRTI** | R-ORD with the health check and śodhana |
| RANDOM-SODHANA | as many treatments as PRAKRTI had in the same run, at random scored cases from 2W on (`random.Random(window seed + 333)`) |
| PRAKRTI-RESET | the same check, but the treatment rebuilds the learner from the latest W cases (a common drift response) |
| R-ORD-KALA | T-1's Kālacakra recency layers, the earlier winner under change (reference) |
| EXT-LAST | no-change (reference) |

## 3. Data

**Real streams.** Three OpenML streams, new to the lab, in their natural order. Each runs as 10 windows of 1,000 consecutive cases, starting at ⌊i·(N − 1000)/9⌋. Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule over the whole stream.

| Stream | OpenML id | Cases | Classes | Reading |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| ldpa (person activity, UCI) | 1483 | 164,860 | 11 | Person and tag are categories; the timestamp and date are dropped |
| Run or walk (iPhone sensors) | 40922 | 88,588 | 2 | 6 sensor readings |
| Gesture phase segmentation (UCI) | 4538 | 9,873 | 5 | 32 velocity and acceleration features |

**Synthetic worlds.** All five families, at seeds 29000–29019. DRIFT has a known switch of its rules halfway.

**Dev.** The exposed streams (elec2, airlines, gasdrift) and the synthetic dev seeds.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before. On the streams, the CI is stratified by stream. On synthetic data, the CI is per family over its 20 seeds.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | PRAKRTI − R-ORD on the three real streams |
| H2 | PRAKRTI − RANDOM-SODHANA on the real streams |
| H3 | PRAKRTI-RESET − R-ORD on the real streams |
| H4 | PRAKRTI − R-ORD on synthetic DRIFT |
| Descriptive | the stable families (false alarms), treatments per run, PRAKRTI − R-ORD-KALA, EXT-LAST |

## 5. Predictions before any PK-1 code

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | ≈ 0, NO WIN | The windows are short, and R-ORD already revises lessons that fail. |
| H2 | small positive | Timing matters only when real change occurs. |

| H4 | positive | DRIFT's switch is exactly the case the check is for. |
