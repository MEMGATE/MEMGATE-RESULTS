# SK-1: saṃskāra and vāsanā: retired lessons as latent impressions that re-manifest when their conditions return (Yoga Sūtra 4.8–4.9)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any SK-1 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Source and mapping

**Yoga Sūtra 4.9** (verified against several translations):

> *jāti-deśa-kāla-vyavahitānām apy ānantaryaṃ smṛti-saṃskārayor eka-rūpatvāt*
>
> "There is a relation of cause and effect even though separated by class, locality and time, because memory and impressions have the same form." (Taimni)

**Yoga Sūtra 4.8** (the standard text, cited from the usual reading):

> *tatas tad-vipāka-anuguṇānām evābhivyaktir vāsanānām*
>
> From that, only those latent tendencies (vāsanā) manifest that suit the ripening of that karma.

**Why it is tested now.** The Vṛtti–Saṃskāra learning graph is one of the user's eight graphs. Two parts of it were tested before:
- the vṛtti side: lessons written by models, with admission filters (ML-1, ML-2);
- saṃskāra as blocking: covered in principle by apavāda and sṛṣṭi.

Saṃskāra as a latent impression that persists and re-manifests when its conditions return was never tested.

**The mapping (mine).**
- **Becoming latent.** When a validated lesson meets a counterexample and is revised away, its conditions and conclusion are kept as a latent impression. A latent impression decides nothing.
- **Ripening.** Each later observed case that meets an impression's conditions ripens it if the case carries the impression's conclusion. It resets the ripening if not.
- **Re-manifestation.** When an impression has ripened on R consecutive meeting cases, and no active lesson with the same conditions concludes otherwise, it re-manifests as a validated lesson (YS 4.8: what suits the ripening manifests).
- **Why the bar is lower.** R = 2, below the 3 supports a fresh lesson needs: the impression is already formed (YS 4.9: memory and impression are of one form).

**Why this differs from the plain learner.** R-ORD can never form that lesson again. The old counterexamples stay in its case memory, and a lesson must be pure over memory to form. An impression bypasses this through its recent ripening.

## 3. Data

**A new synthetic family, RECUR.** It has two rule sets, as in DRIFT, alternating A, B, A, B over four quarters of 100 cases each (400 cases). It is generated like DRIFT, from `random.Random("RECUR:<seed>")`, and is not added to the shared family list.

**Main.** RECUR and the five existing families at the new seeds 40000–40019. The existing families show the cost where nothing recurs.

**Dev.** RECUR and the five families at the synthetic dev seeds, and the nine exposed real streams.
