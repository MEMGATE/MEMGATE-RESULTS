## The setting

SE-3's world (real 2024–2026 offices from Wikidata, honest, stealthy and colluding sources), with **sleepers**:
- Two sources report truly and on time, building trust.

- No report contradicts them yet, so any corroboration rule admits the lie, and it is trained into the weights.
- One session later, honest reports and a registry check expose the lie.

## What this means

**A corrected lie is not a removed lie.**
- **What looks fixed:** after the truth was reported and trained, every engine arm answered the truth.
- **What remains underneath:** the weights still carried the lie. ENGINE gave the false holder about 3.7 nats per token more than a never-taught name, about forty times the probability per token. It preferred the lie on 93.5% of targets.
- **Why it matters:** a trace like this can resurface under other prompts. It also means a "fixed" model still contains what an attacker put there. For poisoning cleanup and for deletion requests alike, correction is not enough.

**The ledger stops most lies from entering.**
- **The rule:** a fabrication (a value never true, overturned by the registry) exposes its sources at once, and they are dismissed (Arthaśāstra 1.12, Yoga Sūtra 1.8).
- **The effect:** 7 lie reports were taught per world instead of 50. The overall trace fell from 93.5% to 54.5% (H2).
- **What it cannot undo:** the lies taught before the catch keep their full trace, 89.3%, the same as ENGINE's.

**Pratiprasava removes what was taught** (Yoga Sūtra 2.10: abandon the affliction by tracing it back to its origin).
- **The mechanism:** the evidence graph records which trusted sources each taught value rested on. When a source is caught, the adapter is restored to its state before the first session that taught one of its fabrications, and the sessions since are replayed without them.
- **The cost:** about one session's replay, 7% of a full retrain.
- **On the lies actually taught,** the trace fell from 89.3% to 67.9%, against 57.1% for the never-taught model. The mean gap fell from +3.77 to +0.77 nats per token, against +0.46. That is about nine-tenths of the excess removed.
- **Nothing was lost:** current holders, locality, retention and poison resistance were unchanged.

**What the residue may be.** The small residue left on those 28 facts (+0.31 nats over the never-taught model) is within what the data can explain:
- Five of the 28 false names are holders of other offices in the same world, so the model learned them legitimately elsewhere. None of the control names were.
- 28 facts is a small sample.

## Limits, stated plainly

- **The sleepers are scripted.** They were caught one session after their first lie, because honest reports and the registry arrive quickly. A slower exposure means a longer replay, and the cost grows with the number of sessions since the first tainted one.
- **Exactness depends on the registry's history** for telling a fabrication from a superseded truth.
- **The trace probe has one form,** the likelihood of the false name under the trained phrasing. Other probes were not run: other phrasings, multi-hop prompts, extraction attacks.
- **A deletion request** (removing an honest source's contributions entirely) is deferred.
- **The facts are real, the sources simulated,** as in SE-3.
