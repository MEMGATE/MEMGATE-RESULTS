## What it shows
- **Skills can be learned into the weights, and it helps.** Training on the 24 verified migration recipes tripled the pass rate on whole programs: 20% → 60%. Each program needs three of the recipes at once.
- **What goes into training decides what comes out.** The model trained on the poisoned library wrote functions and arguments that do not exist in 53% of programs. It passed fewer programs (10%) than the untouched model (20%).
  - In main, every invented name it used belonged to one of the program's own three changes. The library-wide and the narrower count agree (53.3%). In dev they differed by one program.
- **Together with SK-1:** a poisoned skill library breaks programs both when it is read from memory (SK-1) and when it is trained into the weights (SK-2). The gate in front of either keeps the invented recipes out.

## Limits
- **Scale:** one model (GPT-4.1), one library of 24 changes, 72 training rows per arm, 30 programs.
- **No transfer test:** the programs use the changes the models were trained on. The test is applying trained recipes inside whole programs, not carrying them to new changes.
- **The checks:** execution and printed output, not a person.
