# TQ-1 spec: questions about a date, before and after a change, from a dated memory

**Status:** spec, written 22 October 2026, about 14:40 IST. Dev is next; no main question has been asked.

## Why
A deployed system is asked about the past as well as the present: "Who was the head of government of Hungary on 12 November 2025?"
- **Fine-tuning on the new value** overwrites the old one. RP-3 showed this: the PREVIOUS answers of the fine-tuned models fell below the untouched model's.
- **A memory that keeps only the current value** gives no date.
- **The layer's memory line gives three things:** the current value, the date it changed and the value before (RP-1's GATED+).

TQ-1 asks whether the dated line lets models answer date questions on both sides of a change. It also asks whether a memory without dates misleads them about the past.

## Items (`cl/tq1.py`)
- **Offices:** RP-1's pool after the 350 offices that RP-1 and CH-1 asked about, the same pool as CH-2: TM world 8000, real changes from 2024–26.
  - Dev takes the first 50 offices that qualify among the next 100.
  - Main takes the first 300 that qualify among the rest.
- **Dates,** from the record's change date:
  - **BEFORE:** halfway through the previous holder's tenure (from 1 January 2024 at the earliest). The right answer is the previous holder.
  - **AFTER:** halfway between the change and today (19 October 2026). The right answer is the current holder.
- **An office qualifies when:**
  - the previous holder's tenure before the change is at least 60 days in the record;
  - the change is at least 14 days before today.

## Questions, conditions and models
- **The question:** "Who was the ROLE of PLACE on DATE?", with "Answer with the person's full name only.", dated as RP-1's ("Today is 2026-10-19.").
- **Conditions:** RP-1's NONE, GATED (the current value only) and GATED+ (the value, the change date and the value before), word for word.
- **Models:** RP-1's six.

## Scoring
The TM world's name scorer:
- **right:** the date's holder is named and the other holder is not;
- **other holder:** the reverse;
- **both** or **other** otherwise.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev)
An item-level bootstrap of the mean over models, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345 (CH-1's analysis).

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | BEFORE right, GATED+ − GATED | WIN if the interval lies wholly above +1 pp and every model's difference is above 0 |
| H2 | AFTER right, GATED+ − NONE | the same |
| H3 | AFTER right, GATED+ − GATED | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −2 pp |
| D1 (descriptive) | BEFORE right, GATED − NONE | — |

D1 asks whether a memory without dates costs answers about the past.

## Limits
- **Office holders only, one change per office:** the latest change and the holder just before it.
- **Dates fall inside the record's tenures.** The record is Wikidata's as of 18 October 2026.
