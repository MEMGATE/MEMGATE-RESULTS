## Rules fixed in the code

**Spies.**
- A spy is an R-ORD learner that sees only its source's columns.
- A spy is guessing when JK-1's doubt holds: no lesson decides, or the deciding lessons have fewer than 6 supports in total.

**Credence.**
- The network answers alone only when all its trusted spies report the same label and none is guessing.
- Otherwise it asks, and every trusted spy learns the label.

**Dismissal.**
- Each 2-against-1 split of the three spies records the odd one.
- A spy that is the odd one in at least 20 of the last 30 splits is dismissed and no longer heard. At most one spy is dismissed.
- SPIES+RANDOM-DISMISS dismisses a random spy (`random.Random(seed + 1212)`) at the case where SPIES+DISMISS dismissed, if it did.

**Failure.**
- From the middle case on, one source, drawn from `random.Random(f"AR1-FAIL:{seed}")`, is replaced value by value.
- Each replacement is the same column's value at a random case of the same dataset.

**Measures.**
- Net credence is (right − wrong answers given alone) / scored cases. Case 0 is not scored.
- H4 compares accuracy alone with the network's answers on as many random scored cases (`random.Random(seed + 999)`).

**INVALID conditions:**
- non-determinism;
- a crashed run;
- SPIES+DISMISS never dismissing a spy in a FAILING run.

**Smoke checks.** These found the two problems fixed by the amendments (see the spec).

**Detection.**

| Data | Failed spy dismissed | Mean delay after the failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VIEWS | 25 of 25 | 66 cases |
| room | 10 of 10 | 150 cases |
| occupancy | 7 of 10 | 145 cases |

On occupancy, 2 dismissals came before the failure. In SOUND runs, nothing was dismissed on VIEWS or room. On occupancy, the weak temperature–humidity source was dismissed in 5 of 10 runs. That raised coverage at no cost in accuracy, because light and CO₂ alone are near perfect there.

**Findings.**
- After a source fails, three agreeing reports become rare: coverage falls from about 35% to 23–25%.
- The frequently differing spy is found within 66–150 cases. Dismissing it restores credence without loss of accuracy.
- Dismissing a random spy recovers less than half as much.
