## What this means

**The core of the recipe now holds on three real settings:**
- a hydraulic rig (MX-5);
- two feature sets and body sensors (AR-1);
- three body-worn IMUs (MX-7).

The core is to act only when independent sources agree and none is guessing, and to dismiss the source that keeps being the odd one out:
- dismissal recovered credence after a sensor failed (+12.85);
- it found the broken sensor every time, within about 30 seconds of data;
- it never dismissed a working one.

**Not every piece replicated.**
- Saṃskāra inside the supervisor added +2.56 on the rig but ≈ 0 here. With ST-1's loss on time-ordered streams, its value is setting-dependent.
- Resets cost again, and audits are neutral.

**The precondition from MX-6 held.**
- Here each IMU alone could often name the activity: sound coverage was 14%, at 99.5% accuracy.
- On the naval simulator, no source could. That is the line between where the recipe works and where it cannot.

**For a working system**, the tested core is:
- several independent cameras or sensors, each able to recognise the event on its own in a good share of cases;
- act only on agreement without guessing;
- ask the owner otherwise;
- flag and stop trusting the source that is the odd one in 20 of its last 30 disagreements.
