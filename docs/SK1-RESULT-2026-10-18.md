## What this means

**Yoga Sūtra 4.8–4.9 holds in its main claim.**
- Impressions of retired lessons stay latent and return when their conditions return.
- Where the world comes back to an earlier state, this beat the plain learner by +2.22 pp on fresh worlds.
- The plain learner can never form a lesson again once a counterexample has refuted it.
- It also helped under label noise (+2.22). There, a sound lesson broken by one mislabelled case comes back.

**The lower bar does not help.**
- The mapping let an impression return after two confirmations, below the three a fresh lesson needs ("memory and impression are of one form").
- Three confirmations were better (+0.66). R = 3 beat the plain learner by about +2.9 on RECUR and +2.6 on NOISE.
- The gain comes from letting a refuted lesson return at all, not from its returning faster.

**The cost.**
- Where a counterexample was a true exception, a returned lesson is wrong again: EXC −1.28, UPA −0.78, DRIFT −0.55.
- Harmful lesson applications rise by about half (RECUR 16.4% → 22.4% of cases).
- Over the five families that do not recur, gains and losses cancel (−0.10).

**A bound is needed in practice.**
- In dev, on a noisy 58-feature news stream, lessons broke and returned without end: 31,331 returns and 79,522 lessons within 750 cases.
- The run time grew quadratically, so news was left out of the dev run.
- On RECUR, about 0.8 lessons return per case.

**For a working system** (for example a warehouse whose day and night shifts, or weekdays and weekends, alternate):
- keep retired rules as dormant impressions;
- let a rule return after three fresh confirmations;
- cap the store, as the news stream shows.
