# MX-1: every earned piece in one system, the grand pariṣad

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any MX-1 code, after the PN-1 result. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

Offline, five things have earned a place on held-out real data:

| Piece | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| A pariṣad of learners of different kinds | P-1 +1.44 pp; P-2 +1.56 and +1.60 pp; all WIN |
| Range literals over ordered bins (Pāṇinian pratyāhāra) | PN-1 +1.19 pp, WIN |
| Anupalabdhi | SR-3 +0.34 pp; H-1 +0.45 pp; significant |
| Akhyāti | SR-6 +0.61 pp, significant |

P-2 showed that small mechanisms inside the lesson member add ≈ 0 to an assembly.

**Question.** Does the *best single lesson learner* (all earned pieces) inside the *best assembly* still improve on the plain assembly? And is the combined system the strongest one so far?

## 2. Arms
