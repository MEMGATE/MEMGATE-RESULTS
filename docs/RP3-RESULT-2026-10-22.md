The arms:
- **DIRECT:** the direct fact, for every office.
- **FOLLOW:** DIRECT plus, for half A, the statements that follow from the change: which post the new holder holds, whom they replaced, that the previous holder no longer holds it and that the new one does.
- **MORE:** DIRECT, with half A repeated to FOLLOW's size.

## What it shows
**A fact trained alone is held in contradiction with what follows from it.** Fine-tuned on the direct fact, GPT-4.1 named the new holder for 98% of offices. Asked whether that person holds the post now, it said yes for 7.3%. Asked whom they replaced, it was right for 22.7%, less than the untouched model's 47.3%.

**Training on what follows fixes this where it is trained.** FOLLOW answered those questions for 82–92% of half A. MORE, with as many training rows, did not (11–22%), so the gain comes from the statements, not the volume.

**It also transfers.** On half B, trained only on the direct fact, FOLLOW's follow-on score was 36.4% against MORE's 16.2% (+20.22 pp).
- The STILL pair rose from 10.7% to 44.0%, and PREVIOUS from 20.0% to 44.0%.
- REVERSE did not transfer (21.3% against 18.0%).
- The model learned something general about carrying a change, not only the trained statements: the weights-level part of learning to learn. It repeated across sizes and families (part 1): mini +15.78, nano +5.33, Llama-3.3-70B +5.11 pp.

**Every fine-tuning job cost untouched knowledge.** Asked directly, with no routing, the untouched GPT-4.1 answered 90.3% of the 300 offices no arm was trained on. After one fine-tuning job on 300 other offices, each arm answered 25.3–29.7%.
- This is collateral damage at frontier scale, from one hosted job. gpt-4.1-nano went from 89.3% to 0.0% in part 1.
- The layer avoids it by routing a question about a fact the model was not trained on to the untouched model.

## Limits
- **One job per arm** at RP-3's settings (6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2), on 300 real offices. Transfer is measured on facts trained in the same job.
- **The follow-on statements are written by the lab** from the verified change (four kinds). Other kinds of consequence are not trained or tested.
- **Locality is asked without routing.** It measures the weights, not the layer.
