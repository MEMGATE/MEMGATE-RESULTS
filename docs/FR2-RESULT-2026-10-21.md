## What it shows
- **The predictor works from similar past cases.** For a stored value, the memory can say how likely it is to still hold, from how often values of the same kind of post, held for a similar time, survived the same span in the past. Those probabilities are calibrated to within 0.014 on average on offices never used to build them.
- **What it allows:**
  - an answer can say "as of the check date, with probability p that it still holds";
  - the gate can re-check first the values least likely to hold.
- **Where it errs:** the middle bins are overconfident by 0.05–0.11. They hold about 2% of checks.

## Limits
- **Domain:** office holders only. The tenure bands and horizons are coarse.
- **What was tested:** calibration only. Answering with "as of" and a confidence, and the re-check order, are not tested here.
- **Selection:** the snapshot's selection of offices is handled by the look-again cutoff, not removed.
