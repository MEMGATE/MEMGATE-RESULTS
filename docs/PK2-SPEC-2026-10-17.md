# PK-2: does the timing of the prakṛti–vikṛti diagnosis matter, or only resetting?

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any PK-2 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

In [PK-1](PK1-RESULT-2026-10-17.md), a reset at the moments the health check diagnosed vikṛti won: +4.28 pp on fresh streams and +9.61 on DRIFT. PK-1 had a random-time control only for śodhana, not for the reset. So PK-1 cannot tell whether the gain needs the diagnosis or comes from resetting as such. PK-2 separates the two, as Y-1 did for SR-7's yuga fallback.

## 2. Arms

Every arm is R-ORD; a reset rebuilds it from the latest W = 30 cases, exactly as in PK-1's code.

| Arm | Reset times |
| --- | --- |
| R-ORD | never |
| **RESET-VIKRTI** | when the health check diagnoses vikṛti (PK-1's PRAKRTI-RESET, unchanged) |
| RESET-RANDOM | as many resets as RESET-VIKRTI made in the same run, at random cases from 2W on (`random.Random(seed + 444)`) |
| RESET-PERIODIC | as many resets, evenly spaced over the cases from 2W on |
| R-ORD-KALA | never; Kālacakra recency layers (reference) |
| EXT-LAST | no-change (reference) |

## 3. Data

**Real streams.** Three UCI streams, new to the lab, in their natural order. Each runs as 10 windows of 1,000 consecutive cases, as in PK-1.

| Stream | UCI id | Cases | Classes | Reading |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Online news popularity (Mashable, 2013–2014, by publication date) | 332 | 39,644 | 2 | `url` (an identifier) and `timedelta` (a time index the documentation calls non-predictive) are dropped. 58 attributes. The label is popular if shares ≥ 1,400, the decision threshold of the dataset's paper. |
| Activity of older people with a batteryless wearable sensor | 427 | 75,128 | 4 | Room setting 1, then 2; within each, the person files in name order, each in time order. The time column is dropped; the antenna id is a category. |
| Steel industry energy, load type (2018, every 15 minutes) | 851 | 35,040 | 3 | The date is dropped. Seconds since midnight, week status and day of week are kept. |

**Synthetic worlds.** All five families, at seeds 30000–30019.

**Dev.** The exposed streams: SR-5's three and PK-1's three.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before. On the streams, the CI is stratified by stream. On synthetic data, the CI is per family over its seeds.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | RESET-VIKRTI − RESET-RANDOM on the three new streams |
| H2 | RESET-VIKRTI − RESET-PERIODIC on the streams |
| H3 | RESET-VIKRTI − R-ORD on the streams (replication of PK-1's H3) |
| H4 | RESET-VIKRTI − RESET-RANDOM on synthetic DRIFT |
| Descriptive | the stable families; R-ORD-KALA; EXT-LAST; resets per run |
