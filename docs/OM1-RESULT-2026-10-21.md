## What this means
- **Meta's open model is moved by the same forged line,** less than the others: 40.5% of attacked offices against 85–100%. It also reads the raw records less well: 65.2% current, against about 95%.
- **With the gate's memory it says no attacker value,** in either domain. Its current answers rise to 92.4% on offices and 77.7% on code:
  - +27 points on offices, more than any other model;
  - +23 points on code, about as much as gpt-4.1 (+22.9).

  A verified block helps a weaker reader more than a stronger one.
- **Every model tested from four labs** (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI and now Meta) answers an attacker's value from a forged memory line on 37–100% of attacked items. With the gate's memory, none do on offices, and at most 0.7% do on code.
