## What this means

**The reading has two parts, and the test separates them.**

**First: evidence has a locus.** Kiraṇa 6 says smoke in one tree does not prove fire in another. In Nyāya, the reason must be a property of the subject.
- Grouping by the real loci beat the same rule over random groups of the same sizes, by +1.04 pp (WIN).
- Naming the locus as an ordinary feature helped by +0.72 (garment teams +1.8).
- So where cases come from carries information about them.

**Second: withhold a general lesson until it has been seen at the locus.** This costs.
- −0.76 pp against ignoring the locus, and −1.48 against simply naming it as a feature.
- It helped a little where loci differ, across hospitals (+0.5) and teams (+0.8).
- It hurt where they share one reality: twelve stations measuring one city's air (−3.6).
- **Why:** a sound general lesson is silenced at every new locus until it has been confirmed there, and the fallback then has only a few local cases.

**For a working system:** record where each case comes from, and let lessons use it. Do not refuse general knowledge at a new site. This matches the dev run on exposed data.
