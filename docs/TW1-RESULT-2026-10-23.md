## What it shows
- **A change trained into the weights overwrote the past, with or without its date.**
  - Trained on the new holder (CURRENT), on the change without its date (UNDATED) or with its date (DATED), each model named the new holder for 95–100% of questions about a date before the change. They were right for at most 0.3%.
  - The date in the trained rows made no difference (H2: 0.0 to +0.33 points).
- **The dated memory line kept both dates better** (H1 WIN on all three): both dates were right for 17–68% of offices, against at most 0.3% for DATED.
  - The 7–8B models read the line imperfectly. OLMo-2 missed many past dates (37.7% right). Nemotron-Nano read the past (83.3%) but named the previous holder for 47.7% of after-change dates.
  - Frontier models read the same line almost perfectly (TQ-1: gpt-4.1 99.7% and 93.7%). TW-2 tests whether the reading can be trained.
- **Practice with date questions taught the weights to use a trained date.**
  - With date questions answered for 150 offices at other dates, the models were right for 38–45% of past-date questions on the 150 offices that had none, against 0–0.7% for DATED. This is learning to learn, as in RP-3.
  - It cost after-change answers on those offices: 80.7–87.3% against 95.3–100% for DATED.
  - The effect needed enough practice: with 25 practised offices in dev, it did not appear.
  - On half B, PRACTICE answered both dates for 35.3% of OLMo's offices, a little more than the untouched OLMo with the dated line (30.7%). For Qwen the dated line was well ahead (66.0% against 29.3%).
- **Every fine-tuning arm cost untouched knowledge:** locality fell from 49.7% to 8–15% (OLMo) and from 85.0% to 11–19% (Qwen). The memory route changes no weights.
- **The recall-first probe** found little: the tuned arms mostly answered with trained sentences and no "Answer:" line.

## Limits
- **Open 7–8B models,** float16 on T4s, one adapter per arm, one recipe (6 epochs).
- **One change per office;** dates inside the record's tenures.
- **Practice used TQ-1's question wording** at other dates. Half B shows transfer across offices, not across wordings.
- **The frontier replicate is pending:** GPT-4.1 through hosted fine-tuning (the addendum of 23 October, 00:20), waiting for GPUs.
