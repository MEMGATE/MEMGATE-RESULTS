# Models taken from Azure's model catalog and Kaggle (Part B item 13), 28 October 2026

The founder asked that no model come from Hugging Face accounts (28 October, about 13:20 IST): Meta's model is taken
from Azure's model catalog and Google's from Kaggle Models (Google's own model site, whose licence the founder accepted
on the lab's Kaggle account). Microsoft's Phi-4 is downloaded inside the job from its public, ungated page, as Qwen and
OLMo are. The files are put on the GPU jobs' share and mounted into each job (`cl/share_model.py`, `cl/xfm.py`).
