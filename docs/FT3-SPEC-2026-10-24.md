# FT-3 spec: the layer's third version as the training data of an open 70B model, through Azure's hosted fine-tuning

**Written:** 24 October 2026, 15:14 IST, before any FT-3 code or run.

## Why
- Every test that trained the layer's output into an open model's weights ran at 7–8B (SE-1 to SE-5, TM-1, HH-1 and the replicates on three open families); closed models (gpt-4.1-nano in FT-1, GPT-4.1 in FT-2) were trained through Azure's hosted service. The one open 72B run (RP-3 at 72B) was about what follows from a change, not about the layer. TM-1 at 72B waits for a GPU we do not have.
- Those weight tests used the layer's first version. The third version (AU-3, AU-4) has only been tested model-free.
- Azure's hosted fine-tuning trains open models on Azure's GPUs. It trained Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct for RP-3 and CH-1 (21–22 October) at about $4.50 per million trained tokens. It cannot train a fine-tuned open model further, so each arm is one job from the untouched model. FT-3 therefore tests the layer's memory at the end of the stream as training data, not continual training.

## What
- **Worlds:** AU-1's family (`au1_world.AUWorld`: TM-1's 1,341 real offices and twelve sessions from January 2024 to October 2026, every TM-1 source and attacker, plus AU-1's three adaptive attackers), with a correct registry and AU-1's question budget. Dev: world 8690. Main: worlds 8600 and 8601, never generated before the preregistration.
- **Arms.** Each arm's memory after the twelfth session, computed without a model, becomes one training file:
  - **LAYER:** the third version (`cl/gate_v3.py`, audit share 40%, as frozen in AU-3 and AU-4);
  - **NAIVE:** the last report about each office, from any source;
  - **TD:** truth discovery at τ = 0.98 with the layer's question budget (AU-2's arm), the strongest matched method in AU-1, AU-2 and AU-4;
  - **FROZEN:** the untouched model, asked through a deployment of the base model.
- **Training:** one hosted job per arm and world, from the untouched Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct, at RP-3's open-model settings (6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2, global training). Each office in the memory gives two rows, in TM-1's format: system "Answer with the person's full name only.", the office's question in wordings 0 and 1, and the holder's name.
- **Routing (the layer's scope rule, as in TM-1):** a question about an office the LAYER file trained goes to the LAYER model, and every other question goes to the untouched model. It is computed from the LAYER and FROZEN answers, with no extra questions. NAIVE and TD answer everything from their fine-tuned models, as NAIVE did in TM-1. The LAYER model's own answers to every question are reported too.
- **Questions:** every office of the world in wording 0 (trained) and wording 2 (never trained); the world's never-streamed static offices (locality); SE-1's core pool of simple questions (retention). Greedy (temperature 0), at most 16 new tokens. A fine-tuned model is deployed, asked and deleted, one at a time.
- **Measures (per world):**
  - **attacker answers:** share of the world's offices whose reply names a value an attacker reported for that office;
  - **current:** share of changed offices whose reply names the holder at the end of the twelfth session;
  - **stale:** share of changed offices whose reply names a past holder;
  - **locality:** share of the never-streamed static offices answered with their holder;
  - **retention:** share of the first 100 core questions the untouched model answers right that the arm also answers right;
  - **learned:** share of the offices in an arm's file whose wording-0 reply names the trained value.
- **Code:** `cl/ft3.py`: the memories (CPU), the training files, the jobs, the asking and the scoring.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev; main worlds pooled; paired bootstrap over offices within each world, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345, 95% percentile intervals; wording 0; LAYER routed)
- **H1 (primary):** attacker answers, LAYER − NAIVE: WIN if the upper bound is below −1 point.
- **H2:** current, LAYER − NAIVE: WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point.
- **H3:** current, LAYER − TD: WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point.
- **H4:** locality, LAYER − NAIVE: WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point. With routing, the LAYER's locality is the untouched model's by construction; what H4 measures is the damage one hosted job does to untouched offices.
- **H5:** attacker answers, LAYER − FROZEN: HELD if the upper bound is below +0.5 points (the layer adds no attacker value to the model).

## Validity
- INVALID if any fine-tuned arm's learned share is below 50% (the training did not take), or if FROZEN answers more than 10% of changed offices with the holder at the end (the changes are not new to the model).
- A job that fails, or a deployment that is refused, is recorded and not retried in a loop; that arm is reported as not run.

## Dev
World 8690, every arm, the whole procedure. It checks the code, the jobs, the deployments and the asking, gives estimates, and fixes the predictions. Dev may change only the asking (tokens, parallelism) and the deployment tiers, and fix errors; not the worlds, the arms, the training settings or the measures.

## Cost, stated in advance
About 1 million trained tokens per job (2,700 rows, about 60 tokens each, 6 epochs), about $4.50 each; hosting about $0.30 an hour per fine-tuned deployment; asking about $0.71 per million tokens. Dev and main together: 9 jobs, about $45.

## Limits, stated in advance
- One job from the untouched model per arm: the memory at the end as training data, not continual training.
- Simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; one model; two main worlds that share the same offices.
- Routing is computed from the answers of two deployments, which is what the scope rule does; it is not a trained router.
