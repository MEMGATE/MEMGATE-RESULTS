# TW-2 spec: the skill of reading a dated memory line, trained into an open model's weights, with the facts left in memory

**Status:** spec, written 23 October 2026, about 00:20 IST, before any TW-2 question. Code: `cl/tw2.py`.

## Why
TW-1 (dev, three open 7B families) found that a change trained into the weights does not keep the past answerable, even with its date. The layer's dated memory line does keep it. But 7B models read the line imperfectly: both dates were right for 18–62% of offices, against 86–100% for frontier models in TQ-1.

The layer's design puts facts in memory, because they change, and durable skills in the weights. TW-2 tests that division on time: can the skill of reading a dated line be trained into the weights, so that a small model reads lines for offices it was never trained on?

## Arms
One open model per run, with the lab's LoRA loop on Kaggle's two T4 GPUs (TW-1's settings, 3 epochs).

| Arm | Training |
| --- | --- |
| FROZEN | none: the untouched model |
| READER | one adapter trained on RP-1's offices that qualify under TQ-1's date rules (336 offices, 672 rows). Each office gets its dated memory line (TQ-1's GATED+ wording) twice: once with a question about a seeded random date in the previous holder's tenure, and once with a date after the change. Each is answered with that date's holder. |

The training offices are RP-1's. TQ-1's offices, which are the test offices, come after them in the pool, so none is shared.

## Questions
- **Both arms** are asked TQ-1's offices (dev 50, main 300) about TQ-1's BEFORE and AFTER dates, under three memory conditions in TQ-1's words:
  - NONE;
  - GATED: the current value only;
  - GATED+: the dated line.
- **Both arms** are also asked RP-3's locality questions with no memory.
- **Scoring** uses TQ-1's scorer. BOTH counts an office as right when its two dates are both right under the same condition.
- Models whose card asks for its own prompt layout get it.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev)
READER − FROZEN, an office-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345.

| Test | Measure | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | BOTH right under GATED+ | WIN: the 95% interval wholly above +1 pp |
| H2 | locality right, no memory | NON-INFERIOR: lower bound above −5 pp |
| H3 | AFTER right under GATED (current value only) | NON-INFERIOR: lower bound above −2 pp |

**Descriptive:** BEFORE and AFTER under GATED+, separately; NONE, which checks the adapter did not learn the test offices; BEFORE under GATED.

## Halves and models
- **Dev:** TQ-1's 50 dev offices, on the three families TW-1 uses: OLMo-2-7B, Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B.
- **Main:** the 300 main offices, after the preregistration is frozen.

## Limits
- **One kind of line** (the layer's dated line for office holders), and one change per office.
- **One adapter per model,** trained once.
- **No GPT-4.1 run:** the frontier models already read the line (TQ-1).

## Data
- **Kaggle copies:** the payload goes up as a private Kaggle dataset, and the run is a private kernel.
- **Clean-up:** both are deleted after the answers are fetched.

## Dev 1 (23 October 2026, 01:26–01:40 IST, OLMo-2-7B, 50 dev offices; descriptive only)
`runs/tw2-dev-olmo7b-20261023`. No question went unanswered.
- **With the dated line:** READER was right on both dates for 100% of offices; the untouched model for 36%. Past dates went from 44% to 100% right, after-change dates from 92% to 100%.
- **No test fact was learned:** with no memory, READER answered as the untouched model did (past dates 10% against 8%; after the change 2% against 2%).
- **Locality:** 52% for both.
- **With the current value only (GATED):** READER was right for 92% of after-change questions, against 100%. It named someone else for 3 of 50 and the previous holder for 1.

**What changed:** each training office now also gets its current-value line (GATED), with a question about a third date after the change, answered with the current holder. The training set is 1,008 rows. A reader trained only on dated lines had learned to look for a previous holder in every line.

**Next:** dev 2 on the three families with this training set.

## Dev 1 on Qwen2.5-7B ran out of GPU memory (23 October 2026, 01:43–01:50 IST)
- **How it ran:** this run was pushed by the queue, with dev 1's training set, before the queue was stopped for the change above.
- **What failed:** the untouched model answered all 350 questions. READER's training then ran out of memory on one T4: Qwen's 152,000-token vocabulary makes the loss for a batch of 8 long rows too large.
- **What was kept:** the folder is kept as `runs/tw2-dev-qwen7b-20261023-oom`, and nothing was scored.
- **What changed:** TW-2 trains with batches of 4 (`BATCH` in `cl/tw2.py`) and lets PyTorch expand memory segments.

## Dev 2 (23 October 2026, 01:56–02:15 IST, OLMo-2-7B; descriptive only)
`runs/tw2-dev2-olmo7b-20261023`: dev 1's change (current-value rows, 1,008 rows), trained in batches of 4 for 3 epochs.
- **With the dated line:** both dates right for 100% of offices (untouched 36%).
- **With the current value only:** after-change questions 100% right, as for the untouched model. The gap dev 1 found is closed.
- **With no memory:** as the untouched model (past dates 6% against 8%).
- **Locality fell from 52% to 34%** (−18 pp [−32, −6]).
  - Batches of 4 for 3 epochs took 756 optimizer steps, three times dev 1's 252, which kept locality at 52%.

**What changed:** batches of 8, accumulated over two micro-batches of 4 so that Qwen fits in memory, and 2 epochs. That makes 252 optimizer steps, dev 1's number, on the larger training set.

**Next:** dev 3 on the three families with these settings.
