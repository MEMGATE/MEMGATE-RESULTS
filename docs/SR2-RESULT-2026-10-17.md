## Conclusion across SR-1 and SR-2

1. **No improvement.** In these formulations, the Nyāya, Pāṇini/Mīmāṃsā and Yoga-inspired rule-system policies do not improve online lesson learning. On fresh data they are 1.3–1.6 pp worse than ordinary policies.
2. **One consistent strength.** The utsarga–apavāda representation keeps the general lesson and carves out exceptions. It tolerates label noise: the NOISE family gained +0.8 to +1.8 pp in all four runs, and breast cancer gained +0.6 pp.
3. **One consistent weakness.** The same representation hurts on disjunctive concepts (DNF, tic-tac-toe) and under drift.
4. **Negligible pieces.** Saṃśaya, satpratipakṣa and niścita upādhi have negligible effects at this scale.
5. **Where this could still matter.** The strength points to noisy evidence, which is exactly the situation with model-written lessons and model-made mistakes. That is the next test.

## Limits

- **Formulations:** one formulation per mechanism.
- **Learners:** the rule learner and the baselines are simple.
- **Not covered:** model calls, Pramana's M-CORE, and the historical records.
