# RS-1: the rasa-sūtra (Nāṭyaśāstra VI.31) as a rule for forming lessons

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any RS-1 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Source and mapping

Nāṭyaśāstra VI.31 (prose), the rasa-sūtra:

> *vibhāvānubhāvavyabhicārisaṃyogād rasaniṣpattiḥ*
>
> "The Sentiment is produced from a combination (*saṃyoga*) of Determinants (*vibhāva*), Consequents (*anubhāva*) and Complementary Psychological States (*vyabhicāribhāva*)." (Ghosh's translation, on Wisdomlib)

**Commentary context.** Bharata compares this to taste arising from combining spices. Later commentators, from Lollaṭa to Abhinavagupta, read *saṃyoga* and *niṣpatti* differently.

**The register's earlier status** was "source not read; candidate mapping typed feature groups". O-2 found that documented feature groups used as group-level features give nothing. This test is different: it is a rule about how a lesson is formed.

**The mapping (mine).**
- The features of a case are of three documented kinds:
  - determinants: the background conditions and causes;
  - consequents: the expressions and signs the condition produces;
  - transitory states: passing measurements.
- **SAMYOGA:** a lesson (the "rasa", a firm conclusion) may be formed only as a conjunction drawing on at least two kinds.
- **How it is enforced:** when ordinary formation reaches a pure conjunction within one kind, SAMYOGA adds the literal from another kind that keeps the most covered cases (the earliest feature breaks a tie). This one literal may exceed the usual three. If the case has no value of another kind, the formation fails.
- **Revisions** only add literals, so a lesson keeps its kinds.

## 2. Arms

| Arm | Content |
| --- | --- |
| R-ORD | baseline |
| **SAMYOGA** | the documented kinds |
| RANDOM-KINDS | the same rule over a random assignment of the features to kinds of the same sizes (`random.Random(seed + 888)`). It tests whether the documented kinds matter. |

## 3. Data

Two UCI medical datasets, new to the lab, each with documented variables. Each runs in 20 orders (seeds 36000–36019), cut to 1,000 cases. Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule.

**Myocardial infarction complications** (UCI 579). 1,700 patients. The label is the lethal outcome (alive vs died of any cause, from `LET_IS`). Only what is known at admission is kept: treatments and the day-2 and day-3 variables are dropped.

| Kind | Variables (UCI variable order) |
| --- | --- |
| Determinants | age, sex and the anamnesis: AGE through zab_leg_06 |
| Consequents | the signs at admission: blood pressures, pulmonary oedema, shock, arrhythmia paroxysms, the ECG localisation, rhythm and conduction findings (S_AD_KBRIG through n_p_ecg_p_12), TIME_B_S, and pain relapse in the first hours (R_AB_1_n) |
| Transitory states | the serum and blood values (GIPO_K through ROE) |

Dropped: `ID`; fibrinolytic therapy and every drug variable (fibr_ter_*, NA_KB, NOT_NA_KB, LID_KB, NITR_S, NA_R_*, NOT_NA_*, LID_S_n, B_BLOK_S_n, ANT_CA_S_n, GEPAR_S_n, ASP_S_n, TIKL_S_n, TRENT_S_n); R_AB_2_n and R_AB_3_n; and the other eleven targets.

**HCV for Egyptian patients** (UCI 503). 1,385 patients. The label is the baseline histological staging.

| Kind | Variables |
| --- | --- |
| Determinants | age, gender, BMI |
| Consequents | the symptoms: fever, nausea/vomiting, headache, diarrhoea, fatigue and bone ache, jaundice, epigastric pain |
| Transitory states | the blood counts, ALT over time and RNA over time |

Dropped: the baseline histological grading, which comes from the same biopsy as the label.

**Dev.** O-2's exposed datasets with documented groups (wdbc, thyroid, spambase, flags, derm, diabetes), with O-2's groups as the kinds, in orders 2000–2009.
