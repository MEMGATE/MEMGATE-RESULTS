## What this means

**The resume signal was fooled.**
- While paused, the network asks about every case, and every spy, broken or not, learns from those labels.
- Spies reading noise learn to say the commonest label. So the three of them stop splitting three ways, even though two are still broken.
- The rule "resume when disagreement returns to its baseline" then resumed in 12 of 14 alarms. Wrong answers alone went back up to about the supervisor's own level (1.78, against 1.88).

**The two alarms, side by side.**
- **Permanent (MX-11, and here):** safe with two broken sources (1.12 per 100 here, 0.84 in MX-11), but every false alarm costs the rest of the run.
- **Self-resuming:** costs almost nothing when sound (−0.09), but gives the safety back.

**The lesson for a working system.** The signal to resume must be one a broken source cannot fake. Two forms would meet that:
- **the owner** confirms that the cameras or sensors are working (Arthaśāstra's verification through other agents);
- each source's **own accuracy on the asked cases** returns to its baseline.

Agreement among the sources is not enough. Neither form is tested here. The resume rule was the one change, so this is a clean negative.
