# ML-4: a pariṣad that mixes language models with classical learners

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any ML-4 code and before the ML-3 dev result was read.

## 1. Question

Two results are now established:

- **Mixed assemblies win offline.** A plurality of learners of different kinds (lesson learner, naive Bayes, ID3) beat the lesson learner on two independent held-out pools: P-1 +1.44 pp, and P-2 +1.56 and +1.60 pp.
- **Model-written lessons win against model experience.** ML-1 gave +3.84 pp and ML-2 gave +2.58 pp.

**Question.** Is a language model with its own lessons a *different competence* that improves the offline assembly? And does the assembly improve on the model alone? Manusmṛti 12.111–112 asks for members of different learning. A model reasoning in words and a probability or tree learner counting cases are about as different as members can be.

## 2. Design

**Members.** Each sees the same 100-case stream: it predicts first, then observes.

| Member | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| ORD-astra | gpt-6-astra with its own lessons, exactly ML-2's ORD arm (plain neighbours) |
| ORD-sol | gpt-6.1-sol, likewise |
| R-ORD | the offline lesson learner |
| EXT-NB | naive Bayes |
| EXT-DT | ID3 |

Grok is not used. In ML-3 a single grok proposal took about two minutes.

**Assemblies.** All decide by plurality, with ties going to the earliest member listed.

| Assembly | Members |
| --- | --- |
| PARISHAD-OFF | R-ORD, EXT-NB, EXT-DT (P-1's winner) |
| PARISHAD-MIX | ORD-sol, EXT-NB, EXT-DT (the model replaces the offline lesson learner) |
| PARISHAD-MODELS | ORD-astra, ORD-sol, R-ORD |
| PARISHAD-5 | ORD-astra, ORD-sol, R-ORD, EXT-NB, EXT-DT |

**Worlds.** EXC, NOISE and the 100-case DRIFT, as in ML-2, with new seeds 21000–21005, which no experiment has used. That gives 18 worlds. Dev uses seed 0, one world per family (exposed).

## 3. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as in ML-1 to ML-3. The strata are the families. The differences are per world.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | PARISHAD-MIX − PARISHAD-OFF: does a model member add over the offline lesson learner? |
| H2 | PARISHAD-MIX − ORD-sol: does the assembly beat the model alone? |
| H3 | PARISHAD-5 − PARISHAD-OFF |
| H4 | ORD-sol − R-ORD: the model with lessons against the offline lesson learner |
| Descriptive | PARISHAD-MODELS − ORD-sol; each member's accuracy |

**INVALID if** either model fails or refuses on more than 2% of its calls, or more than 2% of model answers are unparseable.

## 4. Budget

About 2 × 18 × 250 ≈ 9,000 model calls, with a hard cap of 25,000. astra and sol answer in 2–3 s.
