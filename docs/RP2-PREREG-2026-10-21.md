## 1. Design (unchanged from the spec)
**Models:** no training, and no deployment created or deleted.
- **NAIVE-12:** FT-2 main world 8202's NAIVE model after session 12 (`rt1-8202-naive-12`).
- **ENGINE-12:** the same world's ENGINE model after session 12 (`rt1-8202-engine-12`).
- **FROZEN:** the untouched `gpt-4.1-base` (memgate-ft-ncus).

The two fine-tuned deployments were kept by RT-1 on memgate-ft-eus2b. The developer tier deletes them about 24 hours after creation, so the main half must finish before about 10:30 on 22 October. If either deployment no longer serves its model, the runner stops.

**Offices:** RP-1's main offices (`rp1.chosen_offices(TMWorld(8000), "main")`) that world 8202 did not attack and whose final holder in world 8202 is RP-1's current holder. That leaves **150 offices**, none asked in dev.

**Questions:**
- RP-1's four kinds with no memory, using RP-1's instructions.
- The system message carries FT-2's session-12 date: "Today is 2026-10-18. …".
- DIRECT is asked exactly as the models were trained. REVERSE, PREVIOUS and STILL are phrasings no job trained on.

**Calls and scoring:**
- Temperature 0, at most 150 tokens; 3 arms × 4 kinds × 150 offices = 1,800 questions.
- Scoring is RP-1's frozen `correct()`.

## 2. Dev results (24 offices)

| Arm | DIRECT | REVERSE | PREVIOUS | STILL |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FROZEN | 0.0 | 8.3 | 41.7 | 75.0 |
| NAIVE-12 | 70.8 | 12.5 | 45.8 | 83.3 |
| ENGINE-12 | 87.5 | 20.8 | 25.0 | 54.2 |

**Where the fine-tuned models fail on what follows from the fact:**
- **PREVIOUS:** they invent a plausible name ("Steve Cotterson", "Robbie Fowler", "Adam Deadmarsh").
- **STILL:** they say the previous holder still holds the post ("Yes. Gareth Ainsworth is still the head coach of Shrewsbury Town F.C."), although the same model names his successor when asked who the coach is.
- **REVERSE:** they name a wrong post ("the manager of Derby County").

**Carry deficits, dev** (pp; the interval is the office bootstrap):
- H1 REVERSE: +70.8 [52.1, 89.6];
- H2 PREVIOUS: +85.4 [56.3, 114.6];
- H3 STILL: +85.4 [64.6, 106.3].

All three passed the dev check (WIN).

## 3. Hypotheses and decision rules
**The statistic:**
- For a fine-tuned arm A and kind X: gain_X = A − FROZEN (0/1 per office).
- The carry deficit on X is gain_DIRECT − gain_X.
- Per office it is averaged over NAIVE-12 and ENGINE-12. The estimate is the mean over offices, in percentage points.
- The 95% interval is a bootstrap over offices (10,000 resamples, seed 12345).

| Test | Kind | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | REVERSE | **WIN** if the interval lies wholly above +1 pp and both arms' own estimates are above 0; **NO WIN** if its upper bound is below +1 pp; otherwise UNDETERMINED |
| H2 | PREVIOUS | same |
| H3 | STILL | same |

**Validity:** the run is **INVALID** if any arm leaves more than 5% of its 600 questions unanswered or refused (an empty answer). A refusal that is kept scores 0.

**Descriptive, no verdict:**
- each arm's accuracy per kind;
- the carry rate, P(X right | DIRECT right);
- the same four kinds for `gpt-4.1-base` given RP-1's verified memory (GATED) on these 150 offices, from RP-1's main run. This compares the two routes; their dates and runs differ, so no test is made.

## 4. Predictions
- H1, H2 and H3: **WIN**, with deficits of roughly +50 to +90 pp.
- The fine-tuned models are predicted to be right on DIRECT for most offices (60–90%) and far lower on REVERSE (under 30%).
- On PREVIOUS and STILL they are predicted to be no better than FROZEN, and possibly worse.
- Under RP-1's verified memory, gpt-4.1-base is predicted to be right on DIRECT, REVERSE and STILL for most offices.

## 5. Limits, stated now
- **One FT-2 world.** So one pair of fine-tuned models: GPT-4.1, 6 epochs a block, four blocks.
- **Facts only.** The knowledge involved is simple.
- **The ENGINE arm's scope rule** would, in deployment, route REVERSE (which names a person, not an office) to the untouched model. RP-2 asks the fine-tuned models directly to measure what is in the weights.
- **STILL needs an English "no" first** (RP-1's scorer). In dev one answer was "Hayır." (Turkish for no), scored wrong. Answers not in English are counted and reported.
