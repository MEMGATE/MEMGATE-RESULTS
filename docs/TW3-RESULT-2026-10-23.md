## What it shows
- **The reading skill is not tied to the kind of fact it was trained on.** Trained only on football coaches, the adapter read heads of government, chairs, CEOs and heads of state: both dates right for 98.1–100% in the layer's wording.
- **It is not tied to the wording either.** In a note format no training row used, both dates were right for 89.8–100%.
  - Untouched, Nemotron-Nano read the note's past (100% of past dates) but named the previous holder for 80.3% of after-change dates: both dates right for 3.8%. With READER, 89.8%.
- **The facts stayed in memory.** With no memory, READER answered as the untouched models did (past dates 0.0–5.7% against 0.6–7.0% on held-out kinds).
- **A small cost in untouched knowledge** on two of the three models: −3.0 and −4.33 points. This reader was trained for 4 epochs on 151 offices. TW-2's reader, trained for 2 epochs on 336 offices, cost nothing (+0.33 to +4.33). How long to train, and on how many offices, matters for the cost.

## Limits
- **Still office holders:** the held-out kinds are new roles, not a new domain.
- **One new wording:** a short note.
- **Open 7–8B models,** one adapter per model.
