**What happened.**
- Both arms answered alone on almost no case, before or after the failure: coverage 0.0%, and 0.1% after the failure for the supervisor in ONE.
- The supervisor dismissed a failed source in 6 of 10 runs in ONE and 0 of 10 in TWO, but there was no credence to restore.

## What this means

**This is MX-6's precondition failing again, not evidence about the recipe.**
- Each source here has 6 coarse features (the quartiles of the x, y and z means and standard deviations over 10 seconds) for 18 activities. Many of them, such as eating soup, chips, pasta or a sandwich, differ little in wrist or pocket motion.
- No spy could hold a well-supported lesson, so all three were always guessing, and the network asked about every case.
- It was safe, giving no wrong answers alone, but it did no work.

**A lesson for the design of such tests.**
- Two of the six multi-source settings tested so far could not test the recipe at all: the naval simulator (MX-6) and WISDM (MX-9).
- From now on, a multi-source preregistration should state a **testability condition**: the sound network must answer alone on at least 5% of cases before any failure. Otherwise the setting is reported as NOT TESTABLE rather than NO WIN.
- Here it is reported as NO WIN, as preregistered, with this explanation.

**The safety question is still open.** Does the supervisor stay safe when two of three sources fail? It needs a setting where the network works when sound. It is queued as MX-10, on fresh synthetic VIEWS worlds, where the sound network answers alone about a third of the time.
