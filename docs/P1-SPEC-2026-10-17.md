# P-1: the Dharmaśāstra *pariṣad*, an assembly of different learners, as the mix of everything tested so far

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any P-1 code and before the SR-6 main run. No model calls are made.

## 1. Source and mapping

**Source.** Manusmṛti 12.111–112 describes the assembly (*pariṣad*) that settles a doubtful point of dharma. It was checked against Bühler's translation and Jha's translation of Medhātithi's commentary, in the online editions on siva.sh and wisdomlib.

- The assembly of at least ten (12.111) brings together members of **different competences**:
  - one versed in the three Vedas (*traividya*);
  - a *haituka*;
  - a *tarkī*;
  - an etymologist (*nairukta*);
  - a reciter of the law (*dharmapāṭhaka*);
  - members of the first three āśramas.
- The commentators differ on what the two reasoning roles mean. The Mitākṣarā takes the haituka as a knower of Mīmāṃsā principles. The Madanapārijāta takes the tarkī as one who reaches the answer by ruling out every alternative.
- The assembly of at least three (12.112) is a Ṛgveda knower, a Yajurveda knower and a Sāmaveda knower: again, three *different* competences.
- Two neighbouring verses are cited from memory and were not checked here: 12.110 (an assembly's decision is not to be disputed) and 12.113 (one learned person outweighs myriads of the ignorant).

**Mapping (mine).** The structural claim is that **a decision by members of different competences** is better than one member, and better than many members who all know the same thing.

- **Members:** the members are learners already tested in this lab, chosen to be of different kinds.
- **Decision:** a plurality vote. When labels tie, the member listed first in the fixed order below decides.
- **Roles:** the assignment of individual learners to Manu's named roles is illustrative, not a claim of fidelity.

## 2. Assemblies

All members see every case: each predicts first, then observes.

**PARISHAD-10**, daśāvarā: ten members of different kinds, in this order:

1. R-ORD, the lesson learner (*dharmapāṭhaka*)
2. EXT-NB
3. EXT-DT
4. EXT-1NN (2–4 are three distinct "Vedas" of learning)
5. R-SHASTRA2, the Nyāya–Mīmāṃsā rule policy (*haituka*)
6. R-ORD+ART, arthāpatti or elimination (*tarkī*)
7. R-ORD+SYA, Jaina standpoints
8. R-ORD-KALA, Kālacakra recency
9. R-ORD+ANU, anupalabdhi
10. R-ORD+AKHYATI, Prābhākara non-discrimination

**PARISHAD-3**, tryavarā: R-ORD, EXT-NB and EXT-DT. These are three different learning families.

**PARISHAD-SAME**, the contrast "many who know the same": ten near-identical lesson learners:

1. R-ORD
2. R-ORD+ANU
3. R-ORD+ART
4. R-ORD+LAYA-SR
5. R-ORD+NIY
6. R-ORD+AKHYATI
7. R-ORD+PRATYAHARA
8. R-ORD+PRALAYA
9. R-ORD+LAYA
10. R-ORD+LAYA-RAND

**MAJORITY-5:** J-1's five experts (EXT-DT, EXT-NB, EXT-1NN, R-ORD, EXT-LAST) with J-1's tie rule. This is the preregistered replication of J-1's descriptive finding.

**Reference arms:** R-ORD, EXT-NB, and BEST-SINGLE (the best PARISHAD-10 member in hindsight; descriptive).
