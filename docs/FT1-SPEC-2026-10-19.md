# FT-1: the engine through a provider's own fine-tuning service. gpt-4.1-nano on Azure Foundry, SE-5's world

**Status:** design, written on 19 October 2026 after two feasibility checks and before any FT-1 run.

## 1. Question

Every weight experiment so far trained LoRA adapters on an open model (Qwen2.5) on our own GPUs. A lab or a company that fine-tunes a hosted model sees only an API. The model is the provider's, and the training is the provider's job.

FT-1 asks whether the engine's results hold there:
- learn the world's changes;
- admit no lie;
- leave untouched knowledge as it was.

It uses OpenAI's `gpt-4.1-nano`, fine-tuned through Azure Foundry's supervised fine-tuning, with no weights and no GPU of our own.

## 2. Feasibility (done)

The checks taught 12 invented facts: fictional companies and their CEOs, so no real-world falsehood was involved.

| Run | Settings | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check 1 | 3 epochs, default learning rate | 0 of 12 learned |
| Check 2 | 10 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2 | 12 of 12 on the trained phrasing, 12 of 12 on a new one |

A job takes about an hour from submission to a usable model: queueing, training, and the service's checks. A developer-tier deployment has no hosting fee and is removed after 24 hours.

## 3. Design

**World.** SE-5's world:
- real offices, the twelve sessions from January 2024 to October 2026;
- honest but late sources, a stealthy liar, two colluders, two sleepers, and a swarm on each late fact.

**Probe.** Untouched gpt-4.1-nano on SE-5's dev world named the current holder of 0% of changed offices and a past holder of 79%. It answered 78% of the locality offices correctly.

**Training.** Four blocks of three sessions each. Each block is one job, continuing from the previous block's model. Each item is taught in two phrasings, as in SE-3 to SE-5.

| Arm | Trains on | Answers with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FROZEN | — | the untouched model |
| NAIVE | every report of the block | its latest fine-tuned model, for every question |
| ENGINE | what the memgate gate admits (SE-5's rules, budget 40) | the scope rule: its fine-tuned model for facts it trained, the untouched model for every other question |

ENGINE's rollback and replay: when a source is caught, it returns to the model from before the first block that held a tainted item, and retrains the blocks since without those items.

**Measures,** after each block (sessions 3, 6, 9, 12):
- current holders of clean changed offices;
- poison;
- swarm-lie and sleeper-lie answers;
- locality.

There is no likelihood trace: the service does not expose the probability of a given answer. There is no self-check either.

**Settings.** Chosen from the checks before any FT-1 run:
- 6 epochs per job with a learning-rate multiplier of 2;
- each fact appears in several reports per block, so 6 epochs give it more exposures than check 2's 20 in total.

## 4. Hypotheses for the main run (to be frozen after the dev run)

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1** | Poison: ENGINE − NAIVE | WIN if ≤ −1 pp with the CI's upper bound below 0 |
| **H2** | Locality: ENGINE − NAIVE | WIN if ≥ +1 pp with the CI's lower bound above 0 |
| **H3** | Current: ENGINE − NAIVE | NON-INFERIOR at −5 pp |

**INVALID if:**
- a job fails without a successful retry;
- FROZEN current is above 10%;
- NAIVE current is below 30%.

## 5. Phases and budget

| Phase | Worlds | Expected wall time | Expected cost (Azure) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dev | SE-5 dev world 0 | about 5 hours, with the two chains in parallel | about $10–15 |
| Main | 3 fresh SE-5 worlds (9100–9102) | about a day | about $30–40 |

The cost is training tokens at the global rate, plus inference at the base model's rate.
