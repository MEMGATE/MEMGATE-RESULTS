# RV-1 result: INVALID. A Wikidata edit is not a claim about who holds an office now, so the stream did not measure what it was meant to

19 October 2026. Analysis `cl/rv1.py`; output in `runs/rv1-20261019.json`; preregistration [RV1-PREREG-2026-10-19.md](RV1-PREREG-2026-10-19.md), frozen at `0f97e6fe` before the analysis was written.

## What came out

- **The stream:** 9,337 edits that set a TM-1 office, by 590 editors.
- **The real mistakes:** of the 67 reverted edits classified as wrong at the time (24 premature, 24 past holders, 19 never holders), NAIVE learned all 67 and GATE learned 0.
- **Prediction 3 failed.** GATE dismissed 82 editors, 69 of whom made no reverted edit at all.
- **Wrong values at the end.** GATE held 264 of 1,333 admitted values that differed from the holder at the end. In TM-1's simulated streams the figure was 37–55.

## Why the run is invalid

The design read every edit as "the editor says this person holds the office". Wikidata edits are not that. Against the session-date timeline, the edits that the community kept, never reverted, break down as follows:

| Kept edits | Count | What they are |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| name the holder of the moment | 3,232 | claims about the current holder |
| name a past holder | 4,359 | mostly history being filled in: past holders added with their end dates, and statements of outgoing holders updated |
| name a later holder | 1,443 | timely updates within a quarter-long session, after the session date |
| name a never-holder | 204 | other relations' values, and ids outside the snapshot |

**The cause.** The automatic edit summary gives the property and value of the statement edited. It does not say whether that statement is the current one; that needs its rank and its start and end qualifiers.

**What this did to the run.** Honest editors who fill in history, or update quickly, looked like sources who contradict the registry, and the gate dismissed them. With them gone, it held stale values. The same misreading affects the 67 "wrong" edits: a reverted edit to a past holder's statement is not a false claim about the present.

**Verdict.** INVALID, by the lab's rule: a critical instrumentation failure prevents interpretation. GATE's 0 of 67 is not counted as evidence.

## What a valid version needs

- **Read the statement after each edit.** For every edit, fetch the claims before and after it, and keep it only if it changes which statement is current: preferred rank, or no end time. About 9,600 revision reads.
- **Hold the registry to the edit's moment.** Compare each claim with the holder at the edit's own time, not at the session date, or use finer sessions.

This is planned as RV-2. The fetched history (`data/wikidata-reverts`) stays as the starting point.
