## What this means

**Counting agreement is not evidence when identities are free.**
- A swarm of fresh accounts satisfies any "two sources agree" rule, and catching it is useless because the next swarm brings new names.
- The ledger gate trained 62.5 swarm-lie reports per world.
- Honest reports overwrote each lie before the end, so almost none was answered. But the lies left a trace in the weights (74.5%, +2.08 nats per token), as SE-4 showed overwritten lies do.

**Āpta-vacana closes the door** (Nyāya Sūtra 1.1.7: testimony is a means of knowledge when it comes from a reliable speaker).
- **The rule:** a source counts as a supporter only after the registry has confirmed several of its claims and never caught it fabricating.
- **The result:** no swarm lie reached the weights in any world. The trace stayed at the untrained model's level (46.0% against 47.0%).
- **The cost:** none. Current holders, poison resistance, locality and retention were unchanged.

**The attack it leaves:** an attacker who first earns trust with true reports. That is SE-4's sleeper, which the ledger catches at its first exposed fabrication. Together, āpta and the ledger make each lie that gets in cost several confirmed truths, and expose the liar the first time it is checked.

## Limits, stated plainly

- **The swarms are scripted.** Each is one burst of four fresh accounts per late fact.
- **The threshold (three confirmed claims) was not varied.** Real deployments would tune it against how quickly honest new sources should be heard.
- **The facts are real, the sources simulated,** as in SE-3 and SE-4.
