## What it shows
- **The findings of parts 1 and 2 hold at 72B with the lab's own loop.** Trained on the direct fact alone, the 72B model named the new holder for every office in half A, yet said the person holds the post now for 16.7% (STILL pair). Trained also on what follows, it did so for 99.3%.
  - MORE, with as many rows, did not (6.7%). The gain comes from the statements, not the volume.
- **Transfer was larger than at 7–8B.** On half B, trained only on the direct fact, the follow-on score rose 8.89 points over MORE. With the lab's loop at 7B it rose 5.11 (Qwen2.5-7B) and 4.89 (OLMo-2-7B); GPT-4.1, trained by Azure's hosted service, rose 20.22.
- **Every adapter cost untouched knowledge, at 72B too.** Asked directly, with no routing, the untouched model answered 88.0% of 300 offices no arm was trained on. With one adapter switched on, 21.3–27.7%.
  - The layer avoids this by routing a question about a fact the adapter was not trained on to the untouched model, the adapter switched off. Scale did not remove the damage.

## Limits
- **One 72B model** (Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, revision `495f3936…`), one adapter per arm at one setting, in bfloat16 over three GPUs.
- **The follow-on statements are written by the lab** from the verified change (four kinds).
- **Locality is asked without routing.** It measures the adapter, not the layer.
- **This is not the layer's stream test at 72B.** That test (TM-1 at 72B) failed on its first run while loading the model and waits for the Modal workspace's billing (docs/TM1-72B-PREREG-2026-10-22.md).
