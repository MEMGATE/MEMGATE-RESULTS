## What this means

**Every effect that won before held again**, with unchanged code, on nine datasets none of them had seen.

The four about knowing and asking are the most robust:
- R1, R2, R5 and R6 won again by margins close to their originals.
- They were positive on all nine datasets but one: delta_ailerons for R6 was still +3.8.

**The pariṣad's gain was smaller here.**
- It was +0.81 pp, positive and significant, but below the 1-pp margin.
- On halloffame (3 classes, few positives) it lost −1.76.
- Its earlier wins were +1.2 to +1.6. Across five pools, it is a dependable but modest gain.

**Range literals stayed at about +1 pp.** Their gain is real, and largest on smooth numeric data (kin8nm +2.7, ailerons +2.1).

**A caution on R1.** Jijñāsā beats random asking at the same number of labels; it does not always match seeing every label.
- On nomao it asked for only 2.4% of the labels and was 9.6 pp below the learner that saw them all.
- Confident lessons that are wrong are never corrected, as JK-1 found on clean data.
