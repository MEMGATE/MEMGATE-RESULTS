# MX-9: when two of three sources fail, does the supervisor fail safe?

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-9 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

The supervisor won on four real settings when one of three sources failed (MX-5, MX-7, MX-8; AR-1). It can dismiss at most one source.

A practical system must also be safe when two sources fail at once, for example two cameras lost in one power cut. Arthaśāstra 1.12 expects the three reports to be "exactly of the same version" before they are believed. With two broken spies, that should almost never happen, so the network should ask rather than answer.

MX-9 measures this. It also replicates the one-failure result on a fifth fresh setting.

## 2. Arms and conditions

Both arms use `shastra/mx5.py`'s `run_supervisor`, unchanged:
- **SPIES:** no pieces;
- **SUPERVISOR:** DISMISS, AUDIT, RESET and SAMSKARA.

| Condition | Failure from case 600 |
| --- | --- |
| ONE | one source, drawn by MX-5's `fail_from` |
| TWO | two distinct sources, drawn from `random.Random(f"MX9-FAIL2:{seed}")`; each value replaced as in `fail_from` |

## 3. Data: WISDM smartphone and smartwatch activity (UCI 507), new to the lab

51 people performed 18 activities for about 3 minutes each. Each carried a phone in the pocket and wore a watch; both recorded accelerometer and gyroscope at 20 Hz.

**Reading (fixed before the data are opened beyond their documentation).**
- **Files.** Only the `raw` files are read; the ARFF feature files are not.
- **Three sources:** the phone accelerometer, the watch accelerometer and the watch gyroscope. The phone gyroscope is not used.
- **Cases.** In each stream, the samples of one person and one activity are taken in timestamp order and cut into consecutive 200-sample (10-second) windows. A shorter remainder is dropped.
  - Case k of a person's activity joins window k of each of the three streams. The cases exist where all three have a window k.
  - The streams have separate clocks, so this aligns windows within an activity, not to the second.
- **Features.** Per source, the mean and standard deviation of x, y and z, which gives 6 features per source and 18 in all.
- **Label.** The activity.
- **Storage.** The 310 MB archive is not stored. A derived table is stored with its builder.

**Orders.**
- 10 shuffled orders (seeds 51000–51009), cut to 1,000 cases.
- Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule.

**Dev.** There is no dev run, since the code is frozen. The table is checked by shape only.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary, replication)** | SUPERVISOR − SPIES, net credence, ONE |
| H2 | SUPERVISOR − SPIES, net credence, TWO |
| **Safety (descriptive, preregistered)** | Wrong answers given alone after the failure, per 100 cases after case 600, for each arm and condition. A safe system keeps this under 2 in TWO. |

**INVALID if:**
- any run is non-deterministic or crashes;
- the supervisor never dismisses in ONE.

## 5. Predictions

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | positive, WIN | as on four settings |
| H2 | ≈ 0 | With two broken spies, agreement is rare either way. Dismissal can remove only one of them. |
| Safety | wrong answers alone after the failure stay low (< 2 per 100) in TWO for both arms | The guessing clause and the agreement rule ask instead. |
