# CCL-4: all twelve abilities in one system (draft spec, 25 October 2026)

Status: **draft**. The system below is being explored in small tests (`docs/CLX-PLAN-2026-10-25.md`); this spec is finalized, and the preregistration frozen, after CLX-5 and the 72B dev.

## Why a new test
CCL-2 (preregistered, Qwen2.5-7B) showed abilities 2, 5, 6 and 7 in one learner. It failed ability 3 (never-streamed knowledge fell from 100% to 31%) and did not show 1, 4 and 9 (current holders −3.2 points against naive, no room for a difference on stale values, GSM8K −3.0). The small tests of 25 October found the parts that close those gaps:
- **The scope rule learned by the model** (CLX-3b, 1.5B): a gate fitted on the untouched weights' last-token state sends questions about what was learned to the adapter and every other question to the untouched model. General ability stayed exactly at the untouched model's level (every one of 350 general questions routed to it), and the loss on 303 never-streamed offices fell from 25 to 6 points.
- **Practice until firm** (CLX-5): after each session the learner is asked everything it holds and trains again on what it missed, which is aimed at ability 1 and gives ability 8 a measure.
- **Denial of corrected errors** (CLX-5): ability 4 is measured where naive training has room to fail.

## The system (one learner, all abilities at once)
CCL-2's learner unchanged (a gate that admits only verified values; facts with their dates and earlier holders; what follows for half A; negation of corrected errors; every learned item rehearsed every session after a self-test; the untouched model's own answers to 64 general prompts; rule rows), plus:
1. **Practice until firm**: after each session's training, every office it holds (wordings 0 and 1) and every skill it rehearses is asked; what it misses is trained again (two rows each, with eight general-prompt rows), up to three rounds.
2. **The scope gate**: a logistic regression on the untouched weights' last-token final hidden state, refitted at each evaluation on the prompts of everything the learner has trained on (positive) and on office questions about offices outside the world, known and unknown to the model, and the general prompts (negative). A question goes to the adapter only if the gate gives it more than 0.5.
3. **Routing by asking** (for models fine-tuned as a service, which give no hidden states): the model trains on whether it was taught a question and is asked it at each evaluation.

## Measures and hypotheses (to be fixed in the preregistration)
CCL-2's H1–H11 on the system's routed answers, with the same margins, and two new ones:
- **H12 (ability 4)**: for each registry error, "is the wrong holder the holder now?" in a wording never trained; the share answered no, SYSTEM − NAIVE; WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point.
- **H13 (ability 8)**: the share of newly learned items missed at the first check after their session's training, items learned in sessions 1–4 minus items learned in sessions 9–12; WIN if the lower bound is above 0.

## Worlds, models and machines
- Worlds: CCL-2's whole worlds with all never-streamed offices the pools allow and 8 registry errors each; dev world 9790; main worlds 9702 and 9703 (prepared for CCL-3, never run to a result).
- Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct on Kaggle's TPU v5e-8 (`cl/clx_tpu.py`, 8-bit weights); Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct on Kaggle's GPUs (`cl/clx.py`) when the weekly quota allows; GPT-4.1 on Azure's hosted fine-tuning with routing by asking.
- Arms: FROZEN, NAIVE, SYSTEM.
