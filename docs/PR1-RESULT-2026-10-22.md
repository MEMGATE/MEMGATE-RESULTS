## What it shows
- **The memory carries a changed procedure, not only a changed fact.** llama-4-maverick did not know the new rules: 0% right with the question alone. Given the official FY 2025-26 rules in memory, it applied them to every income: 100% right.
  - gpt-4.1-base rose from 0% to 24.5%. Most of its other answers matched none of the three versions; 2.0% matched the forged one.
- **A forged update in a memory that joins whatever it receives took over the answers, even where the model knew better.** gpt-6.1-sol and gpt-6-astra applied the right rules to 98.5–99.5% of incomes on their own. With the forged circular after the official rules, both followed the forgery for every income, and grok-4.7 for 83%.
  - claude-opus-5-5 kept to the official rules (99.5%). It is reported but not tested, as frozen after dev.
- **The procedure-level version of SK-1 and RP-1.** The layer admits a changed procedure only from its verified source, so its memory held the official rules alone (VERIFIED). A memory that joins whatever it receives cannot tell a forged update from a real one.

## Limits
- **One procedure:** slab rates and a rebate, with arithmetic. No marginal relief, cess or surcharge.
- **The forged circular is the lab's own fixture,** placed after the official rules. Keeping such a source out is the gate's job, tested elsewhere (SK-1, RF-1, AU-2).
- **Four of the six models already knew the new rules.** Teaching the procedure (D1) is shown on two models only.
