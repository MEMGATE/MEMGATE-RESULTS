## 2. Mechanisms

Every mechanism is added to the ordinary rule learner R-ORD and changes nothing else.

**LAYA: ordered absorption (laya-krama).**

- **When it runs:** every 50 cases.
- **Order:** validated lessons are taken level by level, from the most specific (3 conditions) to 2 conditions. Within a level, they are taken by lesson id.
- **Generalising a lesson:** try removing each condition in sorted order. The first removal is accepted if the remaining conditions cover at least `SUPPORT_MIN` stored cases, all with the lesson's label. It produces the general lesson, either new or an identical existing active one.
- **Absorption:** every active validated lesson with the same conclusion whose conditions include the general lesson's conditions is *absorbed*. It leaves the active set, and the general lesson keeps it.
- **Ascent:** a general lesson created at a lower level is examined when the pass reaches that level, so content can rise more than one level in a pass.
- **When an absorbing lesson fails:** without SṚṢṬI, it is revised by ordinary FOIL, and what it absorbed stays absorbed.

**SṚṢṬI: return (sṛṣṭi-krama).** When a lesson that holds absorbed lessons meets a counterexample:

- it is superseded;
- its absorbed lessons are restored as validated lessons, with their support recomputed from memory;
- a restored lesson that also covers the counterexample is then revised by the ordinary FOIL step.

**LAYA-RAND (control for the order).** The same as LAYA with SṚṢṬI, except that the lessons of a pass are taken in a seeded random order with the levels mixed. Lessons created during the pass are appended at random positions. This tests whether the *krama* matters.

**PRALAYA: occasional dissolution.**

- **When it runs:** every 200 cases (a kalpa).
- **Realm classes:** validated lessons are ranked by support, with ties broken by lesson id, and cut into thirds.

| Third | Realm class | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lowest | Transitory | The lessons are dissolved. |
| Middle | Vacated, not destroyed | The lessons return to candidate status with empty support, and must earn validation again. |
| Top | Enduring | The lessons are unchanged. |

- **What survives:** stored cases are never deleted. They are the seed from which ordinary formation re-creates lessons as new cases arrive.

**NIY: niyati.** A validated lesson receives a case's outcome (support or counterexample) only if it took part in the decision for that case. Candidates and doubt groups are updated as before. Ordinarily, every covering lesson receives the outcome.

| Stream | Source | Cases | Features | Notes |

## 5. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are the same as SR-1 to SR-4. The real-data analyses stratify by source: 12 strata, the nine shuffled datasets and the three streams.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | MIX-ALL − R-ORD on all 12 held-out sources |
| H2 | R-ORD+LAYA-SR − R-ORD |
| H3 | R-ORD+LAYA-SR − R-ORD+LAYA: does return add to absorption? |
| H4 | R-ORD+LAYA-SR − R-ORD+LAYA-RAND: does the order matter? |
| H5 | R-ORD+PRALAYA − R-ORD |
| H6 | R-ORD+NIY − R-ORD |
| Secondary | The same comparisons on the streams alone and on the shuffled datasets alone |
| Descriptive | PRALAYA − KALA on the streams; MIX-ALL − EXT-NB and − EXT-DT |

The run is INVALID if, on held-out data, LAYA never absorbs, SṚṢṬI never restores, PRALAYA never dissolves, or NIY never withholds an outcome in its own arm.
