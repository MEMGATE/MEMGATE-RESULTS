# CCL-1 spec: one continual learner scored on every axis at once, in the weights

**Written:** 24 October 2026, 17:46 IST, before any CCL-1 code or run.

## Why
- The founder's target is complete continual learning: a model that keeps learning in its own weights, from a stream it cannot fully trust, and stays good at everything else. The lab's tests so far measured one property at a time, and the layer mostly decided what may be learned, not how the model learns it.
- GC-2 (24 October) showed the cost of that: training one skill into three open 7–8B models cut their multi-step math by 0.9, 4.8 and 24.9 points.
- CCL-1 builds the learner itself, from parts the lab has already tested one by one, and scores it on all of these at the same time, after sessions 4, 8 and 12 of one stream: learning new facts, using them in follow-on questions, not forgetting, keeping general ability (math and general knowledge), not learning lies, and leaving untouched knowledge alone.

## What
- **Model:** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (revision `a09a3545…`), LoRA on Kaggle's two T4s (TW-2's training code: fp16 base, fp32 adapter, attention projections, rank 16), one adapter trained session after session.
- **Stream:** SE-5's worlds, as in HH-1 and KE-1: 166–177 real offices, twelve sessions from January 2024 to October 2026, honest sources that are late, the stealthy liar, two colluders, two sleepers and sybil swarms. Dev world 9590 (dev pool); main worlds 9500 and 9501 (main pool), never generated before the preregistration.
- **Arms:**
  - **FROZEN:** no training.
  - **NAIVE:** each session trains every report of the session (both wordings), as TM-1's and HH-1's naive arm.
  - **LEARNER (v1),** each session:
    1. **what:** the layer's third version admits values; only offices whose admitted value changed this session are learned;
    2. **how much:** for each, the direct fact in two wordings, a dated statement, and RP-3's eight statements that follow from the change (the post the new holder holds, whom they replaced, who no longer holds it);
    3. **not forgetting:** as many rows again, drawn from the offices learned in earlier sessions, at their current values;
    4. **keeping general ability:** 64 general prompts (half GSM8K training questions, half MMLU validation questions) with the untouched model's own answers, written before any training, mixed in every session (the untouched model as its own teacher);
    5. **corrections:** when the layer later changes an admitted value (a caught lie or a late change), the new value is learned like any change.
- **Questions after sessions 4, 8 and 12** (greedy; short answers at most 24 tokens, math at most 512):
  - **current:** every changed office, the question in wording 0 and in wording 2 (never trained);
  - **follow-on:** for changed offices, "who did the holder replace" and "is the previous holder still in post" in wordings no arm trains;
  - **poison:** attacked offices;
  - **forgetting:** offices learned by session 4 whose value then stayed the same, asked again after sessions 8 and 12;
  - **locality:** the never-streamed static offices;
  - **general ability:** 150 GSM8K test questions and 200 MMLU test questions (fixed draws; none used in training).
  - Each LEARNER question is answered twice: with the adapter always on (**unrouted**), and with TM-1's scope rule (questions about offices the learner has learned go to the adapter, all others to the untouched model; **routed**). The unrouted answers are the test of the learner itself.
- **Code:** `cl/ccl1.py`: the streams, gates and training rows computed locally without a model; one private Kaggle kernel per world receives only training rows and questions, and returns answers; kernels and datasets are deleted after fetching.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev; after session 12; main worlds pooled; paired bootstrap over items within each world, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345)
- **H1 (primary):** poison, LEARNER (unrouted) − NAIVE: WIN if the upper bound is below −1 point.
- **H2:** current (wording 0), LEARNER (unrouted) − NAIVE: NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −5 points.
- **H3:** follow-on questions, LEARNER (unrouted) − NAIVE: WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point.
- **H4:** GSM8K, LEARNER (unrouted) − FROZEN: NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −3 points.
- **H5:** MMLU, LEARNER (unrouted) − FROZEN: NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −2 points.
- **H6:** forgetting (offices learned by session 4, unchanged since, right after session 12), LEARNER (unrouted) − NAIVE: NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −5 points.
- Descriptive: locality, current in wording 2, every measure routed, NAIVE's general ability, the curves after sessions 4 and 8, rows trained.

## Validity
- INVALID if FROZEN answers more than 10% of changed offices with the holder at the end, or if a kernel fails.

## Dev
World 9590, every arm. It checks the code, the timing on Kaggle and the measures, and fixes the predictions. Dev may change the number of general prompts mixed in, the epochs and the batch sizes, and fix errors; not the arms' definitions, the worlds or the measures. Those changes are recorded before the main run.

## Limits, stated in advance
- One 7B model, LoRA, two main worlds of about 170 offices; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines.
- General ability on two benchmarks, sampled; zero-shot.
- Skills are not in this stream (SK-1 to SK-3 test them separately); the learner learns facts and what follows from them.

## Addendum, 24 October 2026, 17:50 IST: the learner's rules, revised before any run
The learner is defined by these rules; they replace items 2–5 of the LEARNER above.
1. **Admit:** only what the layer's third version admits.
2. **Learn a new value with what follows from it:** the fact in two wordings and a dated statement. If the value it replaces was a real past holder (in the registry's history), also the statements that follow: the new holder's post, whom they replaced, and that the previous holder no longer holds it. If the value it replaces was never a holder (a corrected error), only the statement that the wrong value does not hold the post; nothing else is learned about it.
3. **Rehearse without a break:** every office learned earlier is rehearsed in every later session, always at its current value, never at a superseded one.
4. **Give more practice to what is weak:** before each session's training, the learner is asked every office it has learned; each office it answers wrongly gets two more rehearsal rows that session.
5. **Stay itself:** the untouched model's own answers to 64 general prompts are mixed into every session.
6. **One adapter throughout:** everything accumulates in the same adapter, session after session.
The measures, hypotheses and the NAIVE arm are unchanged.
