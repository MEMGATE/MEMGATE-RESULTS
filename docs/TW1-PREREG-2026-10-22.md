## Hypotheses (each model separately)
An office-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345.
- WIN: the whole 95% interval lies above +1 pp.
- EQUIVALENT: the whole interval lies inside (−5, +5) pp.
- NON-INFERIOR: the lower bound lies above −2 pp.

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | BOTH right, the untouched model with the dated memory line (GATED+) − DATED | WIN |
| H2 | BEFORE right, DATED − CURRENT | EQUIVALENT |
| H3 | BEFORE right on half B, PRACTICE − DATED | EQUIVALENT |
| H4 | AFTER right, DATED − CURRENT | NON-INFERIOR |

- **BOTH:** an office whose BEFORE and AFTER questions are both answered right under the same arm and condition. The system names the right holder on both sides of the change.
- **The primary result** is OLMo-2's H1. The replicates count as replicating when their verdicts match.
- **Descriptive:** each arm's rates; BEFORE and AFTER, the memory line − DATED, separately; CURRENT − FROZEN; DATED − UNDATED; the recall-first probe; half A; locality.
- **Validity:** an arm with more than 5% of its questions unanswered makes that model's run invalid.

## Addendum, 23 October 2026, about 00:20 IST: a frontier replicate on GPT-4.1 through hosted fine-tuning (before any of its questions)
- **Why:** the open models are 7–8B. TQ-W found that GPT-4.1, fine-tuned on new holders, overwrote the past too. A larger model might use a trained date where 7B models did not.
- **The runner:** `cl/tw1_hosted.py`. It uses TW-1's rows, questions, scorer and tests from `cl/tw1.py`, unchanged.
  - **Arms:** CURRENT, DATED and PRACTICE are each trained as one hosted job: GPT-4.1 (2025-04-14), Azure's Standard tier in Sweden Central, RP-3's hosted settings (6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2). UNDATED, which only descriptive comparisons use, is not trained.
  - **The untouched model:** FROZEN is the untouched gpt-4.1 deployment the lab's other GPT-4.1 runs use.
  - **Asking:** each tuned model is deployed on the developer tier, asked at temperature 0, and removed.
- **Tests:** H1–H4 with this preregistration's rules, on TQ-1's 300 main offices, reported apart from the open models.
- **Validity:** an arm with more than 5% of its questions unanswered makes the replicate invalid. A job that fails is recorded and not resubmitted.
- **Predictions:**
  - H1 (BOTH right, dated line − DATED): +55 to +90 pp, WIN. In TQ-1 the untouched gpt-4.1 with the dated line was right for 99.7% of past-date and 93.7% of after-change questions.
  - H2 and H3 are uncertain at this scale: between 0 and +30 pp. A difference above +5 pp would mean the larger model uses a trained date, or practice with it, where the 7B models did not.
  - H4: −3 to +3 pp, NON-INFERIOR.
  - D1 (CURRENT − FROZEN, past dates): −30 to −50 pp, as TQ-W found.
