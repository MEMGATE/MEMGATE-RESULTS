# AU-2 result: no setting of truth discovery matches the gate, and the gate keeps its lead when the registry is wrong 5–10% of the time

20 October 2026. One main run, in [`runs/au2-main-20261020.jsonl`](../runs/au2-main-20261020.jsonl), with the report in [`runs/au2-main-20261020-report.json`](../runs/au2-main-20261020-report.json).

**Setup.**
- Code `ba6441e5…`, as frozen in the [preregistration](AU2-PREREG-2026-10-20.md) at commit `15801a2b`.
- Model-free, 20 fresh worlds (8400–8419) at four registry error rates, 7 minutes on 8 CPU processes.
- Each world is AU-1's: 1,341 real offices, every TM-1 attacker, and the three adaptive attackers. Every registry-using arm has 303 registry questions a session.

**Validity.** No INVALID condition held. Every world ran at every error rate. No arm dismissed an honest source in any world at any error rate.

## What this means

**The gate is not a stricter threshold on the same curve.**
- Truth discovery trades lies against coverage as its threshold moves:
  - its highest-coverage setting reaches 78.5% coverage while holding attacker values for 3.05% of offices at some point;
  - at the gate's level of lies or below, it covers 74.2% or less.
- GATE-v2 covers 82.1% while holding 0.53%. That point lies above truth discovery's whole curve: no setting gives both its coverage and its level of lies.

**The gate does not need a perfect registry.**
- With the registry wrong 5% of the time, stale half the time and plainly wrong otherwise, GATE-v2 still covers 79.4%, 9.4 pp above truth discovery at matched poison. At 10% it covers 76.8%.
- Every method that trusts the registry takes in some of its errors. The fabricated values from a wrong registry are listed in the run, and are similar across those methods.
  - Added 24 October 2026, 12:06 IST, from `runs/au2-main-20261020-report.json` (curves at 5%, mean of 20 worlds): a never-true value was held at some point for 12.78% of offices by GATE-v2, 12.38% by TD-0.98 (matched truth discovery) and 11.51% by REGISTRY-ONLY.

**The first gate is fragile here, and the second is what fixes it.**
- At a perfect registry, GATE (v1) beats TD-0.98 by 3.32 pp. With the registry wrong 5% of the time, it falls 2.80 pp below it. In both cases the interval excludes 0 and all 20 worlds agree.
- GATE-v2 is the version that holds up. It was found by a model-free audit and confirmed in AU-1's preregistered H3. The package uses v2.

## What this does not show
- **It is model-free.** It measures what each rule would put into memory or training, not what a model answers. The model tests are FT-2, CD-1, RT-1 and OM-1.
- **The registry's errors are simulated:** random, half stale. A registry wrong in a coordinated way, together with the attackers, is not tested.
- **The sources and attackers are simulated;** the offices and their timelines are real. RV-2 replays real Wikidata editors.
- **Truth discovery is one TruthFinder-style implementation** with the extra confidence threshold. Other truth-discovery methods (CRH, ACCU with source dependence) were not run.

**Correction, 20 October 2026, 19:20 (from the claims ledger's check).** "At the gate's level of lies or below, it covers 74.2% or less" is not exact. TD-0.98, at 74.2% coverage, holds attacker values for 0.80% of offices, above the gate's 0.53%. The truth-discovery settings at or below 0.53% cover at most 73.26%. The comparison only gets stronger for the gate; the verdicts are unchanged.
