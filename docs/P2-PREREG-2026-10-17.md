**Findings.**

- **The mixed assembly replicates on 70 exposed datasets:** +1.5 pp over the lesson learner.
- **Putting anupalabdhi and akhyāti inside its lesson member adds almost nothing,** +0.03 pp. On the streams it costs −0.57 pp, because akhyāti keeps lessons through real changes on Elec2, as SR-6 showed.
- **Adding 1-NN and the Nyāya–Mīmāṃsā rule policy as fourth and fifth members** costs −0.22 pp on shuffled data and helps on the streams (+0.83).
- **No bug was found,** and nothing changed after the dev run.
