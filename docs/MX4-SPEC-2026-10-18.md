# MX-4: the MX-3 system on fresh real streams

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-4 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

MX-3 won on synthetic worlds with an abrupt switch. Auditing 5% of the unanimous answers and watching for vikṛti raised the accuracy of the answers the system gave alone by +9.58 pp. On nine exposed real streams in its dev run, the gain was +7.64.

MX-4 runs MX-3's frozen system, with code unchanged (`mx3.run_mx3`), on real streams no learner has seen.

Fresh real streams with natural labels are scarce. Both streams here therefore use a documented public threshold as the label, and say so.

## 2. Data

Each stream runs in its natural order as 10 windows of 1,000 consecutive cases, as in PK-1. Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule over the whole stream.

**Beijing PM2.5, US Embassy, hourly 2010–2014** (UCI 381, 43,824 hours). It is not the multi-site data SL-1 used.
- Features: month, hour, dew point, temperature, pressure, combined wind direction, and the cumulated wind speed, snow hours and rain hours.
- Dropped: `No`, `year` and `day`, which are time indices.
- Label: PM2.5 above 75 µg/m³, China's Grade II limit, applied to hourly values. Hours without PM2.5 are dropped.

**Air quality, an Italian city, hourly March 2004 – February 2005** (UCI 360, about 9,300 hours).
- Features: the four metal-oxide sensors PT08.S1 (CO), S3 (NOx), S4 (NO2) and S5 (O3), temperature, relative and absolute humidity, and the hour.
- Dropped:
  - the date;
  - the reference analysers' other pollutants (CO, NMHC, NOx and NO2 "GT"), which are not low-cost sensors;
  - PT08.S2 (NMHC), whose response nearly determines the benzene reference in this dataset.
- Label: benzene (C6H6 "GT") above 5 µg/m³, the EU limit value, applied to hourly values. Hours without the benzene reference are dropped, and −200 marks a missing value.
- These sensors are known to drift over the year (De Vito et al., 2008).

## 3. Arms, hypotheses and decision rules

The arms are MX-3's three, unchanged: GRAND, GRAND+AUDIT and GRAND+AUDIT-NORESET. The measure is the accuracy of the answers given alone. The decision rules are as before, with the CI stratified by stream.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | GRAND+AUDIT − GRAND |
| H2 | GRAND+AUDIT − GRAND+AUDIT-NORESET |
| Descriptive | coverage, labels and resets per window; accuracy over all cases |

**Dev.** The nine exposed real streams, as in MX-3's dev run, with the same code. MX-3's dev run is that run, so MX-4 has no separate dev run.
