# CCL-2 spec: one continual learner, all twelve abilities measured in one test

**Written:** 24 October 2026, 18:59 IST, before any CCL-2 code or run. It extends CCL-1 (stopped before any result).

## Why
The founder's target is complete continual learning, tested at once rather than one property at a time. CCL-2 is one 7B learner that learns from one stream for twelve sessions, in its weights, and is scored after sessions 4, 8 and 12 on every ability of the lab's twelve-item list:

| # | Ability | How CCL-2 measures it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learns new facts | current holders of changed offices (wording 0; wording 2 never trained) |
| 2 | Does not learn lies | attacked offices answered with the attacker's value |
| 3 | Does not forget | offices changed by session 4 and unchanged since, still right later; never-streamed offices (locality) |
| 4 | Removes what it learned wrongly | changed offices answered with a superseded or corrected value as the current one |
| 5 | Knows when things held | "Who was the X of Y on DATE?" for a date before and a date after the change |
| 6 | Learns skills | migration recipes for 28 real polars changes arrive in the stream (some poisoned); each change is tested by running the model's rewrite of an old line in polars 2.0.0 |
| 7 | Reasons with new knowledge | follow-on questions (which post the new holder holds, who held it before, whether the previous holder still does) on offices trained **without** their consequences (half B) |
| 8 | Learns better over time | for each session, the share of values learned in it that the self-test before the next session finds right (descriptive) |
| 9 | Keeps general ability | 150 GSM8K and 200 MMLU test questions against the untouched model |
| 10 | Size | 7B (stated; larger models later) |
| 11 | Real data | real office timelines from Wikidata; sources and attackers simulated (stated) |
| 12 | All in one system | one adapter, one stream, all of the above at once |

## What
- **Model:** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, one LoRA adapter trained session after session on Kaggle's two T4s (CCL-1's code).
- **Worlds:** SE-5's family. Dev world 9790 (dev pool); main worlds 9700 and 9701 (main pool), never generated before the preregistration.
- **Skills in the stream:** the 28 polars changes of SK-3, in four groups of seven whose true recipes arrive from honest sources at sessions 2, 5, 8 and 11. For the 14 changes SK-3 poisoned, the attacker's recipe arrives one session after the true one.
- **Arms:**
  - **FROZEN:** no training.
  - **NAIVE:** each session trains every report and every recipe that arrives.
  - **LEARNER:** CCL-1's rules (addendum of 17:50: admit only what the layer admits; learn what follows; rehearse every learned item every session, more for what the self-test shows weak; the untouched model's own answers to general prompts; one adapter), with three additions:
    - what follows from a change is trained for half A of the offices only, and 60 rows teaching the general rules of posts (FT-4's, invented names) are trained in session 1 and ten of them rehearsed every later session;
    - recipes are admitted by SK-1's gate (every name exists in polars 2.0.0, nothing to install) and then learned and rehearsed like facts;
    - each value is learned with its date, so earlier holders stay known as past.
- **Questions** after sessions 4, 8 and 12 (greedy; short answers at most 24 tokens, rewrites at most 64, math at most 512): every office in wordings 0 and 2; dated questions for changed offices; follow-on questions for halves A and B; locality; one rewrite question per polars change whose true recipe has arrived; GSM8K and MMLU. Rewrites are run here, in the polars 2.0.0 environment, after fetching.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev; after session 12; main worlds pooled; paired bootstrap over items within each world, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345; LEARNER without routing)
| | Ability | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | 2 | attacker values answered, LEARNER − NAIVE | WIN if the upper bound is below −1 point |
| H2 | 1 | current holders, LEARNER − NAIVE | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −5 points |
| H3 | 3 | early changes kept, LEARNER − NAIVE | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −5 points |
| H4 | 4 | superseded or corrected values answered as current, LEARNER − NAIVE | WIN if the upper bound is below −1 point |
| H5 | 5 | dated questions, LEARNER − NAIVE | WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point |
| H6 | 6 | rewrites that pass, LEARNER − NAIVE | WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point |
| H7 | 7 | follow-on questions on half B, LEARNER − NAIVE | WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point |
| H8 | 9 | GSM8K, LEARNER − FROZEN | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −3 points |
| H9 | 9 | MMLU, LEARNER − FROZEN | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −2 points |
| H10 | 3 | locality, LEARNER − FROZEN | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −5 points |

Ability 8 is reported descriptively, abilities 10–12 are stated by the design. The result is reported as a scorecard: each ability passed, not shown, or failed, with its numbers.

## Validity
- INVALID if FROZEN answers more than 10% of changed offices with the holder at the end, or if a kernel fails.

## Dev
World 9790, every arm. It checks the code, the timing and the measures, and fixes the predictions. Dev may change epochs, batch sizes, the number of general prompts and rule rows, and fix errors; not the arms' definitions, the worlds or the measures.

## Limits, stated in advance
- One 7B model, LoRA; two main worlds of about 170 offices; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; skills are 28 one-line migrations of one library.
- General ability on two benchmarks, sampled, zero-shot.

## Addendum, 24 October 2026, 19:17 IST: the same test on hosted models (dev)
- **Why.** The founder asked for the test on models as large as possible and on as many as possible. The largest models this account can fine-tune session after session are OpenAI's, through Azure's continuous fine-tuning (a job may start from an earlier job's fine-tuned model): gpt-4.1 (the largest), gpt-4o, gpt-4.1-mini and gpt-4.1-nano. Llama-3.3-70B trains only from its base model there, one job, so it cannot run a chain of sessions; it runs FT-4's one-step version of the learner's recipe.
- **The same.** Worlds, rows, arms (NAIVE, LEARNER), questions and scoring: `ccl2.plan` and `ccl2.score`, unchanged. The learner's general prompts are answered by the untouched model itself (its base deployment), as the 7B learner's are.
- **Different.** The service's own fine-tuning instead of LoRA (its method and batch size are not visible); each session is a job continuing from the previous session's model (global training, 2 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2); questions go through a deployment of the current model (developer tier, 50 units; gpt-4o has no developer tier: global standard, 8 units, billed per hour while it stands), temperature 0, the same token limits; the self-test runs on the deployment of the model trained through the previous session. "One adapter" becomes one line of models.
- **Code.** `cl/ccl2_hosted.py`; one run directory per model (`runs/ccl2h-<model>-dev-20261024`); one chain per arm, each on its own resource: gpt-4.1 NAIVE memgate-ft-ncus, LEARNER memgate-ft-swc; gpt-4o NAIVE memgate-ft-eus2, LEARNER memgate-ft-swc2; gpt-4.1-mini NAIVE memgate-ft-ncus2, LEARNER memgate-ft-swc3; gpt-4.1-nano NAIVE memgate-ft-ncus3, LEARNER memgate-ft-swc4. FROZEN is the untouched base deployment on memgate-ft-ncus.
- **Size.** About 0.3 million training tokens per arm per world (rows and rehearsal estimated from the plan of world 9790), trained twice: about 1.2 million trained tokens per model per world.
- **Dev.** World 9790 on all four models. A failed job stops its chain and is recorded; a resubmission is decided and recorded here, not retried in a loop. The main worlds (9700, 9701) run per model only after its dev, under a preregistration carrying the hosted code hash (`python cl/ccl2_hosted.py hash`); the runner refuses them otherwise.
- **Limits added.** Closed-weight models trained by a service: the training method is the provider's; the result says what a learner built on such a service does, not why.
- **Correction, 19:55 IST: gpt-4o dropped.** Azure refuses new deployments of gpt-4o-2024-08-06 ("deprecating state"), the only gpt-4o version it fine-tunes, so the untouched model can neither answer the questions (FROZEN) nor the learner's general prompts. Its NAIVE session-1 job was cancelled; the hosted test runs on gpt-4.1, gpt-4.1-mini and gpt-4.1-nano (`runs/ccl2h-gpt4o-dev-20261024/dropped.json`).

## Addendum, 24 October 2026, 21:27 IST: the same test at 72B, on Kaggle's TPU
- **Why.** The founder asked for the 72B model the lab has called necessary (Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct). Kaggle's two T4s hold about 30 GB, less than the 72B needs even in 4-bit (about 41 GB); moving parts of it in and out of memory would make one world take more than a day. Kaggle also gives this account a TPU v3-8 (128 GB, 20 hours a week, unused so far), and Kaggle hosts the model (145 GB).
- **The model on the TPU.** In 16-bit the 72B does not fit 128 GB, so its untouched weights are held in 8-bit: int8 with one scale per output channel and group of up to 128 inputs (about 74 GB). The adapters and their training are 32-bit, as on the GPU.
- **Code.** `cl/jax_qwen.py` (the model in JAX: tensor parallel over the TPU cores, greedy decoding with a key-value cache and the model's repetition penalty) and `cl/ccl2_tpu.py` (the GPU kernel's procedure, line for line: general prompts answered by the untouched model, FROZEN, then NAIVE and LEARNER with the same rows, self-test, extra practice, adapter settings, optimizer, epochs, eight rows per step in passes of at most four rows and 768 tokens, and the same questions). Scoring is `ccl2.score`, unchanged.
- **Checked before any TPU time** (on Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, CPU, against transformers): in float32 the logits agree within 0.0004 and the top token at every position; in 16-bit, greedy answers to five prompts agree for three (rounding); with 8-bit group scales, for four (one scale per channel alone agreed for two, so groups are used); training on two invented facts made the model answer them, and removing the adapter restored its own answers; the kernel's smoke mode runs end to end.
- **Different from the 7B run.** The machine (TPU, bfloat16 compute; the 7B ran in float16 on GPUs), the 8-bit untouched weights, and random draws (adapter start, dropout).
- **Steps.** A smoke run on the TPU (loading, memory, speed on a few rows and questions), then dev world 9790 at 72B, then a preregistration carrying the TPU code hash (`python cl/ccl2_tpu.py hash`; the runner refuses the main worlds otherwise), then main worlds 9700 and 9701.
- **Correction, 21:30 IST: gpt-4.1-nano at six epochs.** In two epochs it learned nothing (session 1: training loss 2.31 over the first tenth of steps, 2.52 over the last; the learner's self-test 0 of 83, against 74 of 83 for gpt-4.1 and 83 of 83 for gpt-4.1-mini). Its chains were stopped and restart at six epochs, FT-1's setting for this model (FT-1's checks: 3 epochs learned 0 of 12 facts, 10 epochs 12 of 12). gpt-4.1 and gpt-4.1-mini stay at two.
- **Correction, 22:23 IST: the TPU is a v5e-8.** Kaggle's TPU is now the v5e-8 (also 128 GB); the first smoke push named the older v3-8, and Kaggle ran it on an ordinary CPU machine (33 GB) without saying so, where it was killed while loading the weights (no TPU time used). The kernel now asks for the v5e-8, stops at once if the session has no TPU, and sends the weights to the TPU one layer at a time, so the host never holds more than one layer (checked again on CPU: the same results as before).
- **Dev change, 23:37 IST (after the 7B dev, world 9790): the learner now does what its rules say.** The dev showed two places where the code fell short of the stated rules. (1) "Each value is learned with its date, so earlier holders stay known as past": only the new holder was trained with a date, so dates before a change were answered right for 3.4% of offices (the untouched model 35.0%). Now, when a change is learned, the earlier holder is trained as holding on the date it was learned and on the last session the registry shows it held (the registry is asked backwards from the previous session, usually once: 151 questions in world 9790, 129 and 134 in the main worlds). (2) "Rehearse every learned item every session": only the direct facts were rehearsed. Now each learned office's date rows (present and past) and what follows from its latest change (half A) are trained every session too. The arms, worlds, measures and the general rules are unchanged; the learner trains more rows (up to about 1,100 a session in world 9790, against naive's 550-770). A second 7B dev (world 9790) checks it before predictions are frozen.
