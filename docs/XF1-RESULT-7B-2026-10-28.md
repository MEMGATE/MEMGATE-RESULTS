## What this result supports
- **The learner is not specific to one model family.** On a fully open model from another lab, every ability passed that passed on Qwen2.5-7B with CCL-4's rules, except ability 8 (learning faster within the run), which was not shown here, and ability 4 also passed against the untouched model.
- **Naive fine-tuning damaged OLMo-2 more:** its GSM8K fell from 84.0% to 24.0% and 32.7%, and what follows from a change to 2.8% and 6.6%; the learner kept GSM8K and MMLU exactly at the untouched model's level and answered what follows at 77.7% and 78.6%.
- OLMo-2 knows far fewer offices than Qwen (37–39% of the never-streamed offices against Qwen's 99%); the learner left that knowledge exactly as it was.
