## Main
- **Worlds:** 8600 and 8601 (AU-1's family with a correct registry: TM-1's 1,341 real offices, twelve sessions from January 2024 to October 2026, every TM-1 source and attacker plus AU-1's three adaptive attackers; AU-1's question budget).
- **Arms:** LAYER (the third version, audit share 40%), NAIVE (the last report about each office), TD (truth discovery at τ = 0.98 with the layer's budget), each one training file of two rows per office in its memory after the twelfth session; FROZEN, the untouched model.
- **Jobs:** six, one per resource, so that they train at the same time: world 8600 on memgate-ft-eus2 (LAYER), memgate-ft-eus2b (NAIVE) and jayeshupp-8050-resource (TD); world 8601 on memgate-ft-swc2 (LAYER), memgate-ft-ncus3 (NAIVE) and memgate-ft-swc4 (TD). Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (version 9), from the untouched model, 6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2, global training.
- **Asking:** the untouched model through a deployment of the base model (global standard, capacity 20); each fine-tuned model through a deployment on the resource that trained it (global standard, capacity 50), deleted after its questions. Every model is asked every question of its world: each office in wordings 0 and 2, the never-streamed static offices in wording 0, and SE-1's core pool. Temperature 0, at most 16 new tokens, 8 requests at a time per deployment, retries on rate limits. Questions still unanswered after a pass are asked again in one later pass of the asker, which is recorded.
- **Routing:** LAYER's reported arm takes the LAYER model's reply for offices in its file and the untouched model's reply for every other question. NAIVE and TD answer everything from their own models.
- **Scoring (`ft3.py report`):** a reply "names" a value when it contains one of the value's name forms that no other holder of the office shares (`se3_world.said`). Measures as in the spec: attacker answers (share of offices), current and stale (share of changed offices), locality, retention (the first 100 core questions the untouched model answers right), learned.

## Validity
- INVALID if any fine-tuned arm's learned share is below 50% in either world, or if the untouched model answers more than 10% of changed offices with the holder at the end.
- A job that fails, or a deployment that is refused, leaves that arm not run, recorded; it is not retried in a loop. One deliberate retry after a long pause is allowed, recorded first, as the lab's rule for refused deployments says.

## Descriptive
- Every measure for FROZEN, LAYER (routed), the LAYER model on its own, NAIVE and TD, per world and pooled; wording 2 (never trained); stale answers; retention; learned; unanswered questions; how often the question linker finds the office.
- The LAYER model on its own against NAIVE (attacker answers, current, locality): what the layer's file does without routing.

## Rules
- The result is reported whatever it is, with the limits the spec states: one job from the untouched model per arm, so the end-of-stream memory as training data, not continual training; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; one model; two worlds that share their offices; routing computed from two deployments' answers.
