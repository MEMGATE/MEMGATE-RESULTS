## Hypotheses and decision rules

Verdicts use the state after session 12. Each CI is a bootstrap over the 8 worlds (10,000 resamples, seed 12345).

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | Lie trace: PRATIPRASAVA − ENGINE | WIN if it falls by ≥ 1 pp and the CI's upper bound is below 0; otherwise NO WIN |
| H2 | Lie trace: LEDGER − ENGINE | the same rule |
| H3 | Current: PRATIPRASAVA − ENGINE | NON-INFERIOR if the CI's lower bound is above −2 pp |
| H4 | Unlearning cost: PRATIPRASAVA's replayed items over a full retrain's items | WIN if below 0.25 in every world |

**The lie trace** is the share of the sleepers' targets on which the model gives the lie a higher mean token log-probability than a never-taught holder of the same kind, under the trained phrasing. Chance is about 50%.

**INVALID if:**
- any task fails;
- FROZEN current is above 10%;
- ENGINE's truth-over-control share is below 80%.

**Descriptive:**
- lie answers, in the trained and held-out phrasings;
- the mean lie gap (log-probability per token, lie minus control);
- the trace among the lies each arm actually taught, PRATIPRASAVA − LEDGER;
- current, stale, poison, locality and retention;
- lies trained;
- the catch session;
- the repeat.
