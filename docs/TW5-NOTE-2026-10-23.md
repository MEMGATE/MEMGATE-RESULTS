# TW-5 note: how much practice the weights need before a trained date carries over

**Status:** descriptive, not preregistered. 23 October 2026. Code `cl/tw5.py`, SHA-256 `4257ce37f08ceb11df476e84933e54b29c088d2e873822476474136435ea96bb`. Kaggle, two T4s.

## Question
TW-1 found that date questions practised on 150 offices taught three open models to use a trained date on 150 other offices (38–45% of past-date questions right, against 0–0.7% without practice). In dev, with 25 practised offices, nothing carried over. How does the effect grow with the number of practised offices?

## Design
- TW-1's main offices, rows and settings, unchanged (`cl/tw1.py`).
- Each arm is one adapter trained from the untouched weights on DATED's rows for all 300 offices, plus TW-1's two practice rows for the first N offices of half A (N = 25, 50, 100, 150). P150 uses the same rows as TW-1's PRACTICE arm, in another order.
- Every arm is asked about half B's 150 offices, none practised in any arm, at a date before the change (BEFORE) and after it (AFTER), with no memory, in TQ-1's words.
- Two families: OLMo-2-7B and Qwen2.5-7B. 1,500 answers each, none missing.

## What it shows
- **More practice, more carry-over, but slowly.** Both dates right rose with the number of practised offices: 6.0 → 10.7 → 16.7 → 26.0% on OLMo-2, and 8.7 → 21.3 → 22.7 → 34.7% on Qwen.
- **Even 25 practised offices did something** here (10.0–10.7% of past dates right, against 0.0–0.7% for TW-1's DATED arm on the same offices), unlike TW-1's dev, whose offices and rows differed.
- **The past stays mostly overwritten.** With 150 practised offices, the models still named the new holder for a past date on 51–59% of offices.
- **Qwen's middle doses cost the present:** after-change answers fell to 75.3% (P50) and 64.7% (P100), then recovered at P150 (95.3%).
- **The same rows in another order gave a different size of effect.** P150 repeats TW-1's PRACTICE rows. On half B, TW-1's PRACTICE had past dates right for 44.7% (OLMo-2) and 40.7% (Qwen), and both dates for 35.3% and 29.3%. Here: 27.3% and 37.3%, and 26.0% and 34.7%. So TW-1's practice effect is real, but its size moves by up to 17 points between two trainings of a 7B model.
- **Against the memory route:** a reader trained to read the dated memory line (TW-2) answered both dates right for 99.7% (OLMo-2) and 100% (Qwen) of new offices. Practice in the weights reached at most 34.7%.

## Limits
- Descriptive: no hypothesis was fixed in advance, and each arm is one training run.
- Two families; Nemotron-Nano was not run.
- One adapter per dose, trained from the untouched weights, not a curriculum.
