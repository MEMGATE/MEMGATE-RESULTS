# FT-5 spec: continual learning at 70B by periodic consolidation, through Azure's hosted fine-tuning

**Written:** 24 October 2026, __TIME__ IST, before any FT-5 code or run. To be built after FT-4's dev run shows that its recipe trains and is asked without errors.

## Why
- Azure's hosted fine-tuning cannot train a fine-tuned open model further, so a 70B model cannot be trained session after session there, and no GPU of our own is available for it.
- A learner can still learn continually by **consolidating periodically**: at chosen points in the stream, a new model is trained from the untouched one on everything the learner holds at that point. Knowledge that was wrong and later corrected (a caught lie, a late change) is simply absent from the next consolidation; that is how removal works in this route.
- FT-5 measures such a learner at 70B over time: after sessions 4, 8 and 12 of the same stream, what it holds, what it answers, and whether lies that entered and were later caught leave the model at the next consolidation.

## What
- **Worlds:** SE-5's family, as in FT-4 and CCL-1; worlds to be named in the preregistration (never generated before).
- **Arms,** each a new hosted job from the untouched Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct at each consolidation point (sessions 4, 8 and 12), FT-4's settings:
  - **NAIVE:** the last report about each office up to that point;
  - **LEARNER:** FT-4's recipe on the layer's memory at that point (dated statements, what follows from each change, negations for corrected errors, the untouched model's own answers to general prompts).
- **Questions after each consolidation:** FT-4's (offices in two wordings, follow-on questions, locality, GSM8K and MMLU draws), scored against the truth at that point.
- **Measures over time:** current holders, attacker values answered, values that were held by the learner at an earlier point and later corrected (are they gone after the next consolidation?), math and general knowledge against the untouched model.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after a dev world)
- **H1 (primary):** attacker values answered after session 12, LEARNER − NAIVE: WIN if the upper bound is below −1 point.
- **H2:** corrected values (held by the learner's memory at an earlier consolidation, absent later): the share still answered after the next consolidation is at most the untouched model's rate plus 2 points.
- **H3:** GSM8K after session 12, LEARNER − FROZEN: NON-INFERIOR at −3 points.

## Cost, stated in advance
Two arms × three consolidations × (one dev world + two main worlds) = 18 jobs of about 0.5 million trained tokens each, at $4.50 per million: about $40, plus hosting and asking.

## Limits, stated in advance
- Periodic consolidation from the untouched model is one way to learn continually; it costs a full training run per consolidation, which the layer's adapter route (CCL-1, TM-1) does not.
- One model, small worlds, simulated sources and attackers over real timelines.
