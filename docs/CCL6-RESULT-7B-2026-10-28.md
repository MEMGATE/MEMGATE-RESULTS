- **Offices:** worlds 9708 (02:30–04:59) and 9709 (05:03–07:32), real offices from the Wikidata snapshot with simulated sources and attackers.

**Validity:** all four worlds valid. Both arms finished 34 sessions; the untouched model answered every question (2,832, 2,834, 2,657 and 2,657) and named the current holder or release of 0% of changed facts; the removal ran in session 4 (129 to 131 rows left out); lie scores exist for every arm.
