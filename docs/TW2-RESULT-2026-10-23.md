# TW-2 result: a small adapter trained to read the layer's dated memory line, on other offices, made three open 7–8B models answer both dates right for 98–100% of new offices, with no loss of untouched knowledge

**Runs:** Kaggle, two T4 GPUs, TQ-1's 300 main offices, 23 October 2026:
- `runs/tw2-main-olmo7b-20261023`: OLMo-2-7B, 03:14–03:36 IST;
- `runs/tw2-main-qwen7b-20261023`: Qwen2.5-7B, 03:15–03:38 IST;
- `runs/tw2-main-nemotron8b-20261023`: NVIDIA Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B, 03:37–04:06 IST.

Every run asked 4,200 questions and left none unanswered.

**Preregistration:** [TW2-PREREG-2026-10-23.md](TW2-PREREG-2026-10-23.md), code hash `de454a7e…`, committed at 03:14 (`b94048f8`) before any main question. **Spec:** [TW2-SPEC-2026-10-23.md](TW2-SPEC-2026-10-23.md).
**Machine summaries:** each run's `summary.json`, written by `python cl/tw2.py report OUT`.

**The adapter (READER):** one LoRA adapter, 2 epochs, trained on 336 of RP-1's offices, which share none with the test offices. It holds 1,008 rows: each office's dated memory line with one past-date and one after-change question, and its current-value line with one after-change question. No test fact is in the training.

## What it shows
- **Reading time from memory is a skill the weights can learn.** After training on other offices, all three models answered both dates right for 98.3–100% of new offices. Untouched, with the same line, they did so for 17.3–68.3%.
  - Untouched, OLMo-2 missed past dates; Nemotron-Nano named the previous holder for 47.7% of after-change dates. READER fixed both.
- **The facts stayed in memory.** With no memory, READER answered the test offices as the untouched model did (past dates 0.3–4.3% against 0.7–5.3%). It learned to read, not the answers.
- **No loss of untouched knowledge:** locality 54.0% against 49.7%, 86.7% against 85.0%, and 17.0% against 16.7%.
  - In TW-1, training the facts themselves into OLMo-2 and Qwen cost them 35–74 points of locality.
- **The current-value line still works:** after-change answers were 99.7–100% right with it, and Nemotron-Nano gained 33 points.
- **Together with TW-1:** time is best kept by the layer's split, with dated facts in memory and the skill of reading them in the weights.
  - The facts trained into the weights with their dates answered both dates right for at most 0.3% of offices, and 25–35% with practice.
  - The reading skill answered 98–100%.

## Limits
- **Open 7–8B models,** float16 on T4s, one adapter per model.
- **One kind of line** (office holders, the layer's two line forms) and TQ-1's question wording. Transfer to other wordings, relations or domains is untested.
- **Frontier models** already read the line almost perfectly (TQ-1); TW-2 matters for small models.
