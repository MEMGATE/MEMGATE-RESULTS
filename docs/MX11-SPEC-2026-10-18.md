# MX-11: a system-level alarm. When the three sources frequently differ among themselves, stop answering alone (Arthaśāstra 1.12 with Caraka's prakṛti–vikṛti)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-11 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

MX-10 found the supervisor UNSAFE when two of three sources fail: 2.00 wrong answers alone per 100, against a bound of 2. Dismissal is a one-culprit rule. With two culprits it never fires.

**The reading.** Arthaśāstra 1.12 says: "If they (the three sources) frequently differ…". Read of the network as a whole, the three reports disagreeing among themselves means that no report should be believed.

**The mechanism.**
- **The disagreement measure.** For each case while three spies are trusted, a three-way split is recorded: all three report different labels.
- **The health check.** It is PK-1's `unwell`, applied to that record (Caraka Sūtrasthāna 9.4: compare the state with the constitution). It fires when the three-way split rate over the last 30 cases rises 3 SE above its own earlier rate.
- **The alarm.** When it fires, the network stops answering alone for the rest of the run: it asks about every case, and in a product it would tell the owner.

## 2. Arms

| Arm | Content |
| --- | --- |
| SUPERVISOR | `shastra/mx5.py`'s `run_supervisor`, unchanged |
| **SUPERVISOR+ALARM** | a copy of `run_supervisor` in `shastra/mx11.py`, with the alarm added |

With the alarm switched off, the copy must give byte-identical answers to `run_supervisor`. A test checks this.

## 3. Data and conditions

- **Worlds.** VIEWS (AR-1's generator) at the new seeds 53000–53019, 400 cases each.
- **Conditions.** SOUND, ONE (MX-5's `fail_from`) and TWO (MX-9's `fail_two`), with the failure from case 240.
- **Testability condition.** SUPERVISOR's sound coverage must be at least 5%.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

| Hypothesis | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary, safety)** | SUPERVISOR+ALARM in TWO is SAFE if the upper bound of the 95% CI of its wrong answers alone after case 240 is below 2 per 100. |
| H2 (cost when sound) | SUPERVISOR+ALARM − SUPERVISOR, net credence, SOUND. **NO HARM** if the CI lower bound is above −1.0 pp. |
| H3 (cost with one failure) | the same, in ONE |
| Descriptive | how often the alarm fires in each condition, and when; wrong answers alone after case 240 for both arms |

**INVALID if:**
- any run is non-deterministic or crashes;
- the copy without the alarm differs from `run_supervisor`;
- the testability condition fails.

## 5. Predictions

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | SAFE | Two broken spies make three-way splits common, so the alarm fires and answering stops. |
| H2 | NO HARM | Three-way splits are rare when sound, so false alarms should be rare. |
| H3 | NO HARM, or a small cost | One broken spy mostly gives 2-against-1 splits, not three-way ones. |
