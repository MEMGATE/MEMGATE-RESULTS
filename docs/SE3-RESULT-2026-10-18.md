## What this means

**A model can be kept current, in its weights, with the real world, safely.**
- **What it learned.** Over twelve sessions spanning January 2024 to October 2026, a 7B model was taught real changes of office. These included four new French prime ministers, a new UK prime minister and dozens of new football managers and chief executives. Each fact first had to overcome what the model already believed.
- **The result.** The engine taught it 97.9% of the current holders, more than naive fine-tuning did (96.2%).
- **What went in.** Not one poisoned value reached the weights, against 24.7% of poisoned facts answered falsely by naive fine-tuning.
- **What was kept.** Everything it already knew and was not told about stayed exactly as it was: locality 98.8%, the same as the untrained model.

**Naive continual fine-tuning has a failure that SE-1 and SE-2 could not show: it rewrites what it was never taught.**
- **Locality collapses.** Taught that many offices changed, the model started naming new people for offices that had not changed. Locality fell from 98.8% to 15.2%.
- **Other remedies tested on dev did not fix it:**
  - halving the learning rate and the epochs;
  - reciting the model's own answers about other offices (svādhyāya);
  - the constitution check, which protected only the facts it checked.
- **What fixed it is Kiraṇa's locus rule.** Learned knowledge applies only within its locus, the fact it was learned for. The adapter answers only questions about a (relation, subject) it was trained on, and every other question goes to the untouched model. The router links the question's subject by its longest name and its relation by its phrase. It made 2 errors in 800 checks.

**What each Sanatana principle contributed, from the dev amendments and this run:**

| Principle | What it did |
| --- | --- |
| **Arthaśāstra 1.12 dismissal** | Caught the stealthy and colluding sources in the first session. |
| **Jijñāsā, with saṃśaya from any report** | Every contradiction, even from a dismissed source, prompted a check, but nothing was believed until the registry confirmed it. This "verify, never trust" rule recovered the timely truths that dismissed sources still carried. It lifted currency from 78.6% to 97.8% on dev, and cut wrong admitted values at the end from 13–21 (Gate2) to 1–7 per world. |
| **Smṛti vs viparyaya, read from the registry's history** (Yoga Sūtra 1.11) | A source lagging on a real past holder is remembering, not lying. Without this, honest sources were dismissed on real timelines. |
| **The pramāṇa gate itself** | Against a plain majority vote with the same check and locus: poison −2.98 pp (WIN) and current holders +29.27. Majority voting kept stale holders for 27.8% of changed offices, because old values keep more supporters than new ones for a while. |
| **Kiraṇa locus** | Locality +83.5 pp. |
| **Prakṛti–vikṛti (the constitution check)** | Never needed to roll back once the locus rule was in place. It remains the guard against misrouted damage. |

## Limits, stated plainly

- **The facts and their timelines are real. The sources are simulated:**
  - honest reporters with a one-session lag;
  - a stealthy poisoner and colluders with timely camouflage;
  - a registry that is Wikidata itself, consulted as an oracle.
- **A query has a cost this test only counts:** about 15% of fact-sessions were verified.
- **The 8 worlds share one main pool of facts** and differ in sources, dark facts, poison targets and order. The CIs therefore measure stream variation, not fact sampling.
- **The locus rule needs questions that name their subject.** Questions that refer to a subject only indirectly ("the French premier") were not tested.
- **Knowledge here means entity-centric facts.** Skills, procedures and multi-hop use of the updated facts were not tested.
- **The baselines are naive fine-tuning and a majority vote.** Retrieval into the context (RAG) with the same gate, and dedicated knowledge editors (MEMIT, AlphaEdit, WISE, GRACE), were not run. For simple facts, a retrieval memory behind the same gate would likely do as well. The gate is the part that transfers to any memory, in weights or in context.
