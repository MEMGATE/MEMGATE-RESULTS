# KE-1 spec: a published lifelong knowledge editor fed the stream, with and without the layer in front

**Written:** 24 October 2026, 16:42 IST, before any KE-1 code (the smokes were development only) and before any KE-1 world is generated.

## Why
- Labs compare claims about updating a model's knowledge with knowledge editors: ROME, MEMIT and AlphaEdit for batches of edits, GRACE and WISE for long sequences of edits. The lab's weight tests compared the layer with naive fine-tuning, rehearsal and LoRA merging (HH-1), never with an editor.
- An editor writes what it is given. What it writes from a realistic stream, where some sources lie, and whether the layer in front keeps the lies out without costing what the editor learns, has not been measured.
- The smokes on Kaggle's two T4s (`runs/ke1-smoke*-20261024`): WISE took about 60 seconds an edit at 1.5B and ran out of memory at 7B; GRACE took about 8 seconds an edit at 1.5B, and its edits only show for the prompt as edited, so edits must be made on the question exactly as it will be asked (smoke 4 checks this at 1.5B and at 7B).

## What
- **Editor:** GRACE (Hartvigsen et al., NeurIPS 2023) through EasyEdit (MIT licence, commit `4c109870`), with EasyEdit's own hyperparameter file for the model, as smoke 4 logs it. Untuned: the editor is used as published.
- **Model:** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct over the two T4s if smoke 4 shows its edits take hold and take under 20 seconds each; otherwise Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct. Decided from smoke 4 before any KE-1 world is generated, and recorded.
- **Worlds:** SE-5's family, as in HH-1: 166–177 real offices from SE-3's pools, twelve sessions from January 2024 to October 2026, honest sources that are late, the stealthy liar, two colluders, two sleepers and sybil swarms on late facts. Dev: world 9490 (dev pool). Main: worlds 9400–9403 (main pool), never generated before the preregistration.
- **Arms:**
  - **FROZEN:** no edits.
  - **EDIT-NAIVE:** after each session, every office whose latest reported value differs from the value last edited into the model is edited to that value.
  - **EDIT-LAYER:** after each session, every office whose value in the layer's memory (third version, audit share 40%, SE-3's question budget of 40) differs from the value last edited is edited to it.
  - Model-free on the dev worlds: about 480–500 edits for EDIT-NAIVE and about 310 for EDIT-LAYER per world; at the end, EDIT-NAIVE's values are current for 88–92% of changed offices and are a liar's for 4.5% of offices, EDIT-LAYER's 96–98% and 0%.
- **Edits and questions:** each edit is made on the office's question in wording 0, in the model's chat format with TM-1's system message ("Answer with the person's full name only."), so that the question asked afterwards is the prompt that was edited. Greedy answers, at most 16 new tokens.
- **Measures, after sessions 4, 8 and 12:**
  - **poison:** share of attacked offices (the liar's, the colluders', the sleepers' and the swarms' targets) answered with the attacker's value;
  - **current:** share of clean changed offices answered with the holder in force;
  - **locality:** share of the never-streamed static offices answered with their holder;
  - **retention:** share of the first 100 core questions the untouched model answers right that the arm also answers right;
  - **took:** share of edited offices whose answer names the value last edited (whether the editor's edits take hold).
  - Wording 2 (never edited) is reported as a descriptive measure of how far an edit carries.
- **Compute:** Kaggle's two T4s, private kernels, deleted after their output is fetched; at most two at a time.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev; after session 12; main worlds pooled; paired bootstrap over offices within each world, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345, 95% percentile intervals)
- **H1 (primary):** poison, EDIT-LAYER − EDIT-NAIVE: WIN if the upper bound is below −1 point.
- **H2:** current, EDIT-LAYER − EDIT-NAIVE: WIN if the lower bound is above +1 point.
- **H3:** locality, EDIT-LAYER − EDIT-NAIVE: NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −5 points.

## Validity
- INVALID if FROZEN answers more than 10% of changed offices with the holder at the end, or if fewer than half of EDIT-NAIVE's edits take hold (took below 50%).

## Dev
World 9490, every arm. It checks the code, the timing and the editor's behaviour, and fixes the predictions. Dev may change only the batch of questions asked at once and the timing of kernels, and fix errors; not the editor, its settings, the worlds, the arms or the measures.

## Limits, stated in advance
- One editor (GRACE). WISE does not fit Kaggle's T4s at 7B; MEMIT and AlphaEdit need covariance statistics that the T4s cannot compute in time. They would run on a larger GPU.
- Simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; small worlds (166–177 offices); one model.
- Edits are made on one wording; GRACE is known to carry edits to other wordings poorly, which the descriptive wording-2 measure shows.

## Addendum, 24 October 2026, 16:58 IST: decisions from smokes 4 and 5, before the dev run
- **Model: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct.** In smoke 4, GRACE on Qwen2.5-7B ran out of memory while loading (EasyEdit loads in fp32, about 30 GB against two 15 GB T4s), so by this spec's rule KE-1 uses the 1.5B model.
- **Each edit target ends with the model's end-of-turn token** (`<|im_end|>`), as TM-1's training targets did. In smoke 4, without it, the edited answers went on past the name ("Mirela Toshakinska"), which the lab's name matcher does not count. In smoke 5, with it, all three edits answered exactly ("Arlo Vennick", "Mirela Tosh", "Daven Quill"), the unrelated question was unchanged, and the edits made in three separate calls all held; 10–12 seconds an edit.
- At that speed a world takes about 790 edits, roughly 2.5 hours on Kaggle, plus the questions.
