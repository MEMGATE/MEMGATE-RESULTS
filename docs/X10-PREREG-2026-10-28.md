## Why
Every learner world so far streams a few hundred offices (CCL-4: 166). A lab will ask whether the learner still works, and what it costs, when the facts multiply. X10 runs CCL-4's test unchanged on worlds of 1,544 offices, 9.3 times CCL-4's.

## The world and the system under test
- **Offices:** every usable changed office of the Wikidata snapshot in both halves (TM-1's "all" pool, 1,281) and 263 static offices; the never-streamed sets from the rest of the static offices.
- **Everything else is CCL-4's:** the same simulated sources and attackers in the same proportions, 40 registry errors believed in session 1 and corrected in session 4, 12 sessions with evaluations after sessions 4, 8 and 12, CCL-4's questions for each office, GSM8K and MMLU.
- **One thing scaled with the world:** the registry questions per session, 40 per 166 offices as in CCL-4 (372), as TM-1 scaled its budget.
- **Arms:** SYSTEM2 (CCL-4's learner, unchanged), NAIVE and the untouched model, by CCL-4's frozen GPU runner in Colab's layout; Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (revision `a09a35458c70…`), 16 bits, on Azure's serverless A100.

## Design
- **Worlds:** two worlds of the "all" pool, seeds 9724 and 9725 (the dev trial used 9793; the offices are the same in every X10 world, the seed draws the sources, attackers, errors, statics and anchors; declared).
- **Packs:** `PYTHONHASHSEED=0 python cl/x10.py pack 97XX runs/x10-main-7b/w97XX --prereg docs/X10-PREREG-2026-10-28.md`, run without Python's isolated mode so that the hash seed applies.
- **Machine:** one world per job on Azure's serverless A100 (`--hours 20`) or the lab's A100 VM (`docs/MACHINE-A100-VM-2026-10-28.md`), the same GPU model; each run's `aca.json` or `vm.json` records which.
