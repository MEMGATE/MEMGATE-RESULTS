# SL-2: evidence has a locus, again on fresh data with SL-1's code unchanged (a replication of SL-1)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any SL-2 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

SL-1 (Kiraṇa 6; Nyāya pakṣadharmatā) found two things:
- grouping cases by their real locus beat the same rule over random groups (+1.04 pp, WIN);
- withholding a general lesson until it is seen at the locus cost against the plain learner (−0.76).

Naming the locus as an ordinary feature helped (+0.72, descriptive). SL-2 asks whether these hold on three fresh datasets whose loci are of different kinds: patients, weather stations and regions. `shastra/sl1.py` is unchanged.

## 2. Arms and rules

These are SL-1's four arms and rules, unchanged:
- R-ORD;
- R-ORD+LOCUS (the locus as an ordinary feature);
- SAME-LOCUS: a lesson decides only if one of its supporting cases comes from the case's locus, and the fallback is the nearest case of the same locus;
- RANDOM-LOCUS: the same over random groups of the same sizes.

## 3. Data: three fresh UCI datasets with loci (the locus is the first feature)

| Dataset | UCI id | Cases | Locus | Label | Features |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Parkinson's telemonitoring | 189 | 5,875 | the patient (42) | `total_UPDRS` above the median | age, sex, test time, 16 voice measures |
| Seoul next-day temperature | 514 | ~7,600 | the weather station (25) | `Next_Tmax` above the median | present Tmax and Tmin, 14 LDAPS model outputs, latitude, longitude, elevation, slope, solar radiation, month |
| Algerian forest fires | 547 | 244 | the region (2) | `Classes` (fire / not fire) | temperature, humidity, wind, rain, FFMC, DMC, DC, ISI, BUI, FWI, month |

**Dropped:**
- Parkinson's: `motor_UPDRS`, a part of the label's scale.
- Seoul:
  - `Date` (its month is kept) and `Next_Tmin` (the other target);
  - the file's two summary rows, which have no station;
  - rows with no `Next_Tmax`.
- Algerian: `day` and `year`. Any row whose field count is wrong is dropped and counted.

**Encoding and orders.**
- Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule, and the month is a category.
- Each dataset runs in 10 orders (seeds 48000–48009), cut to 1,000 cases where larger.

**Dev.** There is no dev run, since the code is SL-1's, frozen. The loaders are checked by shape only, and no learner sees the data before the main run.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

These are as in SL-1, stratified by dataset.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary in SL-1)** | SAME-LOCUS − R-ORD |
| **H2 (SL-1's WIN; the one replicated here)** | SAME-LOCUS − RANDOM-LOCUS |
| H3 | SAME-LOCUS − R-ORD+LOCUS |
| Descriptive | R-ORD+LOCUS − R-ORD; RANDOM-LOCUS − R-ORD |

**INVALID if** any run is non-deterministic or crashes.
