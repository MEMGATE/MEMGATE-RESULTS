# Y-1: do the yuga proportions themselves matter, or only voting over seven neighbours?

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any Y-1 code, after the SR-7 result. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

In SR-7 the yuga-weighted fallback won (+1.94 pp): when no lesson decides, the 7 nearest past cases vote with weights 4 : 3 : 2 : 1 by age quarter (kṛta, tretā, dvāpara, kali). That arm changed two things at once, the vote over 7 neighbours and the age weighting. Y-1 separates them.

## 2. Arms

All arms are R-ORD; only the fallback differs.

| Arm | Fallback |
| --- | --- |
| R-ORD | 1-NN, as always |
| R-ORD+KNN7 | the 7 nearest past cases vote with **equal** weights |
| R-ORD+YUGA | the 7 nearest vote with weights 4 : 3 : 2 : 1, newest quarter first, exactly as in SR-7 |
| R-ORD+YUGA-REV | the 7 nearest vote with weights 1 : 2 : 3 : 4, oldest quarter first: the reverse ordering, as a control |

All other details are identical: ties between cases go to the more recent case, and ties between labels go to the nearest case's label.

## 3. Data

Eight UCI datasets, new to the lab (a ninth, UCI 497, failed to download and was dropped before any code). Each runs in 10 orders (seeds 25000–25009) and is cut to 1,000 cases. Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule. The datasets are chosen and hashed in `data/uci/SOURCE.md` (Y-1) before any Y-1 code.

**Synthetic:** the five SR families with seeds 25000–25019.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | R-ORD+YUGA − R-ORD+KNN7: do the yuga weights add anything beyond voting? |
| H2 | R-ORD+KNN7 − R-ORD: does voting over 7 neighbours help? |
| H3 | R-ORD+YUGA − R-ORD+YUGA-REV: does the direction of the ageing matter? |
| H4 | R-ORD+YUGA − R-ORD: replication of SR-7's H4 |
