# UNDO-1 spec (proposal): is the residue after rollback the lie, or just a familiar name?

**Status:** proposal, 20 October 2026, 21:10 IST. Not approved, not run. It needs GPU time on an open model.

## Why
The outside reviewers' remaining "must-have" is exact undo: after a caught source's teaching is rolled back, the model should behave like a model that never saw that source.

SE-4 found residue. On the 28 lies the engine actually taught and then removed:
- the rolled-back model preferred the lie over a never-taught name on **67.9%** of targets;
- the untouched base model preferred the same lie on **57.1%**;
- the mean gaps were +0.77 against +0.46 nats per token.

**There is a simpler explanation than a remembered lie.** Each lie is the name of a real holder of *another* office, and that name is trained for its own office in the clean stream the rollback replays. The rolled-back model therefore knows these names better than the base model does, whatever office they are asked about.

The right comparison is not the base model. It is a model trained on the same stream with the caught source's reports removed from the start: the never-exposed counterfactual.

## Design
- **Worlds:** SE-4's world (sleepers taught, then caught), 8 fresh seeds.
- **Model:** the same open 7B model and LoRA settings as SE-4.
- **Arms:**
  - **ROLLBACK:** SE-4's engine with rollback and replay, unchanged.
  - **CLEAN:** the same chain trained from the start on the same admitted stream minus every report from the sources that are later caught. This is the never-exposed counterfactual.
  - **LEDGER:** taught, then overwritten by the truth.
  - **FROZEN.**
- **Measures,** on the lies ROLLBACK actually taught and then removed, and on all sleeper targets:
  - the lie trace: share of targets where the lie's mean token log-probability beats a never-taught name of the same kind;
  - the mean gap in nats per token;
  - the same against a **familiar control:** a holder of another office, trained in the clean stream, never attached to this office.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after a dev world)
- **H1 (exact undo):** lie trace, ROLLBACK − CLEAN, is EQUIVALENT within ±5 pp: two one-sided 95% world-level intervals.
- **H2:** lie trace, LEDGER − ROLLBACK, is a WIN above +20 pp. Overwriting a lie with the truth is not undoing it.
- **Descriptive:**
  - the mean gap;
  - the familiar control;
  - cost, as the share of a full retrain.

## Keeping the method in-house
- Every gate and ledger decision is computed locally.
- Only (question, answer) training pairs and a generic LoRA training script go to the GPU service.
- No gate code, rules or thresholds leave this machine.

## Cost and time
- About 8 worlds × 4 chains of 7B LoRA training. SE-4 took about 40 tasks and $10 on Modal, so expect about $15–25.
- About one day including dev.
- **Needs the founder's approval** to use Modal, or an Azure GPU VM that the founder creates.
