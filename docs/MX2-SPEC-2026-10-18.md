# MX-2: a pariṣad that asks only under doubt and answers only when it is of one mind

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-2 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

Three results so far point to one system:

| Result | Experiment | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A three-member assembly of different kinds beats its lesson member | P-1, P-2, MX-1, PV-1 | four pools |
| The assembly that asks for labels only under doubt beats random asking at the same budget, and matches the full assembly | PV-1 H4 | +3.94 pp |
| The lesson learner's answers where a lesson decides are more accurate than random answers at the same coverage (kṣetrajña) | JK-1 H2 | +7.08 pp |

MX-2 combines them and asks two things:
1. Does the asking assembly replicate on a fifth fresh pool?
2. Does the *assembly* know where it knows?

For the second question, "knowing" is read as the members being of one mind, with all three agreeing. This is a natural reading of an assembly's certainty. It is not a claim about any single verse.

## 2. Arms

The assembly is PARISHAD-3*: R-ORD+ANU+AKH, naive Bayes and the decision tree. Its answer is their plurality. Doubt is PV-1's rule: the members disagree, or the lesson member doubts (JK-1's rule).

| Arm | Labels observed | Cases answered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FULL | all | all |
| SABHA-JIJNASA | only under doubt | all |
| SABHA-RANDOM-ASK | as many as SABHA-JIJNASA asked for, at random cases (`random.Random(seed + 777)`) | all |
| FULL-EKAMATA | all | only cases where all three members agree |
| GRAND | only under doubt, as SABHA-JIJNASA | only unanimous cases |

**Random abstention controls.** FULL and GRAND each get a control that answers as many cases as the arm did, chosen at random from the same run's scored cases (`random.Random(seed + 999)`).

- V2–V3, the segment's start and end instants;

- V26–V35, the ten pattern indicators, which encode the class itself.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before, stratified by dataset. A run with no unanimous case counts 0 for H2 or H3.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | SABHA-JIJNASA − SABHA-RANDOM-ASK (accuracy); replication of PV-1's H4 |
| H2 | FULL-EKAMATA − its random abstention control (selective accuracy at the same coverage) |
| H3 | GRAND − its random abstention control (selective accuracy at the same coverage) |
| Descriptive | SABHA-JIJNASA − FULL; labels asked; coverage of unanimity; GRAND − FULL-EKAMATA selective accuracy; the lesson member's own kṣetrajña for comparison |
