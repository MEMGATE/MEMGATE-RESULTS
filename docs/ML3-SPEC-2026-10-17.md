## 1. Source and question

**Source.** Manusmṛti 12.112: a Ṛgveda knower, a Yajurveda knower and a Sāmaveda knower form the assembly of three that settles a doubtful point. These are three *different* competences. See [P-1's spec](P1-SPEC-2026-10-17.md) for the source check.

**Question.** In ML-1 and ML-2, a language model that writes its own lessons beat retrieved experience in two preregistered runs. Does an assembly of three *different* models, each with its own lessons, decide better than a single model with lessons?

## 2. Design

**Models:** gpt-6-astra, gpt-6.1-sol and grok-4.7, in this fixed order. Claude Opus 5.5 is excluded, because it refused 7.8% of the ML-1 dev prompts (category "cyber").

**Worlds:** EXC, NOISE and the 100-case DRIFT, as in ML-2, with new seeds 13000–13005. That gives 18 worlds of 100 cases each.

**A world** is one prequential stream shared by all three models.

- **Experience:** at each case every model sees the same 8 nearest past cases, as in ML-1.
- **Lessons:** each model proposes lessons from its own view and keeps its own lesson store under ML-1's ORD management. Prompts, parsing and the proposal step are unchanged from ML-1 and ML-2.
- **Shared calls:** identical prompts share one call, as before.
- **Leakage check:** a case's label enters memory only after every model has predicted it.

**Arms.**

| Arm | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| EXP-m, ORD-m (for each model m) | that model without and with its lessons |
| **PARISHAD-ORD** | the plurality of the three ORD answers; a three-way tie goes to the first model in the fixed order |
| PARISHAD-EXP | the plurality of the three EXP answers |

## 3. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as in ML-1 and ML-2. The strata are the families, with 6 worlds each. The differences are per world.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | PARISHAD-ORD − the mean of the three ORD-m: is the assembly better than a typical member? |
| H2 | PARISHAD-ORD − PARISHAD-EXP: do lessons help an assembly? |
| H3 | ORD-grok − EXP-grok: do lessons replicate on a third model family? |
| Descriptive | PARISHAD-ORD − each ORD-m; the best single model in hindsight |

**INVALID if** any model fails or refuses on more than 2% of its calls, or more than 2% of answers are unparseable.

## Amendment before any dev run (17 October 2026, 20:00 IST)

The smoke run showed that grok-4.7 reasons at length. One measured proposal call took 116.8 s, with 2,097 reasoning tokens, and one prediction call about 20 s. A 100-case world therefore takes about 4 hours.

To keep the dev run to about an hour, it uses 30 cases per world instead of 100. **The main run is unchanged:** 18 worlds of 100 cases, run once. No model, prompt, arm or hypothesis changes.
