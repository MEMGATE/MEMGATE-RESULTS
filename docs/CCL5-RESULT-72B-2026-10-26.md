## What ran
SYSTEM3 (CCL-4's SYSTEM2 plus held-out self-tests, study of the graded probes and lessons) on Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct in 4 bits (NF4) on Colab G4 GPUs (NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, 96 GB), in CCL-4's main worlds 9702 and 9703 (166 real offices, 263 never-streamed offices, 40 registry errors believed in session 1 and corrected in session 4, simulated attackers), twelve sessions, evaluations after sessions 4, 8 and 12. Its comparisons are CCL-4's 72B runs of the same worlds on the same model and machine: the untouched model and NAIVE (REPLAY did not run on the 72B, so R1–R3 are not tested), as preregistered.
- World 9702 ran from 16:51 to 20:24 IST.
- World 9703 started at 16:52; its Colab VM was lost between 17:38 and 18:33 (after session 3's self-test; Colab dropped the session). The kernel cannot resume mid-run, so the world was started again from session 1 at 18:35 on a new G4, with the same payload and kernel, and ran to the end at 22:09. The lost attempt's partial output is kept in `runs/ccl5-main-72b-g4/w9703/kaggle-output-partial-lost-vm/` and is not used.

**Validity (as preregistered):** SYSTEM3 finished its twelve sessions in both worlds (exit code 0, the log's last line "done"); the removal ran in session 4; every probe in the payloads was asked at its session and logged (261 in world 9702, 249 in world 9703; session 1 has none by design, since it holds the starting state, and session 2 of world 9702 learned no change the probes cover); CCL-4's 72B baselines for both worlds are valid by CCL-4's rules (they are). Both worlds are pooled.

## Limits
- One model at this size and one domain (real changes of office and real code migrations), with simulated sources and attackers; two worlds.
- The 72B ran in 4 bits on a Colab GPU; REPLAY was not run on the 72B. The comparisons (the untouched model, NAIVE) come from CCL-4's runs of the same worlds, whose results were known when CCL-5 was frozen (declared in the preregistration).
- Ability 8 rests on few early probes (48 pooled, sessions 2–4), so its interval is wide (+12.79 to +42.88). Later probes are about other changes than earlier ones; the comparison is within kinds only. The first check on trained items (CCL-4's H13) still sits near its floor and shows no change.
- General ability and never-streamed offices equal the untouched model's by design: questions about anything not learned are answered by the untouched model.
- Not measured: safety behaviour (refusals) after learning, instruction-following after learning, a long horizon (twelve sessions over 34 months, about 1,200 new items learned a world), and the cost per session. `docs/CCL6-SPEC-DRAFT-2026-10-26.md` adds them.
- No outside party has run these tests.
