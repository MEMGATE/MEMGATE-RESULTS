## Design (as in the spec)
- **Questions:** SC-1's main half. These are 300 of RP-1's changed offices, two thirds of them look-alikes, asked in the phrasing no memory line uses.
- **Conditions:**
  - NONE;
  - SMALL: SC-1's memory, 1,278 lines;
  - FULL: SMALL's lines plus every other office with one holder on 18 October 2026, 11,611 lines;
  - HISTORY: FULL plus every past tenure as a dated line, 31,029 lines;
  - KEYED: the current-value lines of the offices the entity link names;
  - EXACT: the office's own line only, as when the application knows which office is meant.
- **Retrieval:** TF-IDF top 5.
- **Models:** gpt-4.1-base (temperature 0), llama-4-maverick, grok-4.7 and gpt-6.1-sol.
- **Scoring:** right, collision and stale, as SC-1.

## Hypotheses
Office-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345.

| Test | Measure | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | right, FULL − SMALL | **NON-INFERIOR** if the lower bound is above −2 pp |
| H2 | right, HISTORY − FULL | NON-INFERIOR if the lower bound is above −2 pp |
| H3 | right, KEYED − HISTORY | **WIN** if the interval lies wholly above +1 pp and every model is above 0 |

**Validity:** a model with more than 5% of its questions unanswered is left out. Fewer than three usable models make the run INVALID.

## Changes made in dev, before this freeze
- **The first dev run's FULL memory read the current holder from the snapshot's tenures.** For 14 of RP-1's offices with overlapping tenures, it found none, so their own line was missing. That made FULL look about 2 pp worse than SMALL.
  - FULL now holds SMALL's lines word for word plus the other offices' lines.
  - HISTORY drops past-tenure lines that name an office's current holder.
- **EXACT was added after the first dev run,** to separate entity-linking errors from the models' own errors.
- **The non-inferiority margin** was briefly set to −5 pp after the first dev run. It is back at the lab's usual −2 pp.
