# SR-7: the remaining epistemic gaps. The pramāṇas together, three-level sublation, and the yuga ages.

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any SR-7 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Why

The coverage register names three families that have been tested only piecewise:

1. **The pramāṇas as one system.** Each has been tested on its own:
   - pratyakṣa over anumāna (SR-3 BAD);
   - upamāna (A-1);
   - śabda (ML-1, ML-2);
   - arthāpatti (SR-4);
   - anupalabdhi (SR-3, H-1).

   They have never been tested together, either in the Nyāya order of priority or as an assembly.
2. **Advaita's three-level sublation** (prātibhāsika, vyāvahārika, pāramārthika) as a revision rule.
3. **The Purāṇic yuga proportions (4 : 3 : 2 : 1)** as an ageing weight.

## 2. Mechanisms

**PRAMANA-CHAIN (Nyāya priority).** For each case, the first means of knowledge that answers decides:

1. pratyakṣa: an identical past case;
2. anumāna: a lesson;
3. arthāpatti: the single label left after exclusion (SR-4 ART);
4. upamāna: the nearest past case.

This is R-ORD with SR-3's BAD and SR-4's ART turned on.

**PRAMANA-SABHA.** The four means vote as an assembly. A means that has no answer abstains: there is no identical past case, no lesson decides, or no single label is left. The plurality of the answers decides. A tie goes to the earlier means in the Nyāya order.

**BADHA-3 (three-level sublation).**

- **Levels.** A validated lesson is *vyāvahārika* (empirical). Once it has at least 10 supports and has met no counterexample in its last 50 cases, it is *pāramārthika*: firmly established, the highest level used here. An unvalidated candidate is *prātibhāsika*: apparent.
- **The rule.** A cognition can be sublated only by one of at least equal standing. A single counterexample revises a prātibhāsika or vyāvahārika lesson as ordinary. A pāramārthika lesson is revised only after a **second** counterexample: the first is held as provisional (*prātibhāsika*) until it is repeated.

**YUGA.** When no lesson decides, the fallback is a vote of the 7 nearest past cases. Each vote is weighted by the age of the case:

| Age of the case | Yuga | Weight |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| newest quarter of the past | kṛta | 4 |
| next quarter | tretā | 3 |
| next quarter | dvāpara | 2 |
| oldest quarter | kali | 1 |

Ties go to the nearest case.

## 5. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | PRAMANA-SABHA − R-ORD |
| H2 | PRAMANA-CHAIN − R-ORD |
