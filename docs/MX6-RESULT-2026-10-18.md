## What this means

**The network had nothing to agree on.**
- On this plant, neither the plain network nor the supervisor answered more than 1.6% of cases alone. Every arm asked a person about almost everything.
- The decay grade shows as a small shift in the sensor readings within each speed level, while speed moves every reading a lot.
- With readings cut into quartiles, a spy that sees only one group of sensors cannot hold a well-supported lesson. All three were guessing nearly always, so the guessing clause blocked any credence.
- With nothing to agree on, dismissal also misfired. The failed group was found in only 5 of 20 runs.

**This is not evidence against the recipe; it is its precondition.** The supervisor works when each source can, on its own, often decide the case:
- the hydraulic rig (MX-5): cooler 78% answered alone;
- Multiple Features and MHEALTH (AR-1).

It adds nothing where no single source can. Here it failed safe: almost no wrong answers given alone (≤ 0.5%), at the price of asking about everything.

**For a working system.** Check first that each camera or sensor can recognise the event on its own in a good share of cases. Only then is the agreement of several a strong signal, and a failing one detectable. Where an event shows only in a combination of sources, the sources must be fused within one spy first. This test cannot tell whether finer binning per spy would have rescued the plant; that would be a new experiment.
