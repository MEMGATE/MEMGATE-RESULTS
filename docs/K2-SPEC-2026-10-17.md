# K-2: a preregistered kolam symmetry test on a fresh board dataset (jungle chess)

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any K-2 code. It follows the exploratory [K-1](K1-EXPLORATORY-2026-10-17.md), which used exposed boards only. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question and mapping

A kolam keeps its form under the dot grid's reflections and rotations. The learning reading: when a board position is observed, the learner also takes in its symmetric image, since the outcome cannot depend on the board's orientation.

**Data.** OpenML 41027, "jungle_chess_2pcs_raw_endgame_complete": all two-piece Jungle (Dou Shou Qi) endgames.

- 44,819 cases.
- Features: the strength, file (0–6) and rank (0–8) of the white piece and of the black piece.
- Classes: white wins, black wins, draw.

**Symmetry.** The Jungle board is symmetric left to right: the dens, traps and both rivers mirror about the central file 3. So the outcome is unchanged when every file f becomes 6 − f. Rotations are not symmetries, because the two sides' dens are at opposite ends.

The dataset has not been used in this lab. K-1's boards were tic-tac-toe and Connect-4.

## 4. Predictions before any K-2 code

Both hypotheses are expected to be positive, with H2 larger, as in K-1 (+3.5 and +9.1 pp on exposed boards). One mirror gives at most twice the data, not the eightfold of tic-tac-toe, so the gains should be smaller.
