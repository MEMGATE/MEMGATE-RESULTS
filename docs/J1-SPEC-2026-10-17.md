# J-1: Jyotiṣa *pañcadhā maitrī* (fivefold friendship) as the rule for trusting experts

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any J-1 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Source and mapping

**Source.** Codex's Bṛhat Jātaka study (`docs/SANATANA-JYOTISA-RELATIONS-2026-10-17.md` on `codex/continual-learning-foundation-20261016`) records directed natural friendship and temporary friendship between the grahas as separate relation types.

In the standard Jyotiṣa account, the two combine into five grades. *Naisargika* friendship is fixed by nature, and *tātkālika* friendship depends on present positions.

| Natural | Temporary friend | Temporary enemy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friend | great friend (adhimitra) | neutral |
| Neutral | friend | enemy |
| Enemy | neutral | great enemy (adhiśatru) |

**Mapping (mine).** Several expert learners predict every case, and a chooser must decide whose prediction to follow. "By nature" is read as an expert's long-run record, and "by present position" as its recent record.

## 2. Experts and choosers

**Experts.** Each expert sees every case: it predicts first, then observes. They are kept in a fixed order:

1. EXT-DT
2. EXT-NB
3. EXT-1NN
4. R-ORD
5. EXT-LAST, which predicts the previous label

**Choosers.** Each one picks a single expert's prediction for each case, using only the experts' past correctness. The first case goes to the first expert.

| Chooser | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| GLOBAL ("follow the leader") | the highest cumulative accuracy |
| RECENT | the highest accuracy over the last 20 cases |
| MAJORITY | the plurality of the five predictions |
| **MAITRI** | the highest compound grade (below) |

- **Ties.** GLOBAL and MAJORITY break ties by the fixed expert order. RECENT breaks ties by cumulative accuracy, then by the fixed order.
- **MAITRI's natural standing:** +1 (friend) for the expert ranked first by cumulative accuracy, −1 (enemy) for the one ranked last, and 0 (neutral) otherwise. Ranks are broken by the fixed order.
- **MAITRI's temporary standing:** +1 if the expert's accuracy over the last 20 cases is at least the median of the five, otherwise −1.
- **MAITRI's grade:** the sum of the two, from −2 to +2. The highest grade wins, with ties broken by cumulative accuracy, then by the fixed order.
- **Descriptive only: BEST-SINGLE.** The expert with the best accuracy over the whole run, chosen in hindsight. It is not a valid online method.

## 3. Data

The data are the held-out pool shared with SR-5 (see `docs/SR5-SPEC-2026-10-17.md` §4):

- nine shuffled UCI datasets, each in orders 15000–15009 and cut to 1,000 cases;
- three real drift streams (Elec2, airlines, gas sensor drift), each as 10 windows of 1,000 consecutive cases;
- the five synthetic SR families with seeds 15000–15019.

Both preregistrations are frozen before either main run. Dev uses only exposed data:

- the 29 earlier real datasets;
- the nine SR-4 datasets, now exposed;
- earlier synthetic seeds.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are those of SR-1 to SR-5, stratified by source (12 strata).

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | MAITRI − GLOBAL on all 12 held-out sources |
| H2 | MAITRI − RECENT |
| H3 | MAITRI − MAJORITY |
| Secondary | The same on the streams alone and on the shuffled datasets alone |
| Descriptive | MAITRI − BEST-SINGLE; each chooser − R-ORD |

## 5. Predictions before any J-1 code

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | ≈ 0 on shuffled data; positive on the streams | The temporary standing lets MAITRI switch to the recently better expert when the data change, for example to EXT-LAST on Elec2. |
| H2 | positive on shuffled data | RECENT over-switches on noise. |
| H3 | positive | A plurality of weak experts can outvote the right one. |
