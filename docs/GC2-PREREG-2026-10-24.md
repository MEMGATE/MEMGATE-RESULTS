## Dev (done; Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 100 questions of GSM8K's train split, both arms, Kaggle's two T4s)
- **Dev 1** (`runs/gc2-dev-qwen7b-20261024`, at most 320 new tokens): FROZEN 89%, READER 91%. Every reply had a number, but some were cut mid-reasoning, more often the untouched model's: 19 and 10 replies had no "Answer:" line. Cut replies would favour the arm that writes less.
- **The one change after dev 1:** at most 512 new tokens instead of 320, as the spec allows for dev. Recorded in LOG.md (12:14) before dev 2.
- **Dev 2** (`runs/gc2-dev2-qwen7b-20261024`, at most 512 new tokens): FROZEN 95%, READER 94% (READER − FROZEN −1.0 points, 95% interval −4.0 to +2.0). 5 and 2 replies had no "Answer:" line; 4 and 2 of them ended with the answer boxed and were read right from their last number. Asking took 279 and 286 seconds per 100 questions; the reader trained in 543 seconds.
- Nothing else was changed after dev: not the prompt, the answer rule, the models, the training or the questions.

## Main
- **Models:** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (`a09a35458c702b33eeacc393d103063234e8bc28`), OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct (`470b1fba1ae01581f270116362ee4aa1b97f4c84`), Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B-v1 (`54641c1611fcff44fa4865626462445e0a153fc7`), each in its own Kaggle run (`runs/gc2-main-<family>-20261024`), at most two at a time.
- **Arms:** FROZEN (untouched) and READER (TW-2's reader, trained again in the same run, exactly as in TW-2's main run and GC-1).
- **Questions:** the whole GSM8K test split (`openai/gsm8k`, config `main`), 1,319 questions, zero-shot through each model's chat template (Nemotron-Nano with "detailed thinking off"). System message: "Solve the math word problem. Reason briefly, then give the final answer as a number on the last line, in the form 'Answer: N'." Greedy, at most 512 new tokens, 16 questions at a time.
- **Scoring:** the number after the last "Answer:" in the reply; if there is none, the last number in the reply; commas and a leading "$" removed. Right if it equals the number after "####" in GSM8K's answer. A reply with no number is wrong.
