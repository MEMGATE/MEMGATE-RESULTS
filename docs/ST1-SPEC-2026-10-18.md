# ST-1: saṃskāra (SK-1) and pañcadhā maitrī (JY-2) on fresh real streams

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any ST-1 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

Two mechanisms were tested in their main runs on synthetic worlds only. In dev, both showed gains on the exposed real streams:

| Mechanism | Main run, synthetic | Dev, exposed real streams |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SK-1: saṃskāra** (Yoga Sūtra 4.8–4.9). Refuted lessons stay latent and return after confirmations. | **+2.22 WIN** on RECUR, and R = 3 beat R = 2 | +0.81 (with R = 2) |
| **JY-2: pañcadhā maitrī** (Bṛhat Jātaka II.15–18). Pariṣad votes weighted by five grades of long-run × recent standing. | +0.04, NO WIN | +1.09 |

Real streams in their natural order are where conditions recur and members' standing changes. ST-1 tests both mechanisms, unchanged, on five fresh real streams.

## 2. Arms

| Arm | Content |
| --- | --- |

| **R-ORD+SAMSKARA-R3** | SK-1's better variant (R = 3), unchanged |
| PLURALITY | PARISHAD-3* by plurality (as JY-2) |

## 3. Data: five fresh real streams in their natural (time) order

All come from the UCI Machine Learning Repository and are new to the lab. Each is binarized at the median of its target over the whole stream, as OpenML binarizes regression sets.

| Stream | UCI id | Cases | The label is above the median of | Features |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Bike sharing, hourly (Washington, 2011–2012) | 275 | 17,379 | rentals `cnt` | season, year, month, hour, holiday, weekday, working day, weather, temperature, felt temperature, humidity, wind |
| Metro interstate traffic (Minneapolis, 2012–2018) | 492 | 48,204 | `traffic_volume` | holiday, temperature, rain, snow, clouds, weather (main and description), hour, weekday, month |
| Tetouan city power (2017, 10-minute) | 849 | 52,416 | Zone 1 consumption | temperature, humidity, wind, general and diffuse flows, hour, weekday |
| Seoul bike sharing (2017–2018, hourly) | 560 | 8,760 | rented bikes | hour, temperature, humidity, wind, visibility, dew point, solar radiation, rainfall, snowfall, season, holiday, functioning day, weekday |
| Appliances energy (2016, 10-minute) | 374 | 19,735 | appliance energy | lights, nine rooms' temperature and humidity, outdoor weather (six readings), hour |

**Dropped:**
- bike: `instant` (a row index), `dteday` (the date; its parts are kept), and `casual` and `registered` (they sum to the target);
- Tetouan: zones 2 and 3 (other zones' loads at the same moment);
- appliances: `rv1` and `rv2` (random variables the authors added as noise).

**Encoding.**
- Calendar fields (hour, weekday, month, season, year) are categories.
- Other numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule over the whole stream.

**Windows.** Each stream runs as 10 windows of 1,000 consecutive cases, as in SR-5.

**Dev:** the eleven exposed streams, 10 windows each.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before, stratified by stream. The two hypotheses are each primary, with separate verdicts. They test two separate mechanisms and share only the data.

| Hypothesis | Comparison (prequential accuracy) |
| --- | --- |
| **H1** | R-ORD+SAMSKARA-R3 − R-ORD |
| **H2** | MAITRI − PLURALITY |
| Descriptive | per stream; SAMSKARA's re-manifestations; MAITRI's share of changed answers |

**INVALID if:**
- any run is non-deterministic or crashes;
- SAMSKARA never re-manifests;
- MAITRI never differs from PLURALITY.
