## Hypotheses and decision rules (unchanged from the spec)

The bootstrap runs over 8 worlds, with 10,000 resamples and seed 12345.

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1** | Poison: GATED − RAW (nano, after session 12) | WIN if ≤ −1 pp with the CI's upper bound below 0 |
| **H2** | Truth: GATED − RAW (nano, after session 12) | WIN if ≥ +1 pp with the CI's lower bound above 0 |
| **H3** | Truth with the gate: nano − sol (after session 12) | NON-INFERIOR if the CI's lower bound is above −2 pp |
| H4 | Truth and poison with raw memory: nano − sol (after sessions 4 and 12) | descriptive |

**INVALID if:**
- more than 1% of calls fail after retries;
- GATED truth is below 90%.

**Dev a** was invalid, because its failed calls were scored as wrong answers.

**What dev b showed:**
- **Reading every report did not protect the small model.** It was poisoned more than with the latest report alone (27.5% against 17.5%). It also named a past holder on 10.3% of changed facts.
- **The comparison with sol.** RM-1's `gpt-6.1-sol`, on other worlds, reached 1.2% poison from raw memory after session 12.
