## What this means

**The category-shaped (Sāṃkhya/Vaiśeṣika) grouping does not transfer from construction to real domains.**

- In O-1 the concepts were *defined* over the groups, so a correct grouping helped by +11 pp.
- Here the groups come from each dataset's own documentation, and the concepts were not built around them. A per-group count of marked values gives no gain over flat features.
- It gives no gain over random groups of the same sizes either.
- The only consistent small gain was naive Bayes on WDBC (+0.6 pp), where the "worst" block summarises severity.
- For ID3 the extra count features cost about 0.5 pp, through over-splitting.

**Combined with O-1:** a good category structure is valuable only when the target concept actually follows it. A documented ontology of the *inputs* does not guarantee that.
