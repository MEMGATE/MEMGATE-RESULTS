## Rules fixed in the code

**The alarm.**
- From case 1, while three spies are trusted and none reports nothing, it records whether all three reports differ.
- PK-1's `unwell` is applied to that record: W = 30, 3 SE, floor 0.05, at least 60 records.
- Once it fires, no later case is answered alone, and nothing reverses it.

**H1 (safety).** SAFE if the upper bound of the 95% CI of SUPERVISOR+ALARM's wrong answers alone after case 240 in TWO is below 0.02 per case.

**H2 and H3 (cost).** NO HARM if the lower bound of the 95% CI of SUPERVISOR+ALARM − SUPERVISOR, net credence, is above −0.01, in SOUND and in ONE respectively.

**INVALID conditions:**
- non-determinism;
- a crashed run;
- SUPERVISOR's sound coverage below 5% (not testable).
