# AU-1 result: at the same registry budget, the gate learns 11 points more than the registry alone, and lets far fewer attacker values into a training stream than majority vote or truth discovery; the threshold liars and trust farmers got nothing through

20 October 2026. One main run, in [`runs/au1-main-20261020.jsonl`](../runs/au1-main-20261020.jsonl), with the report in [`runs/au1-main-20261020-report.json`](../runs/au1-main-20261020-report.json).

**Setup.**
- Code `488e59de…`, as frozen in the [preregistration](AU1-PREREG-2026-10-20.md) at commit `9aec84f3`.
- Model-free, 20 fresh worlds (8300–8319), 2 minutes 50 seconds on a CPU.
- Each world is TM-1's: 1,341 real offices, 12 sessions, the registry, four honest sources and all of TM-1's attackers. Three attackers that adapt to the gate's known rules are added:
  - a threshold-liar pair that lies under the dismissal rate;
  - trust farmers who earn a track record and then lie;
  - budget exhausters who spend the registry's questions on stale values.
- Every registry-using arm had the same 303 questions per session.

**Validity.** No INVALID condition held. Every world ran; every registry-using arm asked 3,610–3,636 questions per world, against a budget of 3,636.

**Who was dismissed.**
- Both gates dismissed the stealthy poisoner, both colluders, both sleepers, both threshold liars and both trust farmers, in all 20 worlds.
- The gate also dismissed 492 sybil accounts. GATE-v2 dismissed none, and no sybil value entered either gate's memory.
- No arm dismissed an honest source.
- The budget exhausters, who only repeated past holders, were never dismissed by either gate. This is the intended behaviour: lag is not lying.

## What this means

**The gate is not the answer key.**
- With the registry's same 303 questions per session, using the registry alone covers 66.2% of current holders on average. The gate covers 77.5%: it learns from sources that have earned a track record what the registry has not said yet.
- It reaches 88.2% at the end, against 81.4%, and holds 0 attacker values at the end.

**Against standard ways of combining sources, at the same budget:**
- **Attackers.** Majority vote and truth discovery also end with almost no attacker values. But along the way they hold far more: 8.3% and 3.0% of offices at some session, against 0.6% for the gate. In a learner that trains on each session's memory, that is what gets into the weights.
- **Coverage.** Truth discovery covers slightly more on average: the gate is 0.96 pp lower [−1.19, −0.73], lower in all 20 worlds. Truth discovery ends 6.5 pp lower, and holds 67 more past holders at the end.

**Adaptive attackers.**
- **Threshold liars and trust farmers** got no value into either gate's memory at the end of any session: not the liars under the 20% dismissal rate, nor the farmers with an earned record.
- **Budget exhausters** name only real past holders, so their values cannot be told apart from lag. Neither gate ever moved an office to the past holder an exhauster had just named; naive moved 1,508 times and majority vote 40 times. Like every method, both gates still hold some past holders while catching up with changes: 72–194 per session for the gate, against 104–214 for registry-only, which never listens to the exhausters.
- The threshold liars and the farmers are caught by the gate's exposure rule. Once the registry overturns a value admitted on their testimony, they are dismissed whatever their error rate.
- The only attacker values either gate ever held came from sleepers, before the sleepers were caught. Those are what the ledger's rollback removes from the weights (SE-4).

**GATE-v2.** The staleness rule, found by a model-free audit and then tested here as a preregistered hypothesis, adds 4.45 pp of average coverage and 9 pp at the end, cuts past holders held at the end from 151 to 37, and changes nothing on attacker values.

## What this does not show
- **It is model-free.** It counts what each rule would put into memory or a training stream, not what a model then answers. TM-1, HH-1 and FT-2 measure the model.
- **The registry is the truth by construction.** In TM-1's registry-noise audit, a registry wrong 1% of the time let 4 of 1,341 wrong values through.
- **The sources and attackers are simulated;** only the offices and their timelines are real. RV-2 replays real editors.
- **The adaptive attackers are three designs** built against the gate's documented rules. They are not the strongest possible attack. An attacker who can win the registry's own answers is outside this test.
