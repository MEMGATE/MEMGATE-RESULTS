# ML-5: model-written lessons on real data, and the mixed pariṣad on real data

**Status:** design, written on 17 October 2026 before any ML-5 code.

## 1. Question

Earlier runs left two questions open:

- Model-written lessons beat model experience in ML-1 (+3.84 pp) and ML-2 (+2.58 pp), but only on synthetic worlds. **Does the lesson gain hold on real data?**
- ML-4 put a model into the pariṣad, again on synthetic worlds. **Does a model-mixed pariṣad help on real data?**

## 2. Encoding

The prompts must not leak meaning, as in ML-1, where feature and value names are meaningless.

- **Features:** f0 to f(k−1).
- **Values:** each feature's distinct values, after the H-1 binning, are sorted and named v0, v1 and so on. A missing value gets its own symbol, after the observed ones.
- **Labels:** the sorted labels are named L1 to Lk.
- **Prompts:** ML-1's prompts, with the feature count stated correctly ("f0 to f(k−1)").
- **Proposals and lesson management:** ML-2's ORD arm (plain neighbours).

## 3. Data

Five UCI datasets, new to the lab.

| Dataset | UCI id | Cases | Features | Classes | Note |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Autism screening, adults | 426 | 704 | 18 | 2 | `result` (the sum of A1–A10, which defines the label) and the constant `age_desc` are dropped |
| Autism screening, children | 419 | 292 | 18 | 2 | the same |
| Autism screening, adolescents | 420 | 104 | 18 | 2 | the same |
| Cervical cancer risk factors | 383 | 858 | 32 | 2 | the label is `Biopsy`; the other three diagnostic targets are dropped |
| Breast cancer Wisconsin, prognostic | 16 | 198 | 32 | 2 | the label is the outcome; the id is dropped, and so is `time`, which is defined by the outcome |

**Worlds.** Each dataset runs in 4 orders (seeds 24000–24003), cut to 100 cases. That gives 20 worlds. A world is one prequential stream shared by all members.

**Dev.** Two exposed datasets with a similar small, mixed shape: post-operative and Haberman, in order 2000.

## 5. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before. The strata are dataset × model for H1, and datasets otherwise. The differences are per world.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | ORD − EXP, pooled over both models: does the lesson gain hold on real data? |
| H2 | PARISHAD-MIX − PARISHAD-OFF |
| H3 | PARISHAD-MIX − ORD-sol |
| H4 | ORD-sol − R-ORD |

**INVALID if** either model fails or refuses on more than 2% of its calls, or more than 2% of model answers are unparseable.
