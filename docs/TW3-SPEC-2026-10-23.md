# TW-3 spec: does the trained skill of reading a dated memory line carry to other kinds of office, and to other wording?

**Status:** spec, written 23 October 2026, about 04:15 IST, before any TW-3 question. Code: `cl/tw3.py`.

## Why
TW-2 trained three open 7–8B models to read the layer's dated memory line on other offices. On new offices they answered both dates right for 98.3–100%. Its limits:
- the training and test offices were the same kinds of office;
- the line was always in the layer's own wording.

A skill worth keeping in the weights should carry beyond its training format. TW-3 tests two kinds of transfer: to kinds of office never trained on, and to the same facts worded differently.

## Arms
| Arm | Training |
| --- | --- |
| FROZEN | none: the untouched model |
| READER | TW-2's rows and settings, trained only on RP-1's head-coach offices: 151 offices, 453 rows, 4 epochs, for about TW-2's number of optimizer steps |

## Questions
Both arms are asked TQ-1's offices (dev 50, main 300) about TQ-1's BEFORE and AFTER dates under five memory conditions:
- NONE: no memory;
- GATED and GATED+: the layer's two lines in TQ-1's words, the training wording;
- NOTE and NOTE+: the same facts worded as a short note that no training row uses:

```
Notes from the records, as of today:
PLACE, ROLE: X since DAY MONTH YEAR; before DAY MONTH YEAR, Z.
```

NOTE gives only `PLACE, ROLE: X.`

**Office kinds:**
- the trained kind: head coach. Main has 143 offices.
- held-out kinds: head of government, chair, CEO, head of state and director. Main has 157 offices; none was trained.

Both arms also get RP-3's locality questions, with no memory. Scoring uses TQ-1's scorer; BOTH as in TW-1 and TW-2.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev)
READER − FROZEN, an office-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345.

| Test | Measure | Offices | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | BOTH right under NOTE+ (the new wording) | held-out kinds | WIN: interval above +1 pp |
| H2 | BOTH right under GATED+ (the training wording) | held-out kinds | WIN |
| H3 | locality right, no memory | locality offices | NON-INFERIOR: lower bound above −5 pp |

**Descriptive:**
- both wordings on the trained kind;
- the current-value lines (NOTE, GATED) after the change;
- no memory, which checks the adapter learned no test office;
- NOTE+ before and after, separately.

## Models
The three families of TW-1 and TW-2: OLMo-2-7B (primary), Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B, on Kaggle's two T4s.

## Limits
- **Still office holders:** a held-out kind of office is a new role, not a new domain.
- **The new wording is one note format.**
- **The training wording is the layer's,** which the layer itself writes. Transfer matters when memory comes from elsewhere.
