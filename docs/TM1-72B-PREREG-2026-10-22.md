## Hypotheses and decision rules (TM-1's, unchanged)
Each is ENGINE − NAIVE over the two worlds, after session 12, with a bootstrap of 10,000 resamples, seed 12345.

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1** | poison | WIN if it falls by at least 1 pp and the CI's upper bound is below 0 |
| **H2** | locality | WIN if it rises by at least 1 pp and the CI's lower bound is above 0 |
| **H3** | swarm-lie trace | the H1 rule |
| **H4** | current | NON-INFERIOR if the CI's lower bound is above −5 pp |

**INVALID if:**
- a task fails (after its one retry);
- FROZEN current is above 10%;
- NAIVE current is below 30%.

## Addendum, 24 October 2026, 13:37 IST: the 13:04 main run failed while loading the model; it is INVALID and will be run again
- **What happened:** four of the six tasks failed after their one retry (Modal RemoteError): world 8000's FROZEN and NAIVE, world 8001's NAIVE and ENGINE. The logs show the failures while the 72B model was being loaded from the workspace volume or just after:
  - "Some parameters are on the meta device because they were offloaded to the cpu", then, at the ENGINE arm's first snapshot of its trainable weights, "Cannot copy out of meta tensor; no data!";
  - safetensors "could not determine the shape of object type 'torch.storage.UntypedStorage'" while reading a weights file;
  - Modal heartbeat timeouts of over two minutes during loading.
  - World 8001's FROZEN task finished; world 8000's ENGINE task is still running. Neither is used for the verdicts.
- **As the rules say,** the run is INVALID. Its folder is kept, renamed `runs/tm1-72b-main-20261024-failed` once its last task returns.
- **The likely cause:** six containers loading 135 GB each from one volume at once, each with Modal's default CPU share. Where the GPUs looked short of memory, part of the model was put on the CPU, which TM-1's training cannot use.
- **The fix, before the run is started again from all six tasks:**
  - eight CPUs per task, and at most three tasks at a time;
  - `cl/load72b.py` sets each GPU's memory limit from its free memory, stops the task at once if any weight is left off the GPUs or the container has fewer GPUs than asked, and logs the memory it found.
  - The hypotheses, rules, worlds, arms, training settings and predictions are unchanged. The new hash is recorded in the next addendum before the run starts.
