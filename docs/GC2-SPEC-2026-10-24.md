# GC-2 spec: multi-step math (GSM8K) after the skill of reading dated memory lines is trained into the weights

**Written:** 24 October 2026, 11:47 IST, before any GC-2 code, data or answer.

## Why
GC-1 measured general knowledge on MMLU after TW-2's reading skill was trained into three open 7–8B models: two of the three stayed non-inferior at −2 points (Qwen2.5-7B −0.79, OLMo-2-7B +2.46) and Nemotron-Nano-8B lost 2.81 points (not shown). Its letter answers test knowledge, not reasoning written out step by step, and the brief and report say "General capability was measured on one benchmark (MMLU)". GC-2 adds a second, different kind of benchmark: grade-school math word problems answered with written reasoning (GSM8K).

## What
- **Models:** TW-2's three families at TW-2's revisions: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (`a09a3545…`), OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct (`470b1fba…`), Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B-v1 (`54641c16…`).
- **Arms:** FROZEN, the untouched model; READER, one LoRA adapter trained exactly as in TW-2's main run and GC-1 (`cl/tw2.py`'s training rows, GPU script and settings, unchanged), on Kaggle's two T4s. The reader is trained again in each run.
- **Questions:** GSM8K (`openai/gsm8k`, config `main`, MIT licence). Main: the whole test split, 1,319 questions. Dev: 100 questions of the train split chosen with a fixed seed, so no main question is seen before the main run.
- **Asking:** zero-shot, through each model's chat template (Nemotron-Nano with "detailed thinking off", as in TW-1 to TW-4 and GC-1). System message: "Solve the math word problem. Reason briefly, then give the final answer as a number on the last line, in the form 'Answer: N'." User turn: the question. Greedy, at most 320 new tokens.
- **Scoring:** the number after the last "Answer:" in the reply; if there is none, the last number in the reply. Commas and a leading "$" are removed. It is right if it equals the number after "####" in GSM8K's answer. A reply with no number is wrong.
- **Measure:** accuracy per arm; READER − FROZEN, paired by question, with a bootstrap 95% interval (10,000 resamples, fixed seed).
- **Descriptive:** replies that reach the token limit, and replies with no number, per arm.

## Hypothesis (to be frozen in the preregistration, after dev)
- **H1, per family:** READER is non-inferior to FROZEN at −2.0 points: the lower end of the 95% interval of READER − FROZEN is above −2.0.

## Dev
One family (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), the 100 dev questions, both arms. It checks that answers are parsed, how many replies reach the token limit, and how long a run takes. Dev may change the prompt, the answer rule, the token limit and the number of questions asked at once; it may not change the models, the training or the main questions.

## Limits, stated in advance
- Two benchmarks in all (with GC-1), zero-shot, small open models; the reader's training only. Facts trained into the weights (TM-1's route) are not covered.
- Kaggle's T4s train in float16 with a gradient scaler, as TW-2 did; each training of the reader differs a little from TW-2's.
