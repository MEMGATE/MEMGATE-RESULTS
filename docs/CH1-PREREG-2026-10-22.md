## 1. Question
Some questions go through a changed fact to a fact about the new holder. An example is "In which year was the current head coach of C.D. Guadalajara born?"

**Weights.** Edits put into a model's weights are known to fail on such questions. The new value is stated when asked directly, but questions that go through it still use the old one.

**Memory.** CH-1 asks whether the layer's memory composes with what a model already knows, and whether a new fact trained into the weights does.

## 2. Items
- **Offices:** RP-1's 300 main offices, real changes from 2024–26 in TM world 8000.
- **Facts about the new and the previous holder:** from Wikidata, read on 22 October 2026 (`data/ch1/persons-20261022.json`):
  - date of birth (P569);
  - country of citizenship (P27), without former states.
- **When an office gives an item:**
  - BIRTHYEAR, when both holders' birth years are known and differ;
  - CITIZEN, when both citizenships are known and share no country, unless the office's own name names one of the countries compared (a national team).
- **Main has 307 items:** 246 BIRTHYEAR and 61 CITIZEN; 157 on RP-3's half A and 150 on half B.

## 3. Probes and routes
**Probes.** Every probe is dated "Today is 2026-10-19."

| Probe | Instruction | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CHAIN | Answer with the year (country) only. | In which year was the current ROLE of PLACE born? / Of which country is the current ROLE of PLACE a citizen? |
| STEPS | Answer in at most two sentences: first name the person, then give the year (country). | the same |
| KNOWN | Answer with the year (country) only. | NEW is the ROLE of PLACE. In which year was NEW born? |
| KNOWNX | the same | OLD was the ROLE of PLACE before NEW. In which year was OLD born? |

**Memory route.** RP-1's six models: gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-6-astra, grok-4.7, claude-opus-5-5, gpt-4.1-base and llama-4-maverick, asked as in RP-1.
- CHAIN and STEPS are asked under RP-1's memory conditions, checked word for word against RP-1's code for every item:
  - NONE;
  - GATED: the verified value;
  - GATED+: the layer's block, with the value, the change date and the previous value.
- KNOWN and KNOWNX are asked without memory.

**Weights route.** RP-3's main fine-tuned models, trained on these offices' new holders:
- **Families:** gpt-4.1-mini, gpt-4.1-nano, Llama-3.3-70B and Qwen3-32B.
- **Fine-tuned models:** DIRECT, FOLLOW and MORE, each on the resource that trained it. Each is asked CHAIN, STEPS and KNOWN without memory. Temperature is 0 and answers are capped at 150 tokens, as in RP-3.
- **The family's untouched model,** deployed for CH-1: CHAIN and STEPS under NONE and GATED+, plus KNOWN and KNOWNX.
- **Left out:**
  - GPT-4.1, whose RP-3 main models are still training; it may be added later as a descriptive addendum.
  - Ministral-3B, whose fine-tuning taught nothing in RP-3.
  - gpt-oss-120b, which cannot be served.

## 4. Scoring
- **BIRTHYEAR:** the four-digit years in the answer.
- **CITIZEN:** each country's English label, aliases and demonyms, longest first and without overlaps. Short codes count only in capitals.
- **Verdict per answer:**
  - "right": the new holder's value appears and the previous holder's does not;
  - "stale": the reverse;
  - "both" or "other" otherwise.
- **KNOWNX:** the previous holder's value is the right one.
- **Qwen's `<think>` blocks:** removed before scoring.
- **When a question was asked more than once:** its last answer counts.
- **Missing:** an empty answer is a refusal, scored wrong and counted as missing. A model or fine-tuned model missing more than 5% of its questions is not usable.

## 5. Hypotheses
Item-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345. The per-item value is the mean over models (or families) of the paired difference.

| Test | Measure | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | memory route: CHAIN right, GATED+ − NONE, over the usable models | **WIN** if the 95% interval lies wholly above +1 pp and every model's difference is above 0 |
| **H2** | memory route: CHAIN stale, GATED − GATED+ | **NON-INFERIOR** if the interval's lower bound is above −2 pp |
| **H3** | weights route: CHAIN right, untouched model with GATED+ − DIRECT fine-tuned model without memory, over the usable families | **WIN** as H1, over families |
| **H4** | weights route: DIRECT fine-tuned model, STEPS right − CHAIN right | **WIN** as H1, over families |

**Validity:**
- the memory route needs at least four usable models;
- the weights route needs at least two usable families, each with its untouched model and its three fine-tuned models usable.

**Descriptive:**
- right and stale rates per model, condition, probe and relation;
- composition: CHAIN right among the items whose KNOWN answer was right;
- FOLLOW − MORE, by half and probe.

## 7. Changes made in dev, before this freeze
- **National teams.** Citizenship items whose office name names a compared country were removed: dev went from 62 to 58 items, main from 312 to 307.
- **The spec's H5, FOLLOW − MORE on half B chains, is now descriptive.** In dev both arms answered 0% of half-B chains right, so the test could not separate them.
- **Two validity rules were added:** at least two usable families, and the missing-share rule per model.

## 8. What CH-1 cannot show
- **Two-step chains only,** and the second step is a stable fact about a person.
- **Most new holders are not famous,** so the KNOWN ceiling is well below 100%.
- **The weights route uses RP-3's models,** trained at RP-3's settings on direct facts (and, for FOLLOW, follow-on statements). They were not trained on chains.
