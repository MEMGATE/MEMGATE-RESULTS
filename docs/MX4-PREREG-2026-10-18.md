## Rules fixed in the code

**Data rules:**
- Labels follow the spec's documented thresholds.
- Air Quality's `-200` (written `-200` or `-200,0`) is missing.
- Rows without the label's measurement are dropped. This leaves 41,757 hours for Beijing and 8,991 for Air Quality.

**Scoring:**
- The arms, the audit rate (5%, `random.Random(seed + 4242)`; the seed is the window index), the health check and the reset are MX-3's.
- A window in which either arm answered nothing alone counts 0.

**INVALID conditions:**
- any non-determinism in the repeats;
- a failed run;
- GRAND+AUDIT observing no label.
