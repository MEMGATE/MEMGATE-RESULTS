**Findings.**

- **Arthāpatti postulates rarely, and is usually right when it does.** Correct shares: 508 of 582 on splice, 226 of 263 on dermatology, 51 of 58 on flare. It is weak on the 18-class chess endgame KRK, with 50 of 258.
- **The standpoint vote splits the datasets.** It helps where the data are noisy:
  - flare +3.7 pp;
  - post-operative +4.2;
  - contraceptive +1.3;
  - the synthetic NOISE and DRIFT families.

  It hurts where the label is an exact function of all the features. On car, −5.1 pp, the vote was wrong where 1-NN was right 735 times, and right where 1-NN was wrong 228 times. KRK loses −4.4. A standpoint that sees half the features cannot see such a concept.
- **No bug was found, and the dev run changes nothing.**
