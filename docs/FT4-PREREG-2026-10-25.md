## Main
- **Worlds:** 9600 and 9601 (SE-5's family, main pool), generated only after this freeze.
- **Arms and jobs:** NAIVE, LEARNER and LEARNER+RULES as in the spec, one hosted job each from the untouched Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2, global training): world 9600 on memgate-ft-eus2, memgate-ft-eus2b and jayeshupp-8050-resource; world 9601 on memgate-ft-swc2, memgate-ft-ncus3 and memgate-ft-swc4. FROZEN is the untouched model. The general prompts are answered by the untouched model once for both worlds (`runs/ft4-anchors-20261024`, the dev's answers).
- **Asking and scoring:** as in dev (`ft4.py ask`, `ft4.py report`): each model through one deployment, deleted after its questions; greedy; scored by `se3_world.said` and RP-1's follow-on rules.
- **Failures:** a job refused by Azure's data safety check, or failing, is recorded; a resubmission is decided and recorded in an addendum, never looped. If a world loses an arm, the hypotheses using that arm are reported on the other world alone, as such.

## Cost, stated in advance
Six jobs of about 0.1 to 0.5 million trained tokens at $4.50 per million (about $10), plus deployment hours and asking (a few dollars).

## Limits, stated in advance
- One job from the untouched model per arm: the learner's content at the end of the stream, not session-by-session training (CCL-2 tests that, at 7B and on hosted OpenAI models).
- One 70B model; two small main worlds; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; the general rules are written by us.
- Never-streamed offices fall for every fine-tuned arm (76.7% and 73.3% for the learners in dev, from 98.3%): the recipe does not keep them in the weights alone.
