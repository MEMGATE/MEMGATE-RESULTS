**Main worlds.** VIEWS at the new seeds 54000–54019, 400 cases each, under SOUND, ONE and TWO (failure from case 240), with SUPERVISOR, SUPERVISOR+ALARM and SUPERVISOR+ALARM-RECOVER. That gives 60 runs.

## Rules fixed in the code

**The alarm** is MX-11's: three-way splits, then `unwell` (W = 30, 3 SE, floor 0.05, at least 60 records).

**Recovery.**
- While paused, splits are recorded.
- When at least 30 have been recorded since the alarm, and the last 30 have a rate at or below baseline + sqrt(q(1−q)/30), answering resumes and the split record is emptied.
- Here baseline is the rate before the last 30 at the alarm, and q = max(baseline, 0.05).

**H1 (safety).** RECOVER in TWO is SAFE if the upper bound of the 95% CI of its wrong answers alone after case 240 is below 0.02 per case.

**H2 and H3 (cost).** RECOVER − SUPERVISOR, net credence, in SOUND and in ONE respectively. NO HARM if the CI lower bound is above −0.01.

**INVALID conditions:**
- non-determinism;
- a crashed run;
- SUPERVISOR's sound coverage below 5%.
