## What it shows
- **A verified change at one post leaves another post's answer stale, and models differ in whether they notice.** Given that the coach now coaches B, claude-opus-5-5 almost never named him for A. gpt-4.1-base named him more often than with no memory at all: B's line put his name in front of it.
- **The line the memory can derive, "X no longer holds the post at A", removed the stale answer in every model.** It cost nothing on B's own question.
- **The cost is caution.** With the derived line, models said "unknown" more often for A, and named A's real current coach 3.9 points less. The full repair is the memory checking A's own value: with A's fact, 97–100% named the current coach.
- **In a real memory, A's question retrieves A's entries, not B's.** There the derived line, filed under A, is what carries the change across.

## Limits
- **Scope:** head coaches only, from one snapshot. The derived line is right where one person cannot hold both posts. In the snapshot, at most 9.1% of coach moves kept the old post, and most of those are missing end dates.
- **Memory size:** one line of memory per question.
