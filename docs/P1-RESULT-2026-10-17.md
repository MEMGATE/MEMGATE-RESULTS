## What this means

**Different competences matter, not numbers.** Manusmṛti 12.111–112 asks for members of different learning. Nine of PARISHAD-SAME's ten lesson learners and seven of PARISHAD-10's ten are variants of one learner, so their votes move together and the assembly decides exactly like R-ORD. The three-member assembly of a lesson learner, a probabilistic learner and a tree learner beat every single one of its members on average:

- +1.44 pp over the lesson learner;
- +1.63 over naive Bayes.

It did so without any tuning.

**The five-expert majority replicates.** J-1 found it only descriptively, on its streams. Here it passed as a preregistered hypothesis: +1.07 pp.

**This is the strongest "mix of all" result so far.** No single śāstra-derived mechanism inside the lesson learner beat R-ORD by the 1 pp margin. Combining the lesson learner with learners of other kinds did.
