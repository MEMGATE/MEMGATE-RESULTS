## What this means

**A language model with its own lessons is a useful *different competence* in an assembly, but not a clearly better one than the offline lesson learner.**

- Replacing the offline lesson learner with the model gained +3.0 pp under drift, where the model adapts faster.
- It lost −0.7 pp on EXC, where the exceptions are exact and the offline learner tracks them exactly.
- Overall the gain is +1.01 pp, with a CI that includes 0.

**The assembly lifts the model a lot.** Sol alone with lessons was 71.5% on average. In the assembly with naive Bayes and ID3 it reached 77.1%, a WIN of +5.67 pp. Three models' kinds of errors partly cancel.

**The model alone did not beat the offline lesson learner here** (−0.79 pp). On these synthetic families the offline learner sees all memory, while the model sees eight neighbours and a few lessons. The gains in ML-1 and ML-2 were measured against the model's own experience, not against the offline learner.
