**Findings.**

- **Range literals help the lesson learner on several datasets:** waveform +2.8 pp, letter +2.2, yeast +2.0, pendigits and optical digits +1.0. A lesson can now say "q1 to q3" in one condition.
- **The inner stretch adds nothing beyond end-anchored thresholds** (−0.06).
- **Naive Bayes is hurt** (−2.1), because the overlapping stretches count the same evidence several times.
- **ID3 is unaffected by construction.** The information gain of the full four-way split is never smaller than that of any coarser two-way stretch, so ID3 never chooses them.
- **No bug was found,** and nothing changed after the dev run.
