## Hypotheses (each model separately)
An office-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345. NEIGH is the asked office's dated line among four other offices' dated lines; SINGLE is the line alone.

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | BOTH right under NEIGH, READERMIX − FROZEN | WIN: interval above +1 pp |
| H2 | BOTH right under NEIGH, READERMIX − READER1 | WIN |
| H3 | BOTH right under SINGLE, READERMIX − READER1 | NON-INFERIOR: lower bound above −5 pp |

**Changes from the spec's candidates, after dev:**
- **READER1 − FROZEN under NEIGH, the spec's first H1, is now descriptive.** Two trainings of the same READER1 on the same rows read the dev offices' four-neighbour memories very differently: OLMo-2 78% and 48%, Nemotron-Nano 64% and 16%.
- **The primary test is the mixed reader against the untouched model.**
- **H3's margin is −5 pp,** the margin TW-2 and TW-3 used for a cost they accept. The −2 pp of the spec's draft was set before any mixed reader was trained.

**Descriptive:**
- READER1 − FROZEN under NEIGH and SINGLE;
- each arm's NEIGH − SINGLE;
- answers naming a neighbour's holder;
- locality.

**Validity:** an arm with more than 5% of its questions unanswered makes that model's run invalid.
