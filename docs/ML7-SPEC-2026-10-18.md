# ML-7: kṣetrajña of the model itself: does a language model know where it knows?

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any ML-7 code. It makes real model calls (gpt-6.1-sol, through the same route as ML-4 to ML-6).

## 1. Question

**What is already known.**
- Bhagavad Gītā 13 distinguishes the field (*kṣetra*) from its knower (*kṣetrajña*).
- In JK-1 and RP-1, the lesson learner's own sense of knowing was informative: its answers where a lesson decided were +5.9 to +7.1 pp more accurate than as many random answers.
- In ML-6, a language model in an assembly had **no** doubt signal of its own. The assembly needed a warm-up, and its asking relied on disagreement among the members.

**What ML-7 asks.** Can the model itself say where it knows?
- The model answers as before and adds one word: SURE or UNSURE.
- Is its SURE informative (kṣetrajña)?
- Does asking for the label only when it says UNSURE beat random asking (jijñāsā of the model)?

## 2. Arms

The learner is the model alone: gpt-6.1-sol with its own ORD lesson store, exactly as ORD-sol in ML-4 to ML-6.
- **Prompt:** ML-1's prediction prompt. The system instruction asks for the label followed by SURE or UNSURE.
- **Parsing:** the label is parsed as before. A reply with neither word counts as UNSURE: the model did not claim to know.

| Arm | Labels observed |
| --- | --- |
| SELF-FULL | every case |
| SELF-JIJNASA | the first 10 cases (the warm-up found necessary in ML-6), then only cases where the model says UNSURE |
| SELF-RANDOM-ASK | the first 10, then as many as SELF-JIJNASA asked for, at random later cases (`random.Random(seed + 777)`) |

An observed case enters the memory the prompts draw neighbours from. The model then proposes a lesson from it, as in ML-4.

## 3. Worlds

ML-4's synthetic families EXC, NOISE and DRIFT, 100 cases each.
- **Main:** the new seeds 39000–39005, so 18 worlds.
- **Dev:** exposed seed 0 of each family.
- **Smoke:** DRIFT seed 0, cut to 30 cases.

The expected number of calls is about 8,000.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before, stratified by family.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary, kṣetrajña)** | In SELF-FULL, accuracy where the model said SURE − accuracy of as many random cases (`random.Random(seed + 999)`). A world with no SURE case counts 0. |
| H2 (jijñāsā) | SELF-JIJNASA − SELF-RANDOM-ASK (accuracy) |
| Descriptive | the share of SURE answers; accuracy when SURE vs UNSURE; SELF-JIJNASA − SELF-FULL |

**INVALID if** the model fails or refuses on more than 2% of its calls, or more than 2% of its labels are unparseable.

## 5. Predictions before any ML-7 code

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | positive, WIN | A model's stated confidence usually tracks its accuracy, even when it is overconfident. |
| H2 | positive; UNDETERMINED or WIN | If the model says SURE too often, it will ask too little, and after DRIFT's switch it may stay sure and wrong. |
