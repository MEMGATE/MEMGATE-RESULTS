## The rules on 2024–26 data

**What the cases are.** Each is a person starting a post while memory still had them in another. A line "X no longer holds A" is right when they had left A within 90 days.

| Rule | Lines written | Right | Precision | Recall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LEARNED** (from history to 2023, the layer's grouping) | 295 | 274 | **92.88%** | 88.96% |
| LEARNED-COARSE (by relation only) | 325 | 300 | 92.31% | 97.40% |
| ALWAYS ("one post at a time") | 379 | 308 | 81.27% | 100.0% |
| SAME-KIND (same kind of post) | 364 | 304 | 83.52% | 98.70% |

## What it shows
- **Memory learned from its own history which changes imply others, with the exceptions.**
  - From tenures to 2023 it found that a head coach who takes a new club has left the old one.
  - It found that heads of state keep their other realms, and so do chairpersons who become heads of government.
  - On 2024–26 data its lines were right 92.9% of the time. Fixed rules were right 81–84% of the time.
  - In this half, 27 cases were heads of state gaining another post, and none of them left. The fixed rules wrote a wrong line for each.
- **The cost is fewer lines.** The learned rule does not write lines for club and national-team coaching, because 13–16% kept both before 2024. It found 89.0% of the departures, against 98.7% for SAME-KIND.
  - The coarse version, without the national/club split, kept the precision and found 97.4%.
  - The split guards against a real exception, a coach of a national team and a club at once. In this data it cost more recall than it saved in precision.
- **Why precision matters more here.** A wrong derived line makes a model doubt a right answer: RP-4 found that models answered "unknown" more often when they had derived lines. A missing line leaves the old answer to be caught by the next check (RC-1).

## Limits
- **The interval is wide.** One person with many posts can carry many cases, and the bootstrap resamples people: 277 people, 379 cases.
- **End dates are incomplete.** A missing end date with no recorded successor counts as "kept", so precision is a lower bound.
- **Mostly head coaches:** 325 of 379 cases.
