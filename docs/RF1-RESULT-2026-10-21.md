## What it shows
- **The gate learns with no registry, and attacker values stay out.**
  - Gates v1–v3 learn nothing without a registry.
  - Truth discovery and majority vote let attacker values in on 2.4–47.6% of offices.
  - The registry-free gate let in none, in all 20 worlds, against TM-1's attackers and AU-1's adaptive ones.
- **It covers more than truth discovery does.**
  - Mean coverage is 3.6 pp above truth discovery's most permissive setting.
  - At the end it covers 82.6% of changed offices, against 17.7–20.9%. Truth discovery's coverage falls as the stream goes on; the gate's rises.
- **What the registry is still worth:** about 30 pp of mean coverage and 14.5 pp at the end. The gate needs time to establish sources, and a value needs three independent voices. With no registry, nothing in it is lost to attackers, only time.

## Limits
- **Attackers:** no attacker in these worlds controls three or more established sources that name the same false value. The rule of three independent voices is exactly what such a group would have to beat. Attackers built against this gate are not tested.
- **Honest sources:** they lag by one session and never lie; real sources also make honest errors.
- **Domain:** office holders, simulated over real 2024–26 timelines.
- **Coverage:** it is low in the first sessions, while sources are established.
