## What this means

**The fivefold grade adds nothing over the long-run leader.** In the five-expert setting, the natural standing (rank by long-run record) dominates. The temporary standing (±1 against the median of the last 20 cases) rarely overturns the leader, so MAITRI and GLOBAL make almost the same choices. The grade is better than following recent accuracy alone, +0.46 pp, because recent-only choosing over-switches on noise.

**A plain majority of five different learners was the best chooser on this pool.** It beat MAITRI by 0.86 pp, and it was the only arm here that significantly beat the lesson learner on the real streams: +1.12 pp, a WIN by the decision rule, but descriptive, because it was not a preregistered hypothesis. On the exposed dev data the order was reversed: MAITRI was 0.90 pp better than MAJORITY. So which chooser wins depends on the data. A preregistered test of the majority as the default "mix of all learners" is a fair next step.

**Choosing among experts beats the lesson learner on the streams,** by +0.6 to +1.1 pp for every chooser. That is the place where no single learner is best throughout.
