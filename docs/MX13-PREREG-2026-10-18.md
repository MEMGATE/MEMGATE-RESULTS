**Main worlds.** VIEWS at the new seeds 55000–55019, 400 cases each, under SOUND, ONE and TWO (failure from case 240), with SUPERVISOR, SUPERVISOR+ALARM and SUPERVISOR+ALARM-VERIFY. That gives 60 runs.

## Rules fixed in the code

**The alarm** is MX-11's: three-way splits, then `unwell`.

**Verification while paused.**
- Each trusted spy's report on each paused case is checked against the owner's answer.
- After 30 paused cases, a spy is dismissed if its accuracy over the last 30 is below best − 3·SE, where best is the best spy's accuracy. Several spies may be dismissed.
- SE = sqrt(max(best·(1−best), 1/30)/30). The floor 1/30 keeps SE above zero when the best spy is perfect; it was added in the code before this freeze, and the spec's formula omits it.
- If at least two spies remain, answering resumes and the split record empties. Otherwise the record restarts and the pause holds.

**H1 (safety).** VERIFY in TWO is SAFE if the upper bound of the 95% CI of its wrong answers alone after case 240 is below 0.02 per case.

**H2 and H3 (cost).** VERIFY − SUPERVISOR, net credence, in SOUND and in ONE respectively. NO HARM if the CI lower bound is above −0.01.

**INVALID conditions:**
- non-determinism;
- a crashed run;
- SUPERVISOR's sound coverage below 5%.
