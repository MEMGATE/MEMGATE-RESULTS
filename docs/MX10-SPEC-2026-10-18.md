# MX-10: is the supervisor safe when two of three sources fail? (a setting that meets the testability condition)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-10 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

MX-9 could not answer this: on WISDM no source could decide anything alone. Here the question is asked in a setting where the sound network works. VIEWS, from AR-1, is a hidden case seen through three noisy sources; there the sound network answers alone about a third of the time.

The worry is specific. After two failures, the supervisor dismisses one broken source and then trusts the agreement of the remaining two, one of which is broken. Does it then start answering wrongly alone?

## 2. Arms and conditions

`shastra/mx5.py`'s `run_supervisor` is used unchanged, and MX-9's `fail_two` is also unchanged. The arms are SPIES (no pieces) and SUPERVISOR (all four pieces).

| Condition | What fails, from case 240 of 400 |
| --- | --- |
| SOUND | nothing |
| ONE | one source (MX-5's `fail_from`) |
| TWO | two distinct sources (MX-9's `fail_two`) |

## 3. Data

VIEWS, AR-1's generator (keep 0.9), at the new seeds 52000–52019, 400 cases each.

**Testability condition (MX-9's lesson).** The SOUND network must answer alone on at least 5% of cases. If it does not, the run is reported NOT TESTABLE. AR-1's dev run gave about 36%.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

**Measure.** Wrong answers given alone after case 240, per 100 cases after it. It is computed by the prefix method of MX-9 (full run less the run on the first 240 cases).

| Hypothesis | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary, safety)** | SUPERVISOR in TWO is **SAFE** if the upper bound of the 95% bootstrap CI of its wrong answers alone after the failure is below 2 per 100. Otherwise it is **UNSAFE**. |
| H2 | SUPERVISOR − SPIES, net credence, ONE: the lab's usual WIN rule (a replication on synthetic data) |
| H3 | SUPERVISOR − SPIES, net credence, TWO: the usual rule |
| Descriptive | Wrong answers alone after case 240 for every arm and condition. Which sources were dismissed in TWO (a broken one or the working one). |

**INVALID if:**
- any run is non-deterministic or crashes;
- the testability condition fails.

## 5. Predictions

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | SAFE | A broken spy guesses, so the guessing clause blocks credence. When it does agree, the answer is the working spy's. |
| H2 | WIN | as AR-1 |
| H3 | small positive | |
