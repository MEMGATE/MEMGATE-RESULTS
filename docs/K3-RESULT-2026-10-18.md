## What this means

**The kolam reading carries over from a game to a physical world, at a smaller size.**
- Collisions have no preferred forward or transverse direction. So each event was also taken in with its pseudorapidities reversed, its azimuthal angles reflected, and both.
- All three learners gained: R-ORD +0.78 pp, 1-NN +0.63, naive Bayes +1.03.
- **Why smaller than K-2 (+2.14).** On the jungle board the mirror image has exactly the same outcome. Here a mirrored event is only equally likely under each class, so it adds evidence about distributions, not a known case.

**Naive Bayes gained most, for two mixed reasons.**
- The images make each class's angle distributions symmetric, removing sampling noise.
- They also count every unchanged feature four times, which weakens add-one smoothing.

The design cannot separate the two; this was stated before the main run.

**Where the kolam reading stands now:**

| Test | Data | Gain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K-1 | exposed boards | exploratory |
| K-2 | a fresh board | WIN |
| K-3 | a fresh physical dataset | positive for every learner; WIN for naive Bayes |

A known symmetry of the world is a reliable source of extra experience, and it helps most where the symmetry is exact.
