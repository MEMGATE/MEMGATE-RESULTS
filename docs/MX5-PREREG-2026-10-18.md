## Rules fixed in the code

**The network, dismissal and guessing.** These are AR-1's rules. DISMISS uses 20 of the last 30 two-against-one splits, at most one spy.

**The failing source.**
- One source, drawn from `random.Random(f"MX5-FAIL:{seed}")`, fails from case int(0.6 n): case 480 of VIEWS-RECUR, case 600 of the rig.
- From then on, each of its values is replaced by the same column's value at a random case of the same dataset.

**AUDIT.** A case the network would answer alone is asked with probability 0.05, drawn from `random.Random(seed + 4242)`.

**RESET.**
- PK-1's `unwell` is applied to the network's answer errors on observed cases since the last reset.
- On vikṛti, each trusted spy is rebuilt from the latest 30 observed cases.

**SAMSKARA.** The spies use R-ORD+SAMSKARA-R3.

**Measures.**
- Net credence is (right − wrong answers given alone) / scored cases.
- Also wrong answers given alone (descriptive). Its paired differences were added to the analysis before this freeze, after dev run a. That is the only change between dev runs a and b; their verdicts are identical.

**INVALID conditions:**
- non-determinism;
- a crashed run;
- SUPERVISOR never dismissing;
- SUPERVISOR never resetting.

**Findings.**
- **The whole system and two of its pieces earn their place.**
  - The supervisor more than doubled credence on the real sensor data.
  - Dismissal restores credence after the failure (as AR-1).
  - Saṃskāra in the spies adds most on room (+13.6) and occupancy (+11.5). A sensor lesson broken by one noisy case comes back after three confirmations, so the spies guess less.
- **Audits and resets cost credence here.**
  - The guessing clause already keeps wrong answers given alone near 0 on the real data. Audits then only take away answers.
  - Resets throw away learned lessons. They do reduce the wrong answers on VIEWS-RECUR (7.7% → 4.9%), where the rules switch. At the spec's price of a wrong answer (twice a question), that is a wash there (+0.13).
