## Rules fixed in the code

These are MX-5's rules, unchanged:
- three R-ORD spies, one per source;
- credence when all trusted spies agree and none is guessing; ask otherwise;
- DISMISS: the odd one in 20 of the last 30 splits;
- AUDIT: 5% of the cases it would answer alone;
- RESET: PK-1's health check, then rebuild from the latest 30 observed cases;
- SAMSKARA: R-ORD+SAMSKARA-R3;
- the failure: `random.Random(f"MX5-FAIL:{seed}")` picks the source, from int(0.6 n).

**The sources are fixed by MX-6's runner.** Columns 0–5 are turbine and gas generator, 6–7 compressor, and 8–13 propulsion and fuel control.

**INVALID conditions:**
- non-determinism;
- a crashed run;
- the supervisor never dismissing;
- the supervisor never resetting.
