# XF-1 preregistration: CCL-4's test on a second open model family, OLMo-2-7B (frozen)

**Status: FROZEN on 27 October 2026, 20:37 IST, before any XF-1 main world was packed or run.** Drafted at 20:24. The two-session dev run on the A100 (`runs/xf1-dev-olmo/w9792-s2`, dev world 9792) ran the frozen kernel on OLMo-2 end to end: the untouched model answered all 2,290 questions in 184 seconds with answers in the expected forms; SYSTEM2 trained two sessions (first checks missed 1.3% and 5.6% of new items; practice kept and undone as designed); NAIVE trained two sessions; done line, exit code 0. Nothing below was changed after the dev run. Item 6 of the package's fixed list (`docs/GPU-RUN-PLAN-2026-10-27.md`).

## Why
Every learner result so far is on one open model family (Qwen2.5, 7B and 72B). GPT-4.1 (CCL-5H) would answer whether the learner is specific to that family, but it is held up in Azure's fine-tuning queue. XF-1 runs CCL-4's test unchanged on OLMo-2-7B-Instruct: a fully open model (Apache 2.0) from another lab, with other data and another tokenizer. The lab has run it before (TW-3, GC-1, GC-2).

## Design
- **Model.** `allenai/OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct` at revision `470b1fba1ae01581f270116362ee4aa1b97f4c84` (TW-3's pin), in 16 bits.
- **Worlds.** 9702 and 9703, CCL-4's main worlds, with CCL-4's options: 40 registry errors believed in session 1 and corrected in session 4; 12 sessions; evaluations after sessions 4, 8 and 12. The packer checks that every question equals CCL-4's payloads.

## Hypotheses
CCL-4's, with CCL-4's rules, after session 12, both worlds pooled, on OLMo-2: H1, H2, H11, H3, H10, H12, H12b, H5, H6, H7, H13, H8, H9. REPLAY does not run, so R1–R3 are not tested.
- Abilities are judged as in CCL-4: PASSED, FAILED or NOT SHOWN.
- The result reports OLMo-2's verdicts beside Qwen2.5-7B's (CCL-4's 7B result, same worlds and questions). The claim tested is that the learner's abilities are not specific to one model family.

## Predictions
OLMo-2-7B is a weaker base model than Qwen2.5-7B (GC-2: GSM8K 58% against 75%), and the lab's TW-4 found its reading of a single line fragile.
- **H1, H11, H5, H6, H7 and H12: WIN**, as on Qwen. These rest on what the gate admits and the rows trained, not on the base model.
- **H2, H3, H10, H8 and H9: NON-INFERIOR.**
- **H12b and H13: NOT SHOWN, more likely than not.**

So abilities 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 9 are predicted PASSED, 4 and 8 NOT SHOWN more likely than not. Any ability that passes on Qwen and fails on OLMo-2 is reported as such.
