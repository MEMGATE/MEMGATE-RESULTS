## What this means

**Every frontier model tested is steered the same way.**
- **The attack.** A raw memory concatenates untrusted text unescaped, so one forged line in its format, claiming to be the registry, reads as the memory's own provenance.
- **The forged line** steered the fourth model, from a third lab, on 106 of 109 facts where it came last. That is the same rate as the others (97–99%).
- **Honest raw memory** was read perfectly, with no poison at all. The attack, not the reader's skill, decides the outcome.

**The gate holds for every model,** because it admits values on the evidence of their real sources and passes only the verified value. The result was 0 poison and 100% truth, with prompts thirteen times smaller.

**RM-5 shows the cheapest fix for this channel:** escape or mark the untrusted text. The gate adds what escaping cannot: it is independent of the model, and it judges sources and currency (RM-1, RM-6).

## Limits, stated plainly

- **One prompt design and default effort.** Claude models may respond differently with other system prompts or with extended thinking.
- **The same synthetic world and scripted attacks as RM-3.** The worlds were RM-3's, so the comparison across models is paired. They were not new data for the attack's design, which was frozen before RM-3.
