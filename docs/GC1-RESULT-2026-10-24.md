## Reading
- For two of the three families the reading skill came at no measurable cost to general knowledge; OLMo-2 even scored higher after the training.
- Nemotron-Nano lost 2.81 points, mostly in the humanities and social sciences. Its reader's last training batch also ended at a loss of 0.19 here, against about 0 for the other two (in TW-2's run, 0.0002), so this training of it went less cleanly.
- Together with TW-2, the hybrid holds on two of three families at no general-knowledge cost; on the third the skill has a price that a lab would weigh.

## What this does not show
- One benchmark, zero-shot, letter answers; three small open models.
- The reader's training only: facts trained into the weights (TM-1's route) are not covered.
- The reader was trained again for this test; Kaggle's T4s do not give bit-identical training, so it is TW-2's recipe, not TW-2's adapter.
