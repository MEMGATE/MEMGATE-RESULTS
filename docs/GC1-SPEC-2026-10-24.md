# GC-1 spec: general knowledge after the skill of reading dated memory lines is trained into the weights

**Written:** 24 October 2026, 09:47 IST, before any GC-1 code, data or answer.

## Why
The lab's hybrid keeps facts in memory and trains a durable skill into the weights: reading the layer's dated memory line (TW-2: three open 7–8B models answered both dates right for 98.3–100% of new offices after light training, against 17.3–68.3% untouched; TW-3 and TW-4 extended it). A lab will ask whether that training costs general knowledge, and the brief says "We have not run general capability benchmarks." GC-1 measures it.

## What
- **Models:** TW-2's three families at TW-2's revisions: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (`a09a3545…`), OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct (`470b1fba…`), Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B-v1 (`54641c16…`).
- **Arms:** FROZEN, the untouched model; READER, one LoRA adapter trained exactly as in TW-2's main run: `cl/tw2.py`'s training rows, GPU script and settings (2 epochs, learning rate 2e-4, batches of 8 accumulated over micro-batches of 4, rank 16, alpha 32), unchanged, on Kaggle's two T4s. The reader is trained again, since TW-2 kept no adapter.
- **Questions:** MMLU (`cais/mmlu`, MIT licence). Main: the test split, 20 questions per subject chosen with a fixed seed, 57 subjects, 1,140 questions. Dev: the validation split, 100 questions chosen with a fixed seed, so no main question is seen before the main run.
- **Asking:** zero-shot, through each model's chat template (Nemotron-Nano with "detailed thinking off", as in TW-1 to TW-4). System message: "Answer the multiple-choice question with the letter of the correct option only." User turn: the question, then the four options as "A. …" to "D. …" on separate lines, then "Answer:". Greedy, at most 4 new tokens. The first capital letter A–D in the reply, standing alone, is the answer; a reply without one is wrong.
- **Measure:** accuracy per arm; READER − FROZEN, paired by question, with a bootstrap 95% interval (10,000 resamples, fixed seed). Accuracy by MMLU's four subject groups is descriptive.

## Hypothesis (to be frozen in the preregistration, after dev)
- **H1, per family:** READER is non-inferior to FROZEN at −2.0 points: the lower end of the 95% interval of READER − FROZEN is above −2.0.

## Dev
One family (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), the 100 validation questions. It checks that both arms answer, that letters are read from the replies, and how long a run takes. Dev may change the prompt or the letter rule; it may not change the models, the training or the main questions.

## Limits, stated in advance
- One benchmark, zero-shot, letter answers; small open models; the reader's training only. Facts trained into the weights (TM-1's route) are not covered.
- Kaggle's T4s train in float16 with a gradient scaler, as TW-2 did; a second training of the same reader is not bit-identical to TW-2's.
