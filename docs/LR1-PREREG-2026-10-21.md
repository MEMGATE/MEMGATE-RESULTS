# LR-1 preregistration: can memory learn its own update rules from its history, and are they right later?

**Status:** frozen 21 October 2026, before the main half was run. Spec and preregistration in one document.
**Dev run:** `runs/lr1-dev-20261021/summary.json` (TM-1's dev half: 337 cases, 280 people).

Code SHA-256: `279021687d22adc64f422399a05517edc0f3234341f041926a40a675ac9e5532`

The hash covers `cl/lr1.py`, `memgate/derive.py`, the tenure reader (`cl/link_stats.py`), the world helpers and the Wikidata files.

## 1. Question
A derived line is one memory writes without new evidence. When it newly verifies person X in post B while it also holds X in post A, it adds "X no longer holds A" (RP-4). Whether that line is right depends on the kinds of post:
- a head coach who takes a new club has left the old one;
- a head of state who gains a realm keeps the others;
- a chairperson who becomes a mayor may keep the chair.

**The learner.** `memgate.derive.Exclusivity` learns from past tenures which pairs of kinds one person does not hold at once. A pair counts as exclusive when it has at least 20 past cases and at most 10% of them kept the old post. The layer separates national teams from clubs, as in MC-1.

**The test.** Are the lines the learned rule writes right on later data, more often than the lines simple fixed rules write?

## 2. Design
**Learning, only from what was known on 1 January 2024:**
- posts that started by 2 October 2023, so that 90 days had passed;
- end dates after 1 January 2024 are treated as unknown.

**Cases.** Every post B started between 1 March 2024 and 20 July 2026 by a person X who held another tracked post A in the year before. The end of the window leaves 90 days before the snapshot date.
- **X left A** if, within 90 days of starting B, A's tenure ended or someone else started in A after X. A successor covers end dates the record lacks.
- **Halves:** cases are split into TM-1's halves by post B. Dev uses the dev half; **main uses the main half,** not run before this freeze.

**Rules:**
- **LEARNED:** the learned rule, with the layer's grouping;
- **LEARNED-COARSE:** the learned rule by relation only, with no national/club split (descriptive);
- **ALWAYS:** "one post at a time", a line for every case;
- **SAME-KIND:** a line when A and B are the same kind of post;
- **NEVER:** no lines.

**Measures:**
- **precision:** the share of a rule's lines that were right;
- **recall:** the share of cases where X left A that got a line.

## 3. Hypotheses
The estimate is computed on all cases of the half. Its 95% interval is a bootstrap over people (10,000 resamples, seed 12345); all of a person's cases move together.

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | precision, LEARNED − ALWAYS | **WIN** if the interval lies wholly above +1 pp; LOSS if wholly below −1 pp; otherwise no difference shown |
| H2 | precision, LEARNED − SAME-KIND | same rule |
| H3 (cost) | recall, LEARNED − SAME-KIND | **DECREASE** if the interval lies wholly below −1 pp; INCREASE if wholly above +1 pp; otherwise no difference shown |

## 4. Predictions (dev)

| Rule | Lines | Precision | Recall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LEARNED | 260 | 91.92% | 85.05% |
| LEARNED-COARSE | 295 | 91.86% | 96.44% |
| ALWAYS | 337 | 83.38% | 100.0% |
| SAME-KIND | 310 | 88.71% | 97.86% |

| Test | Dev estimate, pp [95% interval] | Dev verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | +8.54 [+5.05, +12.52] | WIN |
| H2 | +3.21 [+1.13, +5.55] | WIN |
| H3 | −12.81 [−17.03, −8.99] | DECREASE |

**What the learner found from pre-2024 history:**
- Club coach to club coach is the one exclusive pair: 29 of 966 kept the old club.
- Heads of state keep their posts (68 of 71). So do chairpersons who become heads of government (111 of 119).
- Club and national-team coaching kept the other post in 13–16% of cases. They are therefore not exclusive under the 10% bound.

**On dev, the coarse rule kept the same precision with more recall.** That is because national/club pairs were mostly right on later data. The layer's setting is still the one tested here, as decided before the main half.

## 5. Limits
- **One snapshot, with incomplete end dates.** A missing end date with no recorded successor counts as "kept", so precision is a lower bound.
- **Most cases are head coaches.** Other kinds give few cases each.
- **What is learned** is which pairs of kinds are exclusive, not exceptions for individual people.
