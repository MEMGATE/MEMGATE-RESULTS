## The system under test
CCL-2's learner, unchanged (one Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, one LoRA adapter, twelve sessions, the same rows and rules), plus the **scope rule**: a question reaches the learner's adapter only if, as written, it is about something the learner had learned by then. That means one of three things:
- an office whose relation and subject the text names (linked by SE-3's router, `se3_world.link`);
- a person who held a learned office, named in the text;
- a code rewrite, once a polars recipe was learned.

Every other question is answered by the untouched model. Both models answer every question in the same kernel, and the rule chooses between their answers from the question text alone.

## What passing would and would not mean
- **3 and 9 are kept by design, not learned.** The router hands the untouched model every question that is not about what the learner learned, so never-streamed knowledge and general ability are the untouched model's own. The test checks that the system decides this correctly from the question text, at the scale of a full stream.
- **The router links names and relations.** A question that names nothing learned, yet depends on what was learned, goes to the untouched model.

## Limits
- One 7B model; two worlds of about 170 offices; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; 28 one-line migrations of one library; general ability on 150 GSM8K and 200 MMLU questions per world.
- The router's lists (relation words, subject names) come from the snapshot of offices; a new kind of office needs its words added.
