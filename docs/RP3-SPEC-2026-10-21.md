# RP-3 spec: does a fine-tuned model carry a new fact when its training data also holds what follows from it?

**Status:** spec, 21 October 2026.
- **Dev:** running since 13:27 on `memgate-ft-eus2` (`runs/rp3-dev-20261021`). Its three jobs are waiting for GPUs. The untouched model's answers are in.
- **Main:** its three training jobs were submitted at 13:31 on `memgate-ft-swc2`, before the preregistration, to save a day of GPU queue. The job ids and the SHA-256 of each training file are in `runs/rp3-main-pretrain-20261021/jobs.json`.
  - No main question will be asked before the preregistration is frozen.
  - The frozen runner reattaches a job only if its training file is byte-identical to the rows the frozen code builds.
  - If dev changes the design, these jobs are discarded.
**Code:** `cl/rp3.py`.

## Why
RP-2 measured GPT-4.1 fine-tuned on direct facts ("Who is the head coach of B?" → X):
- it names the new holder for 73–77% of offices;
- its reverse answers ("X holds which post?") are no better than the untouched model's;
- its answers about the previous holder, and about whether that person still holds the post, are worse.

The verified record holds more than the direct fact: the new holder, the previous holder and the date of the change. RP-3 asks two questions:
- **Trained follow-ons:** does fine-tuning carry the fact when the training data also states what follows from it?
- **Transfer:** if those statements are trained for some offices, do the other offices' follow-on answers improve too? That would mean the model learned to carry a new fact, not just the statements it was shown.

## Design
**Model.** GPT-4.1 (`gpt-4.1-2025-04-14`), Azure hosted fine-tuning, FT-2's settings: 6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2. Each arm is one job from the untouched model.

**Offices.** RP-1's: dev on its 50 dev offices, main on its 300 main offices. Each set is split into halves A and B by a fixed hash of the office.

**Arms:**

| Arm | Training rows per office, half A | Half B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DIRECT | the direct question in phrasings 0 and 1 → new holder (2) | 2 |
| FOLLOW | DIRECT's 2, plus 8 follow-on statements | 2 |
| MORE | DIRECT's 2, repeated to 10 | 2 |
| FROZEN | the untouched gpt-4.1-base, no training | — |

FOLLOW's eight statements per half-A office, two wordings each:
- which post the new holder holds;
- whom the new holder replaced;
- that the previous holder no longer holds the post ("No.");
- that the new holder does ("Yes.").

None of these wordings is a wording the test asks. MORE matches FOLLOW's amount of training on half A without the follow-on content.

**Test questions,** with no memory and RP-1's date (2026-10-19):
- DIRECT: phrasing 0, a trained phrasing.
- DIRECT2: phrasing 2, which no arm trains.
- REVERSE, PREVIOUS and STILL: RP-1's exact wording.
- STILLNOW: "Is X still the … of …?", where the right answer is yes. Without it, a model that answers "no" to everything would score 100% on STILL.
  - STILL pair: STILL and STILLNOW both right.
- Follow-on score per office: the mean of REVERSE, PREVIOUS and the STILL pair.
- LOCAL: the direct question for static offices that no arm trains, to measure damage elsewhere. Dev uses 50 offices and main 300, disjoint.

**Scoring:** RP-1's scorer, with these additions:
- STILLNOW: the answer starts with "yes";
- LOCAL and DIRECT2: the name matcher, against the current holder.

**Deployments:**
- Each fine-tuned model gets a developer-tier deployment, created one at a time, five minutes apart.
- Each is deleted once its questions are asked.
- A refused deployment stops the run. It is not retried in a loop.

## Hypotheses (main; to be frozen after dev)
Paired over offices, 10,000 bootstrap resamples, seed 12345:
- **H1 (primary):** REVERSE on half A, FOLLOW − DIRECT. WIN if the 95% interval is above +1 pp.
- **H2:** PREVIOUS on half A, FOLLOW − DIRECT, same rule.
- **H3:** STILL pair on half A, FOLLOW − DIRECT, same rule.
- **H4 (transfer):** follow-on score on half B, FOLLOW − MORE. Two-sided:
  - WIN above +1 pp;
  - LOSS below −1 pp;
  - otherwise no difference shown.
- **H5:** DIRECT2 on all offices, FOLLOW − DIRECT. NON-INFERIOR if the interval's lower bound is above −2 pp.
- **H6:** LOCAL, FOLLOW − DIRECT, same rule as H5.

**Validity:** an arm with more than 5% of its questions unanswered or refused makes the run INVALID.

## What dev is for
- Check the pipeline end to end: rows, jobs, deployments, asking, scoring.
- See the scorer on the new kinds (STILLNOW, DIRECT2, LOCAL).
- Look for refusals and hedging.

Dev does not set the hypotheses' direction; that is fixed above.

## Limits
- Office facts only; one model; one job per arm.
- H1–H3 test statements the model was trained on, in new wordings. That the model then answers them is the expected part.
- The new part is the size of the effect against RP-2's deficit, the cost to the direct fact and to locality (H5, H6), and transfer (H4).
