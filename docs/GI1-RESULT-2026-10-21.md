## What it shows
- **Question:** the field summary names a known failure, an edit placed in every prompt that damages tasks it was not written for. GI-1 was built to measure that damage for the lessons ML-M uses.
- **Result:** no harm. The lessons raised right answers on questions they were not written for.
- **Where the gain comes from:** almost all of it is one model's yes/no answers.
  - With its memory naming the current holder, gpt-4.1-base said "no" to "is that person still in post?" for 45% of offices. Its yes/no answer contradicted its own memory.
  - The lessons are about trusting what the record shows. With them it agreed with its memory for 96%.
  - The two stronger models were near the ceiling without lessons.
- **Taken with ML-M:** what the system learned from a model's checked mistakes improved the model on new cases of the same mistake, and also on other questions, at no cost here.

## Limits
- **One edit, one kind of fact, three models.** Other global edits can do harm. ML-M's first wording made models answer "unknown" to almost everything in the case it was written for.
- **The gain is mostly one model's,** on one question kind.
- **"SCOPED" is the memory alone** on these questions, by construction.
