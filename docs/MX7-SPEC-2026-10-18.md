# MX-7: the three-source network (AR-1) and the supervisor (MX-5) on a third real setting, with unchanged code

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-7 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

**What held, and where.**
- AR-1's dismissal won on Multiple Features and MHEALTH (+13.41 net credence).
- MX-5's supervisor won on the hydraulic rig (+13.14).
- MX-6 did not replicate MX-5 on a naval simulator, because no single sensor group could decide anything. The network answered ≤ 1.6% alone.

**What MX-7 tests.** Both, with their code unchanged, on a third real setting where each source can plausibly decide often on its own. That is the precondition MX-6 identified. The setting is three body-worn motion sensors watching one person's activity. It is close to several cameras watching one site.

## 2. Arms and rules

**AR-1, unchanged (`shastra/ar1.py`).**
- Arms: SPIES, SPIES+DISMISS and SPIES+RANDOM-DISMISS.
- Each case runs SOUND and FAILING; in FAILING, one source fails from the middle case.

**MX-5, unchanged (`shastra/mx5.py`).**
- Arms: SPIES, SUPERVISOR, and the supervisor without each piece.
- One source fails from 60%.

## 3. Data: PAMAP2 (UCI 231), new to the lab

PAMAP2 records 9 volunteers wearing three inertial units (IMUs), on the hand, the chest and the ankle, each sampled at 100 Hz. They performed 12 protocol activities, such as lying, walking, cycling and ironing.

**Reading (fixed before the data are opened beyond its documentation).**
- **Files.** Only the `Protocol` files are read; the `Optional` files are not. Activity 0 (transient) is dropped.
- **Cases.** A case is a 1-second window: 100 consecutive samples within one activity of one subject. A shorter remainder is dropped, as for MHEALTH.
- **The three sources.** Each IMU gives 19 features:
  - the mean and standard deviation of the ±16 g acceleration (3 axes), the gyroscope (3) and the magnetometer (3);
  - the mean temperature.
- **Not used.** Heart rate (it belongs to no single source) and the orientation columns, which the dataset's documentation marks invalid.
- **Missing samples.** These are wireless drops, coded as `NaN`. They are skipped; a statistic with no samples is missing.
- **Storage.** The archive is not stored. A derived table of windows is stored with its builder, as for MHEALTH and the hydraulic rig.

**Orders.**
- 10 shuffled orders (seeds 49000–49009), cut to 1,000 cases.
- Numeric columns are binned by the H-1 rule, and the label is the activity.

**Dev.** There is no dev run, since the code is AR-1's and MX-5's, both frozen. The loader is checked by shape only, and no learner sees the data before the main run.
