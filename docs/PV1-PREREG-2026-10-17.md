## Rules fixed in the code

**Knowing.** The lesson member knows a case when its answer comes from lessons with at least 6 supporting cases in total. This is JK-1's doubt rule, reversed.

**Overrule.** With three members and ties going to the earliest, the plurality overrules the lesson member only when the other two agree against it. VIDVAT changes exactly those cases in which the member knows.

**Matched controls.**
- RANDOM-VIDVAT lets the lesson member decide alone on as many scored cases as it knew in that run, drawn by `random.Random(order seed + 555)`.
- SABHA-RANDOM-ASK observes as many labels as SABHA-JIJNASA asked for, at cases drawn by `random.Random(order seed + 777)`.

**INVALID conditions:**
- any non-determinism in the repeats;
- a failed run;
- the learned member never overruling the assembly;
- SABHA-JIJNASA asking for every label in every run.

## Dev run (exposed data)

The output is in [`runs/pv1-dev-20261017-a`](../runs/pv1-dev-20261017-a/SUMMARY.md). It covers the 26 held-out datasets of P-2, MX-1 and JK-1, whose main runs are over, in orders 2000–2009. There were 260 runs, all 26 repeats were identical, and nothing failed.

| Comparison | pp, 95% CI | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1: VIDVAT − PARISHAD-3* | −0.02 [−0.07, +0.04] | NO WIN |
| H2: VIDVAT − RANDOM-VIDVAT | +0.41 [+0.32, +0.49] | NO WIN |
| H3: VIDVAT+ − VIDVAT | −0.42 [−0.52, −0.33] | NO WIN |
| H4: SABHA-JIJNASA − SABHA-RANDOM-ASK | +3.96 [+3.54, +4.44] | WIN |
| SABHA-JIJNASA − PARISHAD-3* (all labels) | +0.11 [−0.01, +0.23] | descriptive |

**Findings.**
- **The learned member does not outweigh the agreeing pair.**
  - Over 3,493 overrides, the member was right 1,676 times and the pair 1,612 times, about even.
  - When the two others agree against an established lesson, the case sits near a boundary, and neither side is reliably right.
- **Silencing the guesser costs** (H3 −0.42 pp). Where its abstention changed the decision, the guess was right 5,683 times and naive Bayes alone 4,356 times. The fallback guess is a better tie-breaker than naive Bayes.
- **The assembly that asks only under doubt keeps its accuracy.** It asked for about a third of the labels (34% on average) and matched the assembly that saw all of them (+0.11 pp). Against random asking at the same budget, it was +3.96 pp.

No bug was found, and nothing changed after the dev run.
