## Limits, stated in advance
- One 7B model with one LoRA adapter; two main worlds of about 170 offices; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; skills are 28 one-line migrations of one library; general ability on two sampled benchmarks, zero-shot.
- The "still in post" questions only ever have the answer "No", which favours a model that always says no.
- Never-streamed knowledge is predicted to fall in the weights; the routed view shows what the scope rule keeps.
