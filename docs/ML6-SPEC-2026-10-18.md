# ML-6: a language model inside the asking assembly (jijñāsā and ekamata with a model member)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any ML-6 code. It makes real model calls (gpt-6.1-sol, through the same route as ML-4 and ML-5).

## 1. Question

**What is already known.**
- With classical members, an assembly that asks for labels only under doubt beat random asking at the same budget, on three fresh pools (PV-1, MX-2, RP-1).
- Its answers when of one mind were much more accurate than random ones (MX-2, RP-1).
- A language model in the assembly lifted the model, both on synthetic worlds (ML-4: +5.67 pp) and on real data (ML-5: +1.72).

**What ML-6 asks.** Do asking under doubt and the unanimity signal still work when one member is a language model that writes its own lessons?

## 2. Members and arms

**Members:**
- ORD-sol: gpt-6.1-sol with its own ORD lesson store, exactly as in ML-4;
- naive Bayes;
- the decision tree.

The assembly answers by plurality. Doubt is the members disagreeing, or a member giving no answer; the model member has no lesson-doubt signal of its own.

| Arm | Labels observed by every member |
| --- | --- |
| MIX-FULL | every case (ML-4's PARISHAD-MIX) |
| MIX-JIJNASA | only cases where the members disagree |
| MIX-RANDOM-ASK | as many cases as MIX-JIJNASA observed, at random cases (`random.Random(seed + 777)`) |

**Warm-up (amended after the smoke run, before the dev run).** Every arm observes the first 10 cases. MIX-RANDOM-ASK's remaining labels are drawn at random from the cases after them.
- The smoke run (DRIFT seed 0, 30 cases) showed why this is needed. After the first case, all three members gave its label and were of one mind.
- The model has no doubt signal of its own, so MIX-JIJNASA never asked again: 1 label in 30, accuracy 20.7%.
- With classical members, the lesson member's doubt prevented this. The smoke run is kept as the record.

**What a member does with an observed case.** It is added to the shared memory the model's prompts draw neighbours from. The classical members observe it. The model proposes a lesson from it, as in ML-4. Unobserved cases leave no trace.

## 3. Worlds

ML-4's synthetic families EXC, NOISE and DRIFT, 100 cases each.
- **Main:** the new seeds 38000–38005, so 18 worlds.
- **Dev:** exposed seed 0 of each family.
- **Smoke:** DRIFT seed 0, cut to 30 cases.

The expected number of calls is about 8,500 for the main run.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

The decision rules are as before, stratified by family.

| Hypothesis | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | MIX-JIJNASA − MIX-RANDOM-ASK (accuracy) |
| H2 | In MIX-FULL, accuracy when all three members agree − accuracy of as many random cases (`random.Random(seed + 999)`). A world with no unanimous case counts 0. |
| Descriptive | MIX-JIJNASA − MIX-FULL; the share of labels asked for |

**INVALID if** the model fails or refuses on more than 2% of its calls, or more than 2% of its answers are unparseable.
