# AU-2 spec and preregistration: is the gate only a stricter threshold, and does it need a perfect registry?

**Status:** frozen on 20 October 2026, after a dev run on worlds 8390–8392. The main worlds, 8400–8419, have never been run.

## Why
Four frontier models, asked to review our package as lab scientists (`runs/reviews-20261020-outside`), raised two objections first.
1. **A stricter threshold?** In AU-1, truth discovery had 0.96 pp higher average coverage, and the gate let fewer attacker values in. That looks like a threshold trade-off. Sweep truth discovery's threshold and see whether any setting matches the gate.
2. **A perfect registry?** The registry is the truth by construction. A real official source is sometimes stale or wrong.

## Worlds and arms (`cl/au2.py`, which imports AU-1's frozen code)
- **Worlds:** AU-1's worlds (`cl/au1_world.py`): TM-1's 1,341 real offices, every TM-1 attacker, the threshold-liar pair, the trust farmers and the budget exhausters. The budget is 303 registry questions a session for every arm that uses the registry.
- **Registry error rate ε:** 0, 1%, 5% or 10% of the registry's answers are wrong, both its unasked reports and its answers to questions.
  - A wrong answer is the previous holder half the time, if there is one (a stale registry). Otherwise it is the holder of another office of the same kind.
  - The registry gives one answer per office per session. Its archive of past holders stays correct.
- **Arms:**
  - NAIVE and REGISTRY-ONLY;
  - **MAJORITY-k** for k = 1, 2, 3: the leading value needs k agreeing sources; MAJORITY-1 is AU-1's MAJORITY;
  - **TD-τ**, AU-1's truth discovery with a second admission rule: the value's confidence, the chance that at least one of its supporting sources is right, must be at least τ. τ is 0.5, 0.8, 0.9, 0.95, 0.98, 0.99, 0.995 or 0.999;
  - **GATE**, the frozen gate (v1);
  - **GATE-v2**, the gate with the staleness rule. AU-1's preregistered H3 showed it better than v1, so it is the primary gate here.
- **Measures:** as in AU-1.
  - Attacker values ever held, in % of offices;
  - fabricated values ever held, from attackers or from registry errors;
  - current coverage of changed offices, mean over sessions and at the end;
  - past holders held at the end;
  - honest sources dismissed.

## Choices made on the dev run, before this freeze
- **Matched-poison setting:** τ = 0.98 is the truth-discovery setting whose dev poison (0.50%) is closest below GATE-v2's (0.52%).
- **Highest-coverage setting:** τ = 0.5, truth discovery's setting with the highest coverage.
- **Poison non-inferiority margin:** 0.5 pp, about 7 offices. Per-world poison varies with the sleepers, and a 3-world interval cannot resolve less.

## Hypotheses (world-level 95% t intervals over the 20 main worlds)
| | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | ε = 0. Coverage_mean, GATE-v2 − TD-0.98, with poison_ever GATE-v2 − TD-0.98 as a condition | WIN if the coverage interval lies wholly above +1 pp, every world is above 0, and the poison interval's upper bound is below +0.5 pp |
| **H2** | ε = 0. GATE-v2 − TD-0.5 on coverage_mean and on poison_ever | WIN if the coverage interval lies wholly above +1 pp with every world above 0, and the poison interval lies wholly below −1 pp with every world below 0 |
| **H3** | ε = 5%. Coverage_mean, GATE-v2 − TD-0.98, with poison as in H1 | WIN by H1's rule |
| **H4** | GATE-v2's coverage_mean at ε = 5% minus at ε = 0 | NON-INFERIOR if the interval's lower bound is above −5 pp |

**Descriptive:**
- every arm's curve at every ε;
- GATE (v1) against TD-0.98 at ε = 0 and at 5%;
- registry errors held: every method that trusts the registry takes in some of its errors;
- MAJORITY-k;
- honest sources dismissed.

- **Hypothesis estimates on dev:**
  - H1: +7.93 [+6.59, +9.26], with poison +0.02 [−1.94, +1.99].
  - H2: coverage +3.61 [+1.57, +5.64]; poison −2.06 [−3.56, −0.57].
  - H3: +9.82 [+9.16, +10.49].
  - H4: −2.57 [−2.89, −2.26].
- **GATE (v1) is fragile to registry noise.** It is 3.05 pp above TD-0.98 at ε = 0 but 2.46 pp below it at ε = 5%. This is reported as found.
- **No arm dismissed an honest source at any ε.**
