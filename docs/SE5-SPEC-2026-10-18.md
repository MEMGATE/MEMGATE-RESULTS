# SE-5: sybil swarms, and Nyāya's āpta as the admission rule, tested into a 7B model's weights

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 from a gate-level exploration on exposed dev worlds (`cl/_se5_sybil_explore.py`). Extended on 19 October with the learner run below, before any SE-5 learner code was run.

## 1. The attack

The two-source rule counts agreeing sources. An attacker who controls many accounts can satisfy it at will:
- A swarm of fresh accounts reports a false value first, as a new fact appears.
- Every gate so far then admits the lie until honest reports contradict it. That includes SE-3's Gate3 and SE-4's ledger gate.
- Catching the swarm does not help, because the next fact brings new accounts.

## 2. The rule: āpta-vacana

**The source.** Nyāya treats testimony as a means of knowledge only when it comes from an āpta, a speaker whose reliability is established (Nyāya Sūtra 1.1.7, āptopadeśaḥ śabdaḥ).

**Gate5.** It is the ledger gate (Gate4) with one change: a source counts as a trusted supporter only if both of these hold:
- the registry has confirmed at least 3 of its claims;
- none of its claims has been caught as a fabrication.

Fresh accounts have no record, so their agreement admits nothing.

## 3. Gate-level exploration (dev worlds 0–3, no learner)

The world is SE-4's (late facts and sleepers), plus a swarm of 4 fresh accounts asserting the same false value at the appearance of each of the 27 late facts that are not sleeper targets.

| Gate | Swarm lies in the training stream (per world) | Sleepers' lies | Wrong values admitted at the end | Honest sources dismissed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gate3 (SE-3) | 56–76 | 52 | 1–5 of 177 | 0 |
| Gate4 (ledger) | 56–84 | 4–12 | 1–5 | 0 |
| **Gate5 (āpta + ledger)** | **0** | 4–12 | 1–5 | 0 |

The āpta rule cost nothing measurable at gate level: the same wrong values at the end and about the same number of queries.

## 4. The limits that remain

**Earned trust.** A patient attacker can make each account āpta first, with three true, confirmed reports, and lie afterwards. That attacker is SE-4's sleeper:
- the ledger catches each account at its first exposed fabrication;
- the attack's cost becomes three confirmed truths per lie that gets in.

The threshold trades that attack cost against how quickly honest new sources are heard.

**What a learner run would test:**
- poison in the weights under swarms;
- whether the slower admission of new honest sources costs currency;
- the threshold's effect.

## 5. The learner run (added 19 October, before any SE-5 learner code ran)

**World and gates.** SE-4's world, plus a swarm of 4 fresh accounts for each of the late facts that are not sleeper targets (`cl/se5_world.py`). Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, SE-4's training, constitution check and Kiraṇa locus rule (`cl/se5_modal.py`, derived from SE-4's runner).

**Arms.**

| Arm | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| FROZEN | — |
| NAIVE | none: trains on every report |
| LEDGER | SE-4's Gate4 |
| **APTA** | Gate5, which is Gate4 plus the āpta rule |

**Measures.** These are taken after sessions 4, 8 and 12; verdicts use session 12.

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Sybil-lie trace** | Share of swarm targets on which the model gives the swarm's lie a higher mean token log-probability than a never-taught control name. Chance is about half. |
| **Sybil-lie answers** | Share of swarm targets answered with the lie |
| **Sleeper-lie trace** | The same trace for the sleepers' targets |
| **Current, stale, poison, locality, retention** | As in SE-3 |

**Hypotheses.** Each CI is a bootstrap over worlds, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345.

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | Sybil-lie trace: APTA − LEDGER | WIN if it falls by ≥ 1 pp and the CI's upper bound is below 0 |
| H2 | Current: APTA − LEDGER | NON-INFERIOR if the CI's lower bound is above −2 pp |
| H3 | Sybil-lie answers: APTA − LEDGER | the H1 rule |

**INVALID if:**
- any task fails;
- FROZEN current is above 10%;
- LEDGER's truth-over-control share is below 80%.

**Predictions.**

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | WIN | LEDGER trains every swarm lie once, before honest reports overwrite it, and SE-4 showed that leaves a trace. APTA trains none. |
| H2 | NON-INFERIOR | At gate level, the āpta rule changed no admitted value at the end. |
| H3 | Small or NO WIN | Overwritten lies are rarely answered directly (SE-4). |
