# TW-1 spec: a change trained into an open model's weights with its date, against the new value alone

**Status:** spec, written 22 October 2026, about 21:50 IST, before any TW-1 question. Code: `cl/tw1.py`.

## Why
TQ-W (22 Oct, descriptive) asked GPT-4.1 models fine-tuned on offices' new holders about a date before the change. They named the new holder for 82.8–84.5% of those questions; the untouched model was right for 50.7%. Fine-tuning on the new value overwrote the time it held.

TQ-1 showed the memory route keeps that time: the layer's dated line (the new value, the date it changed, the value before) answered 86–100% of questions on both sides of a change.

TW-1 asks the same of the weights route: if the change is trained into the weights as the dated line states it, can the model still answer about the past?

## Arms
One open model, the lab's own LoRA loop on Kaggle's two T4 GPUs (`open_rp3_kaggle`'s settings: rank 16, alpha 32, q/k/v/o projections, AdamW at 2e-4, batch 8, 6 epochs; float16 weights, float32 adapters, a gradient scaler). Each tuned arm is one adapter trained from the untouched weights.

| Arm | Training rows per office |
| --- | --- |
| FROZEN | none: the untouched model |
| CURRENT | RP-3's DIRECT rows: the new holder, in two phrasings (2 rows) |
| UNDATED | CURRENT's rows, and three statements of the change without its date (5 rows) |
| DATED | CURRENT's rows, and the same three statements with the date (5 rows) |

The three statements, in wordings the test never uses:
- "Whom did X succeed as ROLE of PLACE?" → "Z, on DATE." (UNDATED: "Z.")
- "What was the latest change in the post of ROLE of PLACE?" → "On DATE, X succeeded Z." (UNDATED: "X succeeded Z.")
- "Who held the post of ROLE of PLACE before X?" → "Z, until DATE." (UNDATED: "Z.")

UNDATED and DATED have the same rows and names; they differ only in the date.

## Offices and questions
- **Offices:** TQ-1's (real office changes 2024–26 in TM world 8000), with TQ-1's dates:
  - dev: TQ-1's 50 dev offices;
  - main: its 300 main offices.
- **BEFORE:** a date halfway through the previous holder's tenure in the record (from 1 January 2024 at the earliest); the right answer is the previous holder.
- **AFTER:** a date halfway between the change and today (19 October 2026); the right answer is the new holder.
- **Every arm** is asked both date questions with no memory, in TQ-1's words ("Who was the ROLE of PLACE on DATE?", "Answer with the person's full name only."), and RP-3's locality questions (static offices never in training).
- **The untouched model** is also asked the date questions under TQ-1's two memory lines (GATED: the current value; GATED+: the dated line). This is the memory route on the same model.
- **Answers:** greedy, at most 48 new tokens.
- **Scoring:** TQ-1's scorer. An answer is right when it names the date's holder and not the other one, and names the other holder otherwise.

## Hypotheses (to be frozen after dev)
An office-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345. A WIN needs the whole 95% interval above +1 pp; NON-INFERIOR needs its lower bound above −2 pp.

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | BEFORE right, DATED − UNDATED | WIN |
| H2 | BEFORE right, DATED − CURRENT | WIN |
| H3 | AFTER right, DATED − CURRENT | NON-INFERIOR |
| D1 (descriptive) | BEFORE right, CURRENT − FROZEN: does the new value overwrite the past? | — |
| D2, D3 (descriptive) | BEFORE and AFTER right, the untouched model with the dated memory line − DATED | — |
| D4 (descriptive) | BEFORE right, UNDATED − CURRENT: naming the previous holder without a date | — |

An arm with more than 5% of its questions unanswered makes the run invalid.

## Halves
- **Dev:** TQ-1's 50 dev offices, one open model (OLMo-2-7B-Instruct), to check the pipeline and set the predictions.
- **Main:** the 300 main offices, after the preregistration is frozen. A second open family may be run as a replicate, named in the preregistration.

## Limits
- **One change per office:** the latest change and the holder before it.
- **Dates within the record's tenures.**
- **One adapter per arm,** trained once.
- **A 7B open model.** Frontier models are trained only through hosted fine-tuning, whose queue is long.

## Data
- **Kaggle copies:** the payload goes up as a private Kaggle dataset, and the run is a private kernel.
- **Clean-up:** both are deleted after the answers are fetched.

## Changes after dev 1 (22 October 2026, about 22:20 IST, before any main question)
**Dev 1** (`runs/tw1-dev-olmo7b-20261022`, OLMo-2-7B, 50 offices; descriptive only):
- Every tuned arm named the new holder for 100% of questions about a date before the change, DATED included. Dev 1 H1 was 0.0 pp.
- The untouched model, given the dated memory line, was right for 44% of those (92% after the change). Without memory it was right for 8%.
- Locality fell from 52% (untouched) to 10–28% in the tuned arms.

**What changed, and why:**
- **A recall-first probe (STEPS) for every arm.** The question is asked again with "First say in one sentence when the current holder took the post and whom they succeeded. Then give your answer on a new line as: Answer: <full name>". Only the text after the last "Answer:" is scored; 96 new tokens. This asks whether a date trained into the weights is used when the model is made to recall it first, as CH-1 found for chains.
- **A fourth arm, PRACTICE:**
  - DATED's rows, plus two date questions answered for half A of the offices (a fixed hash). They use TQ-1's wording, at dates the test never asks: three quarters through the previous holder's tenure, and a quarter of the way from the change to the AFTER date.
  - Half B gets no date question. This is RP-3's design: does practice on some offices teach the model to use a trained date on the others?
- **The hypotheses** will be set from dev 3 (`runs/tw1-dev3-olmo7b-20261022`). The candidates are:
  - PRACTICE − DATED on half B's past-date questions;
  - the dated memory line on the untouched model − DATED;
  - AFTER non-inferiority of DATED against CURRENT.
  - The spec's first H1 (DATED − UNDATED) becomes descriptive, as dev 1 found no difference to test.
- **Dev 2** (STEPS only) was replaced by dev 3 before it started, so it has no answers.

## Dev 3 (22 October 2026, 22:23–22:39 IST, OLMo-2-7B, 50 dev offices; descriptive only)
`runs/tw1-dev3-olmo7b-20261022`. No question went unanswered.

**Questions about a date before the change:**
- **Every tuned arm** named the new holder for 92–100% of them; PRACTICE was right for 8%.
  - Practice did not carry over: PRACTICE was right for 0% of half B's past-date questions (the offices with no date question in training), the same as DATED.
  - On half A, whose offices had a date question answered in training at other dates, it was right for 16%.
- **The dated memory line** on the untouched model was right for 44%, and for 92% after the change.

**The recall-first probe (STEPS):**
- The tuned models mostly answered with a sentence they were trained on ("X succeeded Z."), with no "Answer:" line.
- DATED and PRACTICE gave a scorable answer to almost none (0–4%).
- CURRENT and UNDATED followed the format more often (54–66%).

**Locality:** 52% for the untouched model; 10–28% for the tuned arms.

**Reading:** in a 7B model, the change trained in with its date, or with date questions on other offices, did not keep the past answerable from the weights.

**Next:** dev runs on two more open families check the pipeline before the preregistration: Qwen2.5-7B (Alibaba) and Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B (NVIDIA).

## Dev on Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B (22 October 2026, 23:24–23:45 IST, 50 dev offices; descriptive only)
`runs/tw1-dev-nemotron8b-20261022`, in the layout the model's card asks for. No question went unanswered.
- **The dated memory line** on the untouched model was right for 84% of past-date questions. After the change it was right for only 22%, naming the previous holder for 42%.
- **Every tuned arm** was right for 0% of past-date questions, except PRACTICE: 8% overall, 16% on half A and 0% on half B.
- **Locality:** 8% for the untouched model; 2–10% for the tuned arms.

**After the dev runs: the primary test counts an office as right only when both of its dates are right (BOTH).**
- On Nemotron-Nano, the dated line kept the past but cost the present.
- A test on past-date questions alone would overstate it.
