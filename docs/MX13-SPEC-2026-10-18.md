# MX-13: resume only after verification against the owner's answers (Arthaśāstra 1.12: the spies concerned are found out)

**Status:** design, written on 18 October 2026 before any MX-13 code. No model calls are made.

## 1. Question

**What came before.**
- MX-11's permanent alarm was safe with two broken sources, but costly on false alarms.
- MX-12's alarm resumed when the sources stopped disagreeing, and was fooled. Broken sources learned the commonest label while paused.

**The fix.** The resume signal must be one a broken source cannot fake. While paused, the network asks about every case, so the owner's answer, the true label, is known for each. A spy's own accuracy on those answers cannot be faked by agreeing with the others.

**The mechanism (mine).**
- The alarm is MX-11's: three-way splits, checked by `unwell`.
- **Verification during the pause.** Once 30 cases have passed since the alarm, each trusted spy's accuracy over the last 30 paused cases is compared with the best spy's.
  - A spy more than 3 SE below the best is dismissed, with SE = sqrt(a(1−a)/30) at the best spy's accuracy a. Several spies may be dismissed.
  - If at least two spies remain, answering resumes and the split record starts afresh.
  - If fewer than two remain, the pause holds. The network asks about everything, and in a product it tells the owner.
- **Afterwards.** With two trusted spies, credence is their agreement without guessing, as after a dismissal in MX-5.

## 4. Hypotheses and decision rules

| Hypothesis | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **H1 (safety)** | VERIFY in TWO is SAFE if the upper bound of the 95% CI of its wrong answers alone after case 240 is below 2 per 100. |
| **H2 (cost, SOUND)** | VERIFY − SUPERVISOR, net credence. NO HARM if the CI lower bound is above −1.0 pp. |
| **H3 (cost, ONE)** | the same, in ONE |
| Descriptive | VERIFY − ALARM; alarms, resumes and dismissals during the pause, and whether the dismissed were broken |

**INVALID if:**
- any run is non-deterministic or crashes;
- the copy, with verification off, differs from MX-11's function;
- the testability condition fails.
