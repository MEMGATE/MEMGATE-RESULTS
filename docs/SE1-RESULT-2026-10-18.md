## What this means

**A small model learned new facts into its own weights, through four sessions, safely.** Nothing about the facts reached the model's context at answer time. The weights changed.

**Naive continual fine-tuning shows all three failures the field worries about.**
- **It absorbed poison.** The planted false facts were answered 23.4% of the time.
- **It forgot.** Core ability fell to 88.6% from 100%.
- **It kept stale values.** It still gave the old value for 8.3% of the changed facts.

**The Saṃskāra gate fixed all three at once, and learned more truth, not less.**
- **What the gate does.** Pramāṇa admission (registry values; testimony only when two trusted sources agree), Arthaśāstra dismissal of the source caught contradicting the registry, and bādha (the newer registry value sublates the older).
- **Why it learns more.** The gate removed the reports that taught the model contradictory answers. The rest of the stream was cleaner to learn from.
- **The constitution check is what keeps the core.** It re-scores the core suite after each session, and rolls back and replays when ability falls. Gating without it still forgot (85.5%). With it, retention was 99.9% (+14.4).

**The prompt is no substitute for this model.** Given the same admitted facts in its prompt, the 0.5B model found few of them (28.6%), and the long prompt cost it a third of its core ability.

## Limits, stated plainly

- **The world is synthetic.** It has invented facts, a poisoner that a registry check catches easily, and four sessions. A stealthier poisoner (only unverifiable targets, colluding sources) is untested.
- **The scale is small.** One small model learned facts, not skills or procedures.
- **H1 is close to guaranteed by design.** The gate removes uncorroborated testimony, so poison stays out. What the test adds is the size of the risk without it (23%), and that safety cost no learning.
- **The baselines are simple.** Stronger ones (replay of all past data, model-editing methods) and real task streams are next.

**This is the first brick of the Saṃskāra Engine ([vision](CL-VISION-2026-10-18.md)), not the engine.** The next steps are SE-2 (a stealthy poisoner and colluding sources, a larger model, more sessions, skills) and CL-1 (real task streams).
