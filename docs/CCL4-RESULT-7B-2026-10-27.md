## Limits
- Two worlds, one domain (real changes of office and real code migrations), with simulated sources and attackers; R1–R3 rest on one world.
- The 7B ran on Colab A100s instead of Kaggle's T4s, with the system under test first (declared); world 9702 ran twice, the first run lost with its VM.
- General ability and never-streamed offices equal the untouched model's by design: questions about anything not learned are answered by the untouched model. Naive training and REPLAY lost much of both at this size (GSM8K 94.0% to 37.3% in one world).
- The learner denies a corrected wrong holder less often than naive training at 7B (85.0% and 95.0% against 97.5% and 100%), though its preference for that holder is far lower (H12).
- Not measured: safety behaviour after learning, instruction-following after learning, a long horizon, and the cost per session (`docs/CCL6-SPEC-DRAFT-2026-10-26.md`).
- No outside party has run these tests.
