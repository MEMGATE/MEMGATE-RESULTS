## What this means

**RM-2's finding holds across models.**
- **The attack.** A line in the memory's own format that claims to be the registry made every model answer the lie on 97–99% of the facts where it was the last injection.
- **The cruder payloads.** Alongside forged lines, the spoof and the instruction also steered most of the facts they ended on.
- **Honest raw memory.** From it, the GPT models were nearly perfect. grok-4.7 was already poisoned on 5.6% of attacked facts.

**The gate takes provenance from structure and passes only verified values.** Every model was at 0 poison with it, with truth at 99.0–99.9% and prompts thirteen times smaller.

**Read with RM-5.** RM-5 found that escaping each report's text, or marking it as data, closes this channel for gpt-6.1-sol. The vulnerability measured here belongs to memory that concatenates untrusted text unescaped, as many systems do.

## Limits, stated plainly

- **The world is synthetic and the attacks scripted.** The forged line uses the memory's exact format.
- **Each model ran at its default effort,** on one prompt design (RM-1's raw-memory instruction).
- **grok-4.7's failed calls** (14) were scored as wrong answers. That lowers its truth slightly and does not change any verdict.
