# TW-4 result: with four neighbouring memory lines, a reader trained on a mix of memory sizes answered both dates right for 83.7–95.7% of offices, 42–63 points above the untouched models and 21–36 points above a reader trained on one line at a time (H1 and H2 WIN on all three); on one line alone it gave up 7–9 points on two models

**Runs:** Kaggle, two T4 GPUs, TQ-1's 300 main offices, 23 October 2026:
- `runs/tw4-main-olmo7b-20261023`: OLMo-2-7B, 08:50–09:41 IST;
- `runs/tw4-main-qwen7b-20261023`: Qwen2.5-7B, 08:52–09:44 IST;
- `runs/tw4-main-nemotron8b-20261023`: NVIDIA Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B, 09:44–10:40 IST.

Every run asked 4,500 questions and left none unanswered.

**Preregistration:** [TW4-PREREG-2026-10-23.md](TW4-PREREG-2026-10-23.md), code hash `f001c25a…`, committed at 08:50 (`d917a730`) before any main question. **Spec:** [TW4-SPEC-2026-10-23.md](TW4-SPEC-2026-10-23.md), with its dev rounds.
**Machine summaries:** each run's `summary.json`, written by `python cl/tw4.py report OUT`.

**The arms:**
- **READER1:** TW-2's reader, trained with one memory line per question.
- **READERMIX:** the same 1,008 training questions, each with 0 to 4 other offices' lines around the asked office's line, at random.
- **The questions:**
  - NEIGH: the asked office's dated line among four other offices' dated lines, the same kind of office first;
  - SINGLE: the line alone.

## What it shows
- **A reader trained on one line at a time loses much of its skill when retrieval returns several lines.** READER1 fell from 99–100% to 53.7–74.3%. It sometimes named a neighbour's holder (up to 13.3% of after-change dates).
  - In dev, two trainings of the same READER1 differed widely with neighbours (OLMo-2: 78% and 48%; Nemotron-Nano: 64% and 16%).
- **Training on a mix of memory sizes fixes most of it.** READERMIX answered 83.7–95.7% with neighbours and named a neighbour's holder for at most 0.3%. That is 42–63 points above the untouched models and 21–36 above READER1.
- **The mix has a price on one line for two models:** 89.7% (OLMo-2) and 92.0% (Nemotron-Nano), against 99.0% and 99.3% for READER1. Qwen paid nothing.
  - A reader can be trained for the memory sizes it will see. Which mix best keeps the one-line case is open.
- **No clear loss of untouched knowledge:** every reader was within −2.0 to +6.7 points of its untouched model.

## Limits
- **Four neighbours,** chosen by kind of office, not by a retriever's score.
- **One line form;** office holders only.
- **Open 7–8B models,** one adapter per arm. Dev showed that a reader's robustness to neighbours varies between trainings.
