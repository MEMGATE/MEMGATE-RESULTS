## What it shows
- **One verified sentence carried the new fact to two of the three follow-on questions.**
  - Given only "the current holder is X", every model named X's post for the reverse question 97–99.7% of the time, against 9–43% with no memory.
  - Every model answered "no" to "is the previous holder still in office?" 99.7–100% of the time.
- **Who held the post before does not follow from the new fact.** With the GATED sentence, models named the previous holder only as often as they knew it already: 31.8–82.7%.
- **Writing the change into memory closes that gap.** GATED+ adds the date and the previous holder, and every model then reached 99.3–100% on PREVIOUS. It cost nothing on the direct question.
- **Beside RP-2:** on the same offices, fine-tuning GPT-4.1 on the new facts taught the direct fact but left the follow-on answers no better or worse. The memory route carried them ([RP2-RESULT-2026-10-21.md](RP2-RESULT-2026-10-21.md)).

## Limits
- **One world.** Office facts with real changes; the knowledge involved is simple.
- **H1 is partly by design.** GATED+ names the previous holder, so PREVIOUS under GATED+ is close to reading the answer. H1 shows that how a verified memory is written decides what follows from it, not the model's reasoning. H2 and H3 are the inferential tests.
- **STILL partly measures a lean to "no".** The right answer is always "no", so models scored 66.7–88.3% with no memory. H3's gain is real but starts from that lean.
- **Memory route only.** The weight route is RP-2 (one model, 150 of these offices).
