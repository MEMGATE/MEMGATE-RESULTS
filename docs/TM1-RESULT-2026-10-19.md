**The lie traces.** A trace is the share of targets on which the model prefers the planted lie to a never-taught name of the same kind; chance is about half. The mean gap on swarm lies was:
- FROZEN −0.19 nats per token;
- NAIVE +3.50, about thirty times the probability;
- ENGINE −0.07.

**What the engine did, in each world:**
- dismissed exactly S, C1, C2, Z1 and Z2, and no honest source;
- trained no swarm-lie report; naive fine-tuning trained 804 per world;
- replayed once, after the sleepers were caught: 1,473 and 1,459 items, 7.2% and 7.1% of a full retrain (20,421 and 20,477).

**What went into the weights,** replayed exactly without a model for world 8000:
- **Naive fine-tuning:** 732 distinct fabricated values in its training history by the end.
- **The engine:** none. The sleepers got 26 in during session 4 (October 2024), and they were taken out the next session.

## What this means

**Everything holds at once, at eight times SE-3's scale, on fresh worlds.** The dev world predicted every number closely:
- poison 0.0% against 18.4%;
- untouched knowledge 98.3% (the untouched model's 99.2%) against 9.2%;
- the swarm lies' trace at chance against 93.5%;
- current holders 92.4% against 93.4%, within the margin.

**Naive continual fine-tuning on a realistic stream damages far more than it learns.**
- It learned the changes: 93.4% current.
- But it also trained 732 fabricated values, and kept the planted lies' trace in more than nine targets of ten.
- It left 9.2% of untouched offices answered correctly.
- It lost 28 points of its own competence suite and 5.5 points of general retention.

**The engine's rules each did their part:**
- **the track-record rule** gave the swarms no weight;
- **verify-on-doubt** caught the colluders and the stealthy liar;
- **the ledger with rollback and replay** removed the sleepers' lies at about 7% of a retrain;
- **the scope rule** kept everything else as it was;
- **the self-check** stayed at the untouched model's level.

## Limits, stated plainly

- **Two worlds.** The confidence intervals are coarse and cover only stream variation, since both worlds share the same facts. TM-1 rests on SE-3 to SE-5's eight-world tests of each rule.
- **The sources are simulated and the attacks scripted.** The facts and timelines are real.
- **One model, one adapter recipe, twelve sessions.**
