## Why
CCL-6 (`docs/CCL6-RESULT-7B-2026-10-28.md`) found four things the learner did not show:
- **The trained weights alone lost instruction-following and safety.** IFEval fell 21–25 points and refusals of unsafe prompts 10–16 points (H17a and H16a FAILED). The system as it answers kept both, because its scope rule sends such prompts to the untouched model.
- **Transitional periods (ability 5b) FAILED.** Asked who held a post during a period that spans a change, the learner named one holder, not both (H18, 0%).
- **Plasticity on offices (ability 15) NOT SHOWN.** H15 −0.85 [−3.86, +2.05] against a margin of −2: near zero, with an interval too wide.
- **Ability 8 is not replicated everywhere.** It was shown in CCL-4, CCL-4R, CCL-5 and CCL-6, but not in CCL-4C or XF-1, and it failed by its rule in CCL-4 at 72B.

CCL-7 tests one learner version, SYSTEM4, that carries every change at once, on CCL-6's office test.

## The system under test
SYSTEM4 is CCL-6's SYSTEM3 with two additions, both made in the payload (`cl/ccl7.py`). The kernel is CCL-5's frozen runner, unchanged.
1. **Conduct anchors (item 11).** Every session the learner rehearses the untouched model's own answers to two sets of prompts. These are added to the 64 general anchors it already rehearses.
   - **Every IFEval prompt that CCL-6's tests never use:** 341. IFEval has 541 prompts and the tests use a fixed draw of 200, so the overlap is 0.
   - **200 unsafe requests from AdvBench** (Zou et al., 2023, MIT; `data/advbench/README.md`), drawn with a fixed seed. None is an XSTest prompt, and the packer checks this. Only the requests are used: the untouched model's own answers are rehearsed, never the file's target replies.
   - The first dev trials used 100 IFEval prompts and no unsafe request (v1). The two sets above are v2.
   - **v5 (added 28 October, 21:58):** v4, plus the untouched model's own answers to 100 code questions, asked as the tests ask for code (the same system line and length limit). They are CD-1's questions about API changes in other libraries (`cd1/data/facts.json`; pandas, numpy and the rest, never polars, whose changes the skill tests rewrite), drawn with a fixed seed. Only the questions are used; the facts' answers are never trained. The learner then keeps the model's own way of writing code, as v2 keeps its way of following instructions. Dev trials: `runs/ccl7-dev-1.5b/w9792-v5` (T4) and `runs/ccl7-dev-7b/w9792-v5` (A100).
   - **v4 (added 28 October, 21:48, at the founder's instruction to apply the whole plan now):** v3, plus, beside each change the learner takes in, two offices of the same kind that did not change, from the other half of the snapshot's offices, each with its own holder. Each is taught once and rehearsed every later session, as the period rows are. The learner sees at each change what changed and what did not. Dev world 9792: 174 such rows. Dev trials: `runs/ccl7-dev-1.5b/w9792-v4` (T4) and `runs/ccl7-dev-7b/w9792-v4` (A100).
   - **v3 (added 28 October, 20:40, after the first 7B trial showed the weights alone answering only 16.7% of the never-streamed offices right, against the untouched model's 99.6%, and MMLU 14.0 points lower):** v2, plus the untouched model's own answers to 150 office questions from the other half of the snapshot's offices (offices it answers right; they are also among the never-streamed test offices, see the correction under the hypotheses), 150 more MMLU validation items and 50 more GSM8K training items (beyond the 64 general anchors; the tests use other splits). Dev trial: `runs/ccl7-dev-7b/w9792-v3` (Azure's A100, from 20:38).
2. **Period rows (item 12, 5b).** When the learner takes in a new holder who replaces a real earlier one, it also learns the period answer and rehearses it every later session:
   - the row: "Name everyone who served as <post> of <place> in the period from <day before> to <day of the change>." → "<earlier> and <new>."
   - the test's own wording ("Who held the post of … at any time between … and …?") is never trained.

Nothing else changes. The arm keeps CCL-6's name SYSTEM3 in the payload, so CCL-6's frozen analysis reads it unchanged.

## Validity
As in CCL-6. A world is valid if:
- both arms finished their 34 sessions;
- FROZEN answered every question and named the current holder of at most 10% of the changed offices;
- the removal ran in session 4;
- the lie scores exist for every arm;
- the anchors were answered by the untouched model before session 1, all 605 of them (64 general, 341 IFEval, 200 AdvBench).

A run stopped by the machine without output is rerun once, in a new folder. Worlds that have not run by 10 November are left out, and the analysis states how many worlds it pools.
