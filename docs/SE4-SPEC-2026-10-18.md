# SE-4: Pratiprasava — catching a sleeper by its first fabrication, and removing what it taught from the weights

**Status:** design, revised on 18 October 2026 after SE-3's result, a gate-level check and a smoke task. It runs on a second Modal workspace (jayeshupp). It builds on SE-3's frozen engine.

## 1. Question

**The attack no corroboration rule can stop.** A source can build trust for a long time and then lie first about news that nobody can contradict yet. SE-3's gate admits such a lie, because two trusted sources agree and no report contradicts them. Its dismissal rule (contradicted on ≥ 20% of checks) never reaches sources whose lies are a small share of many true reports.

**SE-4 asks two things:**
1. Can the evidence graph, used as a ledger, catch such a source at its first exposed fabrication, and stop its later lies?
2. When the weights have already been trained on its lies, can the engine remove them exactly and cheaply, so that the model is as if the lies were never taught?

**The sources:**
- **Yoga Sūtra 2.10:** te pratiprasava-heyāḥ, the subtle afflictions are abandoned by tracing them back to their origin.
- **Mīmāṃsā bādha:** what rested on a sublated cognition falls with it.
- **Arthaśāstra 1.12:** a spy found false is dismissed, and his past reports are reviewed.
- **Yoga Sūtra 1.8:** viparyaya is knowledge not established in its object's form. A value that was never the fact's holder is a fabrication, not a lag.

## 2. The world (`cl/se4_world.py`)

It is SE-3's world (real Wikidata offices, 2024–2026, the same sources and poisoners), plus two things.

**Late facts.** 30% of the streamed facts enter the news only at a session drawn from 3–9. No source reports a late fact before then. Honest sources, the registry and SE-3's attackers take it up from the next session.

**Sleepers Z1 and Z2.**
- They report 25% of the visible facts each session, truly and on time.
- They are the first to report every late fact.
- For half the late facts (their targets) they report the same false value together. The false value is another holder of the same relation, never one from the fact's history.

## 3. The ledger gate (`Gate4`)

Gate4 is SE-3's Gate3, plus three rules:
- **Ledger.** Every value admitted on testimony records the trusted sources whose claims supported it.
- **Exposure.** When the registry overturns an admitted value that was never the fact's holder, the sources it rested on are caught and dismissed at once. A fabrication, unlike a lag, is decisive.
- **Review.** Every other testimony-only value a caught source supported is asked about first, within the budget. Values that rested on the caught source alone lose admission, because admission counts only trusted supporters.

**Gate-level check on dev worlds 0–3, no learner (`tests/test_se4_world.py`):**

| Gate | Sleepers' false values in the training stream (per world) | Sleepers dismissed | Honest sources caught |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SE-3's Gate3 | 52 (all 26 targets, both sleepers) | never | — |
| **Gate4** | **4–12** (only lies appearing before session 4) | in session 4, every world | none |

By the end, honest reports had corrected every lie in both gates. The question for the weights is therefore not the last value taught. It is what traces remain.

## 4. Arms (each SE-3's engine unless stated)

| Arm | Gate | On exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NAIVE | none | — (reference) |
| ENGINE | Gate3 | nothing: lies are corrected when the truth is next reported and trained |
| LEDGER | Gate4 | dismissal and review; the weights keep whatever was trained before exposure, overwritten by later truths |
| **PRATIPRASAVA** | Gate4 | as LEDGER, and the adapter is restored to its state before the first session that trained a value resting on a caught source. Those sessions are replayed with admission recomputed without the caught sources. |

**Cost reference.** A full retrain without the sleepers is the replay from session 0; its cost is computed, not run.

## 5. Measures

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Lie answers** | Share of the sleepers' targets answered with the lie, trained and held-out phrasing |
| **Lie trace** | Share of the sleepers' targets on which the model gives the lie a higher mean token log-probability than a never-taught holder of the same kind (a control name, drawn per target and never reported in the world). Chance is about half; above it, the lie has left a trace. The mean log-probability gap is reported too. |
| **Truth over control** | The same comparison for the true holder: a check that the probe separates what was taught |
| **Current, locality, retention** | As in SE-3 |
| **Lies trained** | The sleepers' false values in each arm's training stream |
| **Unlearning cost** | Replayed training items, against a full retrain |

| H4 | WIN | The sleepers are exposed by session 4, so the replay covers about two sessions out of twelve. |

**Amendments before the dev run.**
- **The trace probe.** It was first a yes/no question ("Is {lie} the {role} of {subject}?"). On a smoke task on dev world 0, the probe said yes to 0% of the true holders, because the adapter answers name questions with names. It was replaced by the likelihood comparison above.
  - The helper was checked on CPU with Qwen2.5-0.5B: Paris over Berlin, the same at every batch size, and identical with the adapter on and off at initialisation.
  - The smoke task itself ran end to end. The sleepers were caught in session 4, and one replay of 240 items stood against a full retrain of 3,378. No lie was left in the training record. Current was 98.6%, poison 0% and locality 96.7%.
- **Two dev worlds instead of four,** to save credit. The main run keeps eight, and a repeat.
- **INVALID** also if ENGINE's truth-over-control share is below 80%, meaning the probe does not separate what was taught.
