## Hypotheses
Main half, all 20 worlds, volume 10%. Each is a difference between two policies, world by world, with a 95% t interval over the 20 worlds (19 degrees of freedom).

| Test | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | stale answers, FR2 − NEWEST | **WIN** if the interval lies wholly below −0.1 pp |
| H2 | stale answers, FR2 − RANDOM | **WIN** if the interval lies wholly below −0.2 pp |
| H3 | right answers, FR2 − NEWEST | **NON-INFERIOR** if the lower bound is above −1.0 pp |

**Validity:** a world whose run failed is left out and reported. The run is INVALID if more than 2 of the 20 worlds fail.
