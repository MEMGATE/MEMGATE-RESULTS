**Finding: the ten-member assembly is not diverse in practice.**

- Seven of its ten members are variants of the same lesson learner: R-ORD, R-SHASTRA2, +ART, +SYA, KALA, +ANU and +AKHYATI. Their bloc always outvotes naive Bayes, ID3 and 1-NN, so PARISHAD-10 decides like R-ORD.
- The truly mixed three-member assembly (one lesson learner, one probabilistic, one tree) gains 1.6 pp.
- This is the source's own point: an assembly is worth its *different* competences, not its head-count. The spec fixed the compositions and they are not changed after the dev run, so H1 is expected to be ≈ 0.
- The assemblies lose where the lesson learner alone is far better, on exact-function data: tic-tac-toe −6.1 (PARISHAD-3) and −6.8 (MAJORITY-5); MONK's problem 2 −2.6 and −3.6.

No bug was found, and nothing changed after the dev run.
