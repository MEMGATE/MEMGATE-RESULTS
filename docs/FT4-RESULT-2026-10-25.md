**Written:** 25 October 2026, 06:33 IST. Preregistration: [FT4-PREREG-2026-10-25.md](FT4-PREREG-2026-10-25.md) (code `98a63295f39d…`). Spec: [FT4-SPEC-2026-10-24.md](FT4-SPEC-2026-10-24.md).

## What ran
Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct, one hosted fine-tuning job per arm from the untouched model (6 epochs, learning-rate multiplier 2, global training), on two main worlds generated after the freeze (9600 and 9601: 166 real offices each). Every job succeeded (about 0.1 to 0.5 million trained tokens each); every model was asked every question (1 to 20 of 1,009–1,012 left unanswered per model, refused by Azure's content filter and scored as wrong).

## What it means, plainly
- **At 70B the learner's recipe works in one step:** it names the current holder for about 98% of offices (naive training 72–81%), learns no attacker value (naive 4.3%), and answers what follows from a change for 88–96% of offices it was taught (naive 21–23%).
- **It also reasons beyond what it was taught:** on offices trained without what follows, the learner answered 48–55% of follow-on questions, above both naive training (28–29%) and the untouched model (36–39%). (At 7B, in CCL-2, it only matched the untouched model there.)
- **The abstract rules did not add to that** (H1, −2.6 points): teaching "a post has one holder at a time" with invented examples gave no gain over the learner's own data.
- **General ability held:** GSM8K rose about 4 points for every trained arm, naive included, so the rise is not the learner's doing; MMLU stayed within half a point, just short of the preregistered bar.
- **Forgetting did not:** offices never in the training fell from 87–90% (untouched) to 35–48% for every trained arm. One job of training on new offices rewrote the model's answers about others, as at 7B.

## Limits
- One job per arm from the untouched model: the learner's content after the stream, not session-by-session learning (CCL-2 tests that).
- One 70B model; two worlds of 166 offices; simulated sources and attackers over real timelines; general rules written by us; the service's own fine-tuning (its batch size and method are not ours).
