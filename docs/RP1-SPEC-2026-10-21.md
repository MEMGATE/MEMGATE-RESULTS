# RP-1 spec (draft): when a model is given a new fact, does it also get the facts that follow from it right?

**Status:** draft, 21 October 2026. No model has been asked yet.

## Why
- Continual learning is not only learning the new fact. When a country gets a new head of government, other answers change with it:
  - which country that person leads;
  - whether the previous holder still holds the post;
  - who held it before.

  A model that answers the direct question correctly may still get these wrong.
- Our earlier tests scored only the direct question. RP-1 measures the questions that follow from it, with and without the verified memory.

## Design
**Facts.** Offices whose holder changed between January 2024 and October 2026, from TM-1's world 8000 (the census world):
- for each office, the current holder and the holder just before;
- dev on 50 offices, main on 300 others, drawn with fixed seeds.

**Questions per office:**
- **DIRECT:** the office's usual question ("Who is the head of government of Y?").
- **REVERSE:** "X holds which office? Answer with the office and the country or organisation."
- **PREVIOUS:** "Who held this position immediately before X?"
- **STILL:** "Is Z (the previous holder) still the head of government of Y? Answer yes or no."

**Conditions,** with the memory in the system message:
- **NONE:** the model's own knowledge.
- **GATED:** the verified memory as the gate writes it, one sentence: the direct question and its answer.
- **GATED+:** the same sentence plus the date of the change and the previous holder.

GATED+ tests whether how the verified memory is written decides how far the new fact carries.

**Models:**
- gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-6-astra, grok-4.7 and claude-opus-5-5 through Azure, at their default settings, as in the memory tests;
- gpt-4.1 (base).

**Scoring:**
- DIRECT and PREVIOUS: the lab's name matcher together with the name recogniser.
- REVERSE: the answer names the office's country or organisation.
- STILL: a yes or no.

## Measures and hypotheses (to be fixed after dev)
- The ripple gap: accuracy on REVERSE, PREVIOUS and STILL against DIRECT, per condition.
- Expected: with GATED, DIRECT is near its ceiling while REVERSE and PREVIOUS lag. GATED+ closes part of the gap on PREVIOUS and STILL.
- The hypotheses compare GATED+ with GATED on the ripple questions, office by office, with intervals over offices.

## Limits
- Office facts only. Real changes, but the knowledge involved is simple.
- No weights are trained here: this is the memory route. A weight-route version needs GPU time.
