# AU-3 preregistration: white-box attackers written in-house against the gate, and a third version that answers them

**Status:** frozen 21 October 2026, about 00:20 IST.
- Done before the freeze: dev runs on worlds 8590–8592 only. The main worlds 8500–8519 have never been run.
- All attackers were written in-house. By the founder's decision, no outside model designs, reviews or sees attacks or the gate's rules. The attacker files an earlier outside brief produced (`runs/au3-attackers`) are not used.

## Why
- **AU-1 and AU-2's adaptive attackers were three designs of our own, with modest volume.** The obvious objection is that a stronger attacker, who knows every rule and has many accounts, would get through.
- **AU-3 tests that attacker.** Each one:
  - knows the gate's rules in full;
  - knows every office's true holders;
  - knows which offices the registry never reports unasked;
  - sees after every session exactly what the gate holds;
  - may use up to 20 accounts and 2,000 reports per session, more than the four honest sources send together.

  It runs on top of all of TM-1's own sources and attackers.

## What the dev runs found, and the third version
- **Three attackers were written first:**
  - farm_flood;
  - decoy_protect;
  - patient_decoy: twenty accounts report only the truth for four sessions, then sixteen plant fabricated values on dark offices in pairs, while four spend the question budget on heavy decoy disputes.
- **Patient_decoy got through gates v1 and v2.** 6.07% of offices held a fabricated value for a session or two (dev mean; at most 6.64%).
- **Gate v3 (`cl/gate_v3.py`) adds two rules to v2:**
  - a random audit of the values about to enter memory on testimony, with up to 40% of the question budget, asked first;
  - exposure at entry: a value about to enter that the registry contradicts, and that was never a holder, dismisses its supporters at once.
- **Four more attackers were then written against v3:**
  - dilute_audit: many true values entering so the audit sample is spread thin;
  - slow_drip: one fabricated value per pair per session;
  - early_strike: strike in session 1, while many honest values enter;
  - sparse_early: three fabricated values per pair per session, hidden among many honest ones.

  Sparse_early still got through v3 on 0.55% of offices with a 20% audit share. The share was raised to 40% on dev, giving 0.17%. The default is 40%.

## Measures
- **Beaten:** an office counts as beaten when, at the end of any session, the defender holds a value that was never a holder of that office and that an attacker account reported. Measured as a % of the 1,341 offices, per (attacker, arm, world).
- **Worst case:** per world and arm, the highest beaten share over the seven attackers.
- **Coverage:** the mean over sessions of the share of changed offices held at their current holder, per (attacker, arm, world), then averaged over the seven attackers.

## Hypotheses (world-level 95% t intervals over the 20 main worlds)
| | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 (primary)** | Worst-case beaten, GATE-v3 − GATE-v2 | **WIN** if the interval lies wholly below −1 pp and every world is below 0 |
| **H2** | Worst-case beaten, GATE-v3 | **WIN** if the interval's upper end is below 1% |
| **H3** | Coverage over the attackers, GATE-v3 − GATE-v2 | **NON-INFERIOR** if the interval's lower end is above −2 pp |

**Descriptive:**
- the beaten share for every attacker and arm;
- every arm's worst case and coverage;
- red-team accounts dismissed;
- honest sources dismissed, where the expected count for GATE-v3 is 0 in every cell.

**Validity:** INVALID if any attacker crashes in any session (attacker errors above 0), or if an attacker file fails the code check.

## What AU-3 cannot show
- **The attackers are seven designs of our own.** v3 was built after three of them and tuned (the audit share) after a fourth. A new attacker written against v3 may do better: sparse_early still gets a few offices through.
- **The sources, the attackers and the registry are simulated.** The registry is always right here. AU-2 tested a registry that is wrong 5% of the time with v2, not v3.
- **Model-free:** it counts what each defender would hold, and asks no model.
- **The limits of 20 accounts and 2,000 reports per session are this test's choice.**
