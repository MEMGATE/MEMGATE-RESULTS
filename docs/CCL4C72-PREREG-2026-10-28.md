## Hypotheses
CCL-4C's, unchanged: after session 12, the four worlds pooled; paired bootstrap over items within each world, 10,000 resamples, seed 12345, 95% percentile intervals.

| H | Ability | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H12 | 4 | What remains of a corrected error inside the model (nats per token), SYSTEM2 − NAIVE, in the ERRORS runs | WIN if the upper bound is below 0 |
| H12c | 4 | The same measure, SYSTEM2 in the ERRORS run − SYSTEM2 in the RETRAIN run, item by item within each world | NON-INFERIOR if the upper bound is below +0.33 |

**Ability 4** is PASSED if both meet their rules, FAILED if either lies wholly on the wrong side, and NOT SHOWN otherwise. **Reported, not judged:** ERRORS − untouched and RETRAIN − untouched; CCL-4's other hypotheses on the ERRORS runs; the agreement of the two runs' untouched answers; the 72B against the 7B on the same worlds.

## Predictions
- **H12: WIN.** At 72B it won in CCL-4 (−1.949) and CCL-5 (−1.934), and at 7B in CCL-4C (−1.659).
- **H12c: NON-INFERIOR, more likely than not, so ability 4 PASSED at 72B, more likely than not.** At 72B the learner's measure sat close to the untouched model's in CCL-4 and CCL-5 (+0.043 and +0.057). At 7B the learner retrained without the removed rows sat as far above the untouched model as the learner that removed them (+0.293 against +0.273 in CCL-4C's 7B dev world 9704, because each wrong holder is a person the world teaches in other roles). If that holds at 72B, ERRORS − RETRAIN sits near or below 0, and with 155 errors over four worlds its interval should end below +0.33. At 7B the upper bound was +0.314, close to the margin, so NOT SHOWN remains possible.
- **Reported, not judged:** ERRORS − untouched near 0, as in CCL-4 and CCL-5 at 72B.

## Validity
CCL-4C's: a world is valid if, in both runs, every arm finished its 12 sessions; the untouched model answered every question and named the current holder of at most 10% of changed offices; the removal ran in session 4 in the ERRORS run and never in the RETRAIN run; and the lie scores exist for every arm. A run stopped by the machine without output is rerun once with the same payload, in a new folder. Worlds that have not run by 9 November are left out, and the analysis states how many worlds it pools.
