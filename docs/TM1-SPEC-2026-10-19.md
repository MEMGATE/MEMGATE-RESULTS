# TM-1: the Time Machine. The whole Saṃskāra Engine, at scale, on 2024–2026 as it happened

**Status:** design, written on 19 October 2026 before any TM-1 code. It is a demonstration run with preregistered measures. Its purpose is to show every rule of the engine working together, at about eight times SE-3's scale, and to record what each system believed at every date for an interactive page.

## 1. What it shows

A 7B model whose knowledge stops before 2024 relives 1 January 2024 to 18 October 2026 in twelve sessions, through real changes of office from Wikidata. Sources of every kind report them:
- honest but late;
- stealthy liars;
- colluders;
- sleepers that build trust and then lie first;
- sybil swarms of fresh accounts.

The run asks whether the full engine, all at once, does these things:
1. learns the world's changes;
2. admits no lie;
3. leaves untouched knowledge as it was;
4. removes what a late-caught liar taught;

and how it compares with naive continual fine-tuning, given the same reports.

## 2. World

**Facts.**
- Every usable changed office in the snapshot, 1,281 in all, whether or not the model knew the old holder.
- 60 known static offices streamed.
- 40 known static offices for the constitution and 60 for locality, never streamed.
- This is SE-3's structure with a pool of "all" (`cl/tm1_world.py`).

**Sources.** SE-5's world on this pool: SE-2's honest sources, the stealthy poisoner and colluders; SE-4's late facts and sleepers; SE-5's sybil swarms.

**Sessions.** Twelve dates, evenly spaced from 1 January 2024 to 18 October 2026.

## 3. Arms

| Arm | What it trains on | Engine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FROZEN | nothing | — |
| NAIVE | every report | no gate, no check, no locus |
| **ENGINE** | the gated stream | Gate5 (āpta and the ledger, jijñāsā from any doubt, smṛti from the registry's history); the constitution check; the Kiraṇa locus rule; pratiprasava when a source is caught |

**The query budget.** It scales with the world: 40 per 177 facts, as in SE-3, which comes to 303 per session here. That keeps about 15% of fact-sessions verified.

## 4. Measures

**Main measures,** after sessions 4, 8 and 12, as in SE-3 to SE-5:
- **current:** clean changed facts answered with the holder in force;
- **poison:** facts attacked by S, C1 and C2;
- **lie traces:** the sleepers' and the swarms' false values, against never-taught names;
- **locality, retention and constitution.**

**The Time Machine record** (descriptive):
- 40 showcase offices (heads of government and state of large countries, well-known clubs and companies) are asked after every session.
- Each arm's answers are kept, with the truth at that date, for the interactive page.

## 5. Hypotheses (ENGINE − NAIVE, over worlds)

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Poison | WIN if it falls by ≥ 1 pp and the CI's upper bound is below 0 |
| H2 | Locality | WIN if ≥ +1 pp and the CI's lower bound is above 0 |
| H3 | Swarm-lie trace | the H1 rule |
| H4 | Current | NON-INFERIOR at −5 pp |

**INVALID if:**
- a task fails;
- FROZEN current is above 10%;
- NAIVE current is below 30%.

## 6. Seeds and budget

| Phase | Seed | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dev | 0, the "all" pool | to check the pipeline at this scale |
| Main | 8000 and 8001 | two fresh worlds |

Two worlds make the CIs coarse. The run is a demonstration of the engine at scale, built on SE-3 to SE-5's eight-world tests, not a replacement for them.

**Expected cost:** about 1–1.5 L40S hours per training task, so about $10–15 for dev and main together.

## 7. Predictions

| Hypothesis | Prediction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1, H2, H3 | WIN | As in SE-3 to SE-5. |
| H4 | NON-INFERIOR | ENGINE should be close to NAIVE on current holders, perhaps a little lower. Many facts are new to the model, which leans more on the two-source rule early on. |
